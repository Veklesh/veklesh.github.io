The figure named Xuan Yi suddenly froze, trembled slightly, and quickly glanced around cautiously. Seeing that the others hadn’t noticed them, she let out a slight sigh of relief.

"Of course I want to go back..."

"I still have many clan members, and eight younger sisters..."

"I heard the Ancient Hidden Civilization was destroyed. I have no idea how our clan is faring now."

Her voice was soft, her face filled with resignation and bitterness.

Ever since she had taken the place of her younger sister Qingyi—who had escaped the Ji Chan clan—and been reduced to a sacrifice offered by the Ancient Hidden Civilization to the Land Of Truth, becoming a handmaiden within this Temple of the True Ancestor, she had been accompanied only by a deathly silence as profound as Xuan She’s eternal slumber, along with coldness, loneliness, and numbness.

Within this vast and boundless Temple of the True Ancestor slumbered the supreme will of the True Ancestor of the Land Of Truth.

Even the “Kings” of the great True Clans must prostrate themselves with every step upon entering, their devotion reaching the utmost limit.

As handmaids of the True Ancestor Temple, they are responsible for maintaining its cleanliness. Since arriving here, they have worked ceaselessly, day and night, wiping the floors, lamps, corridors, and pillars… though this place contains supreme divine might, there is not a speck of dust to be found.

Besides her, there are tens of thousands here, just like her, who were sacrificed from various chaotic realms and eras to come to the Land Of Truth, serving as handmaids to the "True Ancestor."

Within the True Ancestor’s Temple, all rules and mighty powers lose their meaning. Time, space, the passage of years, and fate… everything has come to a standstill, leaving no trace or sense of existence.

Thus, here, they do not die, nor do they face the so-called end of their lifespans; they are like flowers that never wither.

Alas… throughout the long ages, no one has ever seen the “True Ancestor,” nor does anyone know what the “True Ancestor” truly is.

All that accompanies them is deathly silence, cold, loneliness, and boundless emptiness—until, in the end, they become numb, reduced to puppets with nothing but instinct, devoid of consciousness.

"I’ve almost forgotten what my people are, forgotten where I come from..."

Beside Ji Xuanyi, a slender figure clad in a pure white robe murmured softly.

Her voice was equally soft. Unlike the numbness of the figures around her, she still retained purity and sentience; her jet-black pupils were like a clear mirror, capable of reflecting the human heart.

"Wuro, if only I could be like you and always stay true to myself." Ji Xuanyi looked at the slender figure beside her with envy.

When it came to the length of time spent in the True Ancestor Temple, no one could compare to the Uro before her.

She looked very petite, like a young girl, but it was said she was among the very first maids to enter the True Ancestor Temple.

The maids who had entered here alongside Uro long ago had grown numb; some, unable to bear such deathly silence and endless time, chose to disperse their souls. Unfortunately, within the True Ancestor Temple, even death is not granted.

No matter how many times one’s spirit was scattered, it would eventually return.

This place was like the Avici Hell—a prison from which one could neither die nor escape.

Ji Xuanyi didn’t know how much longer she could hold on. Though she clung to her resolve, she had no idea how long that resolve would last or when it would finally fade.

“True heart?”

Wu Luo shook his head, gazing at the vast, boundless starry sky beyond the temple, yet his hands never stopped moving. He continued to hold the broom, sweeping the ground that was already spotless.

Ji Xuanyi sighed. Perhaps it was precisely this simplicity and clarity that allowed Wuro to maintain that sliver of untainted true self within this endless hell—preventing him from breaking down or becoming numb.

"Xuanyi, I can actually help you get back."

Suddenly, Wu Luo murmured these words.

Ji Xuanyi froze in place, as if she had misheard him, doubting her own ears.

“It’s not that you can’t leave the True Ancestor Temple…”

Seeing Ji Xuan Yi’s confusion, shock, and bewilderment, Wu Luo continued to speak in a low voice.

Ji Xuanyi’s eyes, however, gradually widened, growing larger and larger until her whole body trembled slightly, her gaze filled with disbelief.

After all these years, this was the first time she had ever heard such a claim.

No, even if one were to leave, it would still be considered an unauthorized departure.

In the Land Of Truth, this constituted defiance against the "True Ancestor." Not even the "Kings" of the great True Ancestors dared to do such a thing. No matter where she fled, she could not escape divine retribution; she would only meet a complete and utter demise.

“Uro, what are you saying…” Ji Xuanyi’s mouth felt dry, her eyes flickering with shock.

Uro whispered, “If you wish to return home, Xuan’yi, I can help you. The Great and Almighty True Ancestor, in truth, will not punish His devout followers. It is like the boundless ocean—it pays no heed to the rise and fall of a single grain of sand on the shore. Even as the tides ebb and flow, they sweep up countless grains of sand…”

“In the True Ancestor’s eyes, you and I are nothing more than a speck of dust or a grain of sand. Why would He care where we go? Since the Temple of the True Ancestor was established, the True Ancestor has never actually appeared…”

Hearing these words, Ji Xuanyi was utterly shocked; her breathing grew rapid, and she could not hide the trace of excitement on her face.

Wuro continued, “It’s just that no one dares to leave here on their own—just look at that lack of courage and resolve…”

Ji Xuanyi took a deep breath, forcing herself to calm down, and couldn’t help but ask, “Since you say that, Wulo, why don’t you choose to leave this place?”

"I am different from you. You all have something you wish to pursue; you all want to escape this place. But I am different. I choose to stay here. I am willing to devote my entire life to the True Ancestor."

Wu Luo spoke in a low voice. Her pupils were pitch-black, as if threads of eerie light were swirling and flickering within them.

“You…”

Ji Xuanyi was completely stunned.

Uro set down the broom in her hand and slowly walked toward the exit of the passageway. Outside the towering, vast temple, the starry sky sparkled brilliantly, and the Milky Way stretched across the heavens. Pointing toward the temple’s exterior, she said, “Follow that Milky Way all the way out. When you can no longer walk any further, you will have left the confines of the True Ancestor Temple…”

Ji Xuanyi followed the direction of her finger and saw the brilliant Milky Way cascading down, as if it truly formed a hazy, celestial pathway.

“Is that really possible?” she murmured.

“Yes.” Wu Luo’s tone was soft, yet firm.

Then, as if hesitating for a moment, she took a pure, immaculate bead from her snow-white robe and handed it to Ji Xuanyi with the utmost care.

The bead looked unremarkable, no larger than a fist, yet its interior was utterly pure—a seamless expanse of white—as if it contained a power transcending the Dao itself, constantly shifting and transforming.

"What is this?"

Ji Xuan’yi asked in confusion.

"This is an 'artifact.' It is called the Transcendent Pearl. I found it while cleaning the meditation cushion deep within the True Ancestor’s Temple; it may have been a gift from the True Ancestor to His devout followers," Wu Luo explained.

"Is this the 'artifact' that all the major True Clans are fighting over? It’s far too precious; I cannot accept it." Ji Xuanyi’s pupils contracted, and she shook her head hurriedly, handing the Transcendent Pearl back.

Though she had never left the True Ancestor Temple, she had heard of the world beyond.

The peculiarity of the “exotic object” was such that even beings at the Heavenly Seal Realm would fight over it.

“To me, it’s just an ordinary bead. It’s of no use to me. Rather than keeping it by my side, it’s better for it to stay with you.”

“Take it. You might find it useful someday.” Wu Luo shook his head, his gaze clear and pure, without a single blemish.

Ji Xuanyi still hesitated.

"The Transcendent Pearl contains unimaginable power that transcends the Great Dao. As long as you possess this Transcendent Pearl, you will possess the 'status' of an absolutely transcendent 'exotic object.' As long as you do not encounter the power of the Heavenly Seal Realm, the stronger your enemies are, the more powerful the transcendent strength you will possess..." Wu Luo continued to explain.

This is the "Transcendent" power possessed by the "Transcendent Pearl." The origin of this power is unfathomable, yet it transcends the Dao itself.

It could be said that even an being at the End of the Path would be powerless against such a force.

Even a being at the Heavenly Seal Realm, without specific countermeasures against "Anomalies," could suffer a severe setback.

Ji Xuanyi’s pupils contracted. She was merely at the ordinary Dao Realm cultivation level, far too distant from the End of the Path Realm. By this logic, didn’t that mean that as long as she held this pearl, even when facing a Heavenly Seal Realm being, she would have a fighting chance?

“No wonder every time an ‘Anomaly’ appears,” it always causes such an uproar, triggering bloodshed and fierce competition among all parties… She suddenly understood.

This power was too defiant of the heavens and too incredible—it didn’t follow any logic or rules.

"It is, after all, a creation born outside the rules, shattering the theory of the constancy of energy and matter. There is a saying that every 'anomalous object' is actually the transformed form of a defier of the heavens or an anomaly who once shattered the rules of order, only to be slain by the True Ancestor after reaching the pinnacle of their power..." Wu Luo spoke in a somber tone, and Du Ji Xuan Yi shuddered, still feeling a chill run down her spine.

In the end, however, she accepted the Transcendent Pearl. As Uro had said, it might come in handy after she left the True Ancestor’s Temple.

“I have only three days. I must hurry to the Hexu Abyss and join the great army of the Heavenly Seal Clans to return to the other side of the Vast Expanse…”

"I only have this one chance."

Ji Xuan’yi kept Uro’s kindness firmly in her heart, then followed Uro’s instructions, setting off along the path indicated by the Milky Way.

Sure enough, though the Temple of the True Ancestor was vast and boundless, as she followed the path of the Milky Way, she could feel herself moving further and further away from that endless hell.

"It seems the old rumors were false. As long as you find the right direction, it is still possible to leave the True Ancestor Temple..."

Ji Xuanyi shook her head, banishing the last trace of doubt, and a look of relief spread across her flawless, lovely face.

Many rumors had once circulated within the True Ancestor Temple: that there was no time or space here, and thus no sense of direction. No matter which way one went, one would eventually return to the starting point, as if trapped in a loop.

Now, as she walked further and further away, that rumor had finally been debunked.

At the entrance to the True Ancestor Temple’s passageway, Wu Luo picked up the broom she had set down earlier. She watched Ji Xuanyi’s figure until it vanished from sight, then withdrew her gaze and turned to walk deeper into the temple.

In her dark, luminous eyes, the deep, ink-like glow gradually faded away.

Vast Expanse—the ancient ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization.

The Sea of Reincarnation remained vast and boundless, its silver radiance as dazzling as a gale. Sparks of spiritual light scattered from all directions, converging here before finally sinking into the endless sea.

"So-called reincarnation is, in a sense, merely a return of earth to earth and dust to dust. After death, the true spirit does not dissipate; a sliver of memory remains. That thread of true spirit, along with the Mother Tree of the Eon, scatters across the various worlds—but is it ultimately still the person it once was?"

"Or perhaps, are they merely similar blossoms?"

"Hah, I don’t believe in reincarnation, nor do I believe in the afterlife or the past. I believe only in the present—and in the present, I am invincible."

Several figures stood tall on the shore, where jagged reefs jutted out and silver waves crashed against them, the atmosphere thick with the aura of reincarnation.

The two engaged in conversation were among them. One stood tall and imposing, his features sharp and distinguished, exuding confidence; his vitality surged like the depths of the ocean and the flow of great rivers, akin to the earth’s crucible. The other held a palm-leaf fan, wearing a tall crown and wide sashes—dressed in the garb of a scholar, he carried an air of carefree elegance.

After the collapse of the Ancient Hidden Civilization, the Heaven Slaying Alliance and the major clans swept through the various ruins, taking away virtually everything that could be carried—except for this boundless, vast Sea of Reincarnation before them.

In the center of the Sea of Reincarnation, a towering Tree of the Ages once rose from the earth, and the Ji Chan clan dwelt upon it.

For the Ji Chan clan, a single tremor of their cicada wings signaled the turn of an era and the passing of all living beings.

From ancient times to the present, wherever the Ji Chan passed, the ages shifted, eras cycled, and the cycle repeated endlessly, renewing all things.

To the Vast Expanse of all worlds, the Mother Tree of the Eon held extraordinary significance.

However, after the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance intervened and uprooted this Tree of the Ages with supreme power, the Sea of Reincarnation gradually dried up and withered. Today, its surface area is less than one-third of what it once was.

Despite this, countless living beings and cultivators still come here every day, hoping to find hidden treasures or seize opportunities. This is especially true since word has spread that the Epoch Mother Tree, once uprooted by the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, is now on the verge of death—plagued by a pestilence and devoured by World-Devouring Beetles.

Once the Epoch Mother Tree dies, a new seed or sapling of the Epoch Mother Tree will emerge.

Many beings speculate that the newly emerging seeds or saplings of the Eon Mother Tree will appear in the vicinity of the Sea of Reincarnation and will emerge within the coming days.
