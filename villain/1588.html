The predetermined trajectory of the future was originally unalterable, but Gu Changge had thrown the future into chaos and disorder. Consequently, the Lord of the Future—who should have emerged only within the future Civilization—appeared prematurely due to certain unpredictable changes.

However, that Future Lord could not truly come to the present age.

From Gu Changge’s current perspective, that period of history was indeed contradictory. The True Realm of Mountains and Seas at that time could not have survived the Third Great Calamity, and thus it was impossible for a Dao Realm entity to have emerged from it.

Thus, had the Lord of the Future attacked with the power of an Immortal Emperor, the True Realm of Mountains And Seas would indeed have faced the risk of collapse—which would have been more than sufficient to resolve the root of all problems.

But Gu Changge’s power at that time far surpassed the Dao Realm—let alone that of an Immortal Emperor.

That is why the Future Lord had felt such despair at that time.

"A laughable attempt."

Gu Changge had no intention of interfering with this future. The Future Lord’s power at that time was merely at the peak of the Sovereign King stage.

As long as the future continues to evolve and expand, powerful beings surpassing that Civilization will soon emerge, and successors will arise one after another, like the ebb and flow of the tides.

This was the inevitable course of the Vast Expanse expanding toward chaos.

"To advance one’s realm and strength as quickly as possible, one must still extract immortal substances and primordial substances from various forms of fortune and faith..."

Gu Changge concluded his analysis of the timeline and withdrew his gaze.

For now, he had merely imparted the cultivation methods involving immortal substances and primordial substances to Yue Mingkong; after all, the supply of such substances from the vast expanse of the cosmos was finite.

He was not selfless enough to supply Immortal Substances and Creative Substances to the entire Vast Expanse.

To accelerate the process of the World Sphere condensing Immortal Substances and Creative Substances, the Vast Expanse must provide more power from the Faith of Destiny.

On this basis, every time a new Pathfinder is born, the destiny of the Vast Expanse expands.

For a Pathfinder to reach the stage of creating things out of nothing—from nothingness to existence—without relying on external objects, they must inevitably break through the tenth layer of the Heavenly Realm, that is, the Eternal Heaven Realm.

During this period, the Pathfinder still cannot achieve complete independence from the boundless realms.

Therefore, fundamentally speaking, Gu Changge’s act of shattering the Heavenly Will into countless fragments is currently a losing proposition.

Not a single Pathfinder is among the possessors of these fragments of the Heavenly Dao, and the day they might reach the Eternal Heaven Realm remains a distant prospect.

However, the beauty of a future that becomes chaotic and disordered lies in the fact that these beings, who would otherwise never have become Pathfinders, through the collision and combination of various karmic causes and effects, allow the future to expand with infinite possibilities.

Within these infinite possibilities, the Vast Expanse of all worlds has expanded by more than a hundred million times. Therefore, as long as even one, two, or three beings of the Eternal Heaven Realm are born in the future, it will allow this future to continue expanding and evolving in a state of disorder.

Therefore, the risky venture Gu Changge is undertaking now is well worth it and will eventually yield stable returns.

At that time, no one knows what kind of beings will emerge.
