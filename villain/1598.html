At the same moment, deep within the Heaven Slaying Alliance, in the divine islands, caves, palaces, and towers, countless powerful figures were left speechless. The mightiest beings from the various True Realm lineages trembled at the sight.

Layer upon layer of formation runes shattered. Above the heavens, endless clouds and mist surged and parted, revealing a gray hand—wider and more boundless than the vast continent itself—that materialized out of thin air. With overwhelming force, it reached down, reaching toward the palaces below.

Its target was none other than the cluster of palaces at the heart of the Heaven Slaying Alliance—especially the area where endless radiance seemed to rise from the depths, accompanied by various mystical substances billowing toward the heavens.

"How dare you..."

At that moment, angry shouts rang out throughout the Heaven Slaying Alliance. Many supreme beings sprang into action, unleashing a surge of Dao-level fluctuations that soared upward to counter the reaching hand.

However, no matter how fiercely the deities from the various divine mountains and islands roared, nothing could alter the course of events.

That colossal hand swept across the boundless expanse, reaching out with such force that any beams of light or array patterns striking it were like mere droplets of water—utterly incapable of halting it for even an instant.

Many beings who attempted to charge forward were crushed by the overwhelming, boundless pressure amidst their furious roars, unable to stand, and plummeted directly from the heavens, crashing to the ground one after another.

This scene sent shockwaves through the Vast Expanse and the infinite True Realm, causing countless beings to tremble with fear.

Following the incident with the Enforcer of the Land Of Truth, such a scene has unfolded once again today. Have they gone mad, or do they have some hidden backing that allows them to so brazenly challenge the Heaven Slaying Alliance?

The Lord of Administration, the Lord of Restraint, and the others, trapped within that ink-and-wash painting, could only watch this unfold, utterly powerless to intervene.

But just then, a faint sigh echoed out, as if reverberating through the boundless expanse of the myriad realms, yet also as if emanating from the very edge of the infinite, shadowy heavens.

Every living being heard this voice.

And as the sound rang out, the massive hand that had been reaching toward the Heaven Slaying Alliance suddenly trembled, as if frozen in place, unable to advance even an inch further.

Then, as if time had reversed, everything began anew. Endless, chaotic beams of light flew back, and immediately afterward, the giant hand shattered inch by inch, turning into an endless shower of sparks.

High above the Heaven Slaying Alliance, a dense fog of divine radiance surged back, completely shrouding the area.

That earth-shattering scene from moments ago seemed more like an illusion.

Li Qingxu’s expression froze. The hand he had reached out with was now rigidly suspended before him, unable to advance even an inch. It was not that he did not wish to withdraw it, but that this hand no longer belonged to him.

In the next instant, under the stunned gazes of countless beings on the battlefield, Li Qingxu’s hand snapped back in a grotesquely twisted posture, then clamped directly around his own throat.

"Hah..."

Li Qingxu felt himself suffocating, unable to breathe. At his level, he no longer possessed a physical form; his human appearance was merely an illusion.

Yet in this moment, he once again experienced what it meant to be human—vulnerable, frail, and capable of suffocating from the inability to breathe. All his techniques and insights were lost to him at that instant.

It was an utterly bizarre scene, yet strangely, no one seemed surprised or found it unbelievable.

For a figure had appeared on the battlefield. No one saw how he materialized; it was as if he had always been there, yet no one had noticed him before.

His posture was casual as he stood simply in the sky, clad in a white robe, spotless and free of dust; apart from that, nothing else adorned him.

Yet whoever laid eyes on him for the first time would instantly perceive him as the very center of the Vast Expanse—the sole existence since the dawn of the universe.

Wherever he was, the heavens and earth existed for his sake; all things in the multiverse rose and fell with his emotions; and the ages shifted with a single thought of his.

"I did not wish to reveal myself, yet you have forced me to do so."

"But once I truly do appear, you will likely begin to regret it."

“So why bother?”

Gu Changge’s expression was utterly impassive; no one could discern the depths of his gaze. It seemed as though he were appraising Li Qingxu before him, yet at the same time, as though he were piercing through the vast expanse of time and space to gaze into the unknown depths beyond.

"Alliance Leader..."

The many powerful figures of the Heaven Slaying Alliance shouted in unison, filled with immense excitement and elation.

With Gu Changge’s appearance, the Alliance Master, the Lord of Restraint, and the others trapped within the ink-wash painting were instantly freed from their confinement, and the ink-wash world swirling around them dissipated completely.

They looked at Li Qingxu, who was now unable to even move a muscle, their expressions a mix of mockery and coldness.

Clearly, had Gu Changge not appeared, they would likely still be trapped within that ink painting. Such a tactic, amounting to a “dimension-lowering strike,” was indeed beyond their current ability to counter.

The entire Vast Expanse fell silent in that moment.

Many members of the Heavenly Dao Alliance found themselves instantly unable to move; no matter how extraordinary their innate talents might be, they could not escape.

In fact, they could distinctly feel the fragments of the Heavenly Dao within their bodies trembling…

Indeed, the fragments of the Heavenly Dao—which had previously been as lifeless as inanimate objects—were trembling, radiating a palpable sense of fear.

This filled them with dread. The fragments of the Heavenly Dao were, after all, part of the Heavenly Will, representing the supreme authority of the Heavenly Dao itself—and yet now they were actually showing fear toward the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance standing before them.

And the moment he saw Gu Changge appear, Li Qingxu suddenly understood why even the Earth Ancestor feared him.

The very ability to suppress the birth and death of this realm without a sound had already surpassed the limits of the Vast Expanse.

He now understood that the gap between them was beyond his wildest imagination—a realm he could never, ever hope to reach.

"I had no intention of interfering with the evolution and succession of the Vast Expanse. Why do you still force me to intervene? Do you think this will make your deaths more meaningful?"

"Or do you think that by wriggling about for a while before I crush you with a single flick of my wrist, you’ll die with a sense of purpose?"

"An ant is still an ant. No matter how high you leap, in my eyes, you remain nothing more than an ant."

Gu Changge spoke in a flat tone, yet merely by looking at Li Qingxu, the latter felt as though all his secrets were laid bare before him, as if he had become a transparent bubble.

His face froze, his skin went numb, and he couldn’t even form a complete sentence.

He felt as though he had been plunged into the depths of the ocean, with overwhelming pressure surging in from all directions, nearly suffocating him, leaving him gasping for air.

“See? Now that I’ve truly revealed myself, you’re starting to regret it again. So what’s the point?”

Gu Changge shook his head gently, his gaze sweeping toward the battlefield beyond the heavens.

The colossal form, originally inlaid with three fragments of the Heavenly Dao and shrouded in the depths of the infinite world plane, suddenly stiffened. Countless, densely packed cracks swiftly spread across its entire surface, and finally, it exploded with a thunderous boom.

The three-headed entity facing it finally felt the pressure on its body ease, though it remained wounded and bleeding.

This great battle had placed immense pressure on him; he had been barely holding on the entire time. The slightest misstep would have meant his death and the collapse of his path.

Three fragments of the Heavenly Dao shot forth like streaks of light, eventually hovering gently before Gu Changge, radiating a misty glow as the profound truths of all worlds unfolded within them.

The entire Vast Expanse fell into a deathly silence.

Lin Busi, Feng Yuqiu, and the others could not help but tremble, their eyes filled with terror.

In their eyes, Senior Brother Li Qingxu—who had seemed nearly omnipotent, even capable of forging the path ahead—could now not even move a muscle; instead, he had reached out to clasp his own throat.

This bizarre scene sent a chill down their spines, making them tremble from head to toe.

The other members of the Alliance of Heavenly Daoists were equally immobilized, not even daring to breathe heavily.

Originally, possessing a fragment of the Heavenly Dao had made them feel favored by the Heavenly Will—beloved children of the Heavenly Dao, chosen ones of destiny, blessed with divine fortune.

But now, even the Heavenly Dao Fragments within their bodies were trembling, struggling to break free.

This stark sense of disillusionment was difficult for them to accept.

"Forget it—you can’t speak right now. Besides, you have no right to speak to me. Have the master behind you come out."

"Or perhaps I should take matters into my own hands and force him to reveal himself?" Gu Changge’s gaze swept over Li Qingxu.

Li Qingxu trembled all over, feeling as if he were about to explode—a state where all traces of his existence would vanish, leaving no trace of causality. Under the weight of that single gaze, he could no longer bear it.

"I’ve known this all along. This was merely an experiment. You wanted to be an observer, lurking behind the scenes, watching everything unfold and shift."

"The fragmentation of the authority of the Heavenly Dao was also your doing. Your origins are even more unfathomable than I had imagined."

At that moment, a dazzling shower of light rained down from the dark void, and a vague figure appeared. Clad in feathered robes, his true features obscured, he stood there, yet seemed to soothe the surging waves of chaos.

"The leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance?"

Gu Changge looked at the figure and smiled faintly.

“This is our first meeting, Leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance.”

The Alliance Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance had appeared, and he spoke, sizing up Gu Changge before him…

Just as Gu Changge had founded the Heaven Slaying Alliance, the Heaven’s Way Alliance that existed today had also adopted the name to stand as an equal rival. Calling itself the Heaven’s Way Alliance implied that those holding the authority of the Heavenly Dao acted on behalf of Heaven, serving as the future pillars of the Dao.

At this moment, the gaze of all the Vast Expanse realms converged here. Many supreme beings were either trembling with awe or filled with disbelief.

After all, the existence of the Heavenly Dao Alliance was known only to a select few supreme beings; ordinary Dao Realm beings might never have even heard of this faction.

Many high-ranking members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance had also realized that the mastermind behind this earth-shaking war was, quite clearly, none other than the leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

Mo Tong, Mu Yan, and the others, having escaped from Wei Shou’s grasp, were subsequently led away from that battlefield beyond the heavens by the high-ranking members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

Even figures like the Lord of Restraint and the Administration Lord were merely watching from a distance at this moment; they, too, had no idea what kind of being this suddenly appearing Alliance Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance truly was.

The waters of the Vast Expanse were far deeper than they had ever imagined. They had believed that the Pathfinders were already a miracle in themselves, having blazed a trail never before seen—after all, no one had ever followed in their footsteps.

Yet the Heavenly Dao Alliance had managed, through fragments of the Heavenly Dao, to create a being comparable to the Pathfinder—how could this fail to shake them to their core?

"Your actions do align with my understanding of you, but your power is truly beyond my comprehension. After clashing with the Heavenly Will and shattering it, you still maintain this state—what exactly are you?" The leader of the Dao Alliance stared at Gu Changge, as if trying to pierce through all his secrets.

"What qualifies you to ask me that?" Gu Changge replied with interest.

As his words fell, the entire Vast Expanse seemed to tremble. An intangible aura radiated outward, and the area surrounding him began to shatter and disintegrate, as if it had become absolute nothingness.

"Perhaps I can offer you some insight. Since you’ve chosen to be a behind-the-scenes observer, it suggests you don’t wish to alter the trajectory of the Vast Expanse—which is why you won’t intervene lightly." The leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance spoke calmly; he seemed unconcerned by the changes around him, nor did he care that he might dissolve along with the void.

“Now that you mention it, I’m actually intrigued. But on what grounds do you believe you can offer me a perspective?” Gu Changge, however, remained thoroughly interested.

The Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance looked at him, his tone taking on a new emotional depth, tinged with a previously unseen air of condescension and arrogance. “In the prehistoric era when I established the Eternal Civilization, how magnificent and flourishing it was!My civilization spanned countless timespaces and universes, giving rise to innumerable mighty beings, and repeatedly challenged the authority of the Heavenly Dao. Though the Vast Expanse has paved the way forward, giving rise to figures like you, the Leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, in my eyes, it remains as weak as a tottering infant.”

"An era like this holds no hope whatsoever. Aside from you, the Alliance Leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance—an unpredictable presence—the current generation lacks even the qualifications to challenge the authority of the Heavenly Dao."

"Do you think that without you, this era—relying on the entire Vast Expanse and its myriad realms—could stand against the Ancient Hidden Civilization? What hope is there, relying on the so-called Nine Heavens, or on those past Heaven Slayers?"

"When the Reckoner of the Land Of Truth descends, all shall return to the Void. Everyone will be buried, reduced to ashes without exception."

The voice of the Alliance Master of the Heavenly Dao was deafening, reverberating throughout all realms, causing many supreme beings to tremble inwardly and their expressions to shift dramatically. They never imagined that their era would be viewed with such contempt.

However, figures like the Lord of Restraint and the Master of Administration did not refute this; in their eyes, it was indeed the truth.

Gu Changge remained silent, continuing to observe the Alliance Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance with keen interest.

"You have helped this era cheat the system, aiding them time and again to survive calamities they could never have endured on their own. Even the Pioneers only reached this point with your assistance. Without you, they are nothing."

“And your existence, to the Vast Expanse of all worlds, is essentially a ‘dimension-lowering strike.’ You yourself must know just how high your true realm has reached.”

“That is why you wish to be an observer, watching the Vast Expanse of multiverses slowly evolve under your guidance. But how long will this process take?”

"One era, ten eras, or tens of thousands of eras?"

"During this period, can you guarantee that the Land Of Truth will never interfere with this side? From the perspective of the Land Of Truth, the entire Vast Expanse will remain on a predetermined trajectory; with the end of every chaotic cosmic era, it will dissolve into nothingness, and everything will begin anew."

"Your observations are meaningless. Their ultimate fate is to perish and return to dust, with everything reverting to the very beginning of creation."
