The beam of light, resembling a shooting star, sliced through the depths of space and hurtled toward the center of Su Beichen’s forehead. Before he could react, it had already merged with him.

"What is this?"

Su Beichen froze for a moment, then a torrent of information surged through his mind like a tidal wave. “So that’s it—this is actually a fragment of the Heavenly Dao possessing supreme power. Could it be that Heaven, seeing my misfortune, has taken pity on me and is helping me deal with this vile beast?”

He rapidly processed the endless stream of information in his mind, feeling as though his skull had been split open, flooded with countless insights into the mysteries of this world—cultivation, enlightenment, experience, systems, realms, and innumerable truths… In that moment, Su Beichen gained profound clarity.

"It’s merely a monstrous beast at the Immortal King realm, yet it dares to treat me with such contempt, feeding on the blood and flesh of the living beings of this lower realm."

Realizing the beast’s cultivation level, Su Beichen’s eyes blazed with fury, his qi and blood surging to the heavens. Behind him appeared nine true dragons, as if forged from black, mysterious, immortal gold, and nine colossal elephants, as lustrous and ancient as white jade, trampled beneath his feet.

That mysterious fragment of the Heavenly Dao had bestowed upon him the innate ability known as the Invincible Golden Body, rendering his physical form indestructible and granting him the terrifying power to shatter the heavens and destroy the earth with a single punch.

Upon contact, it possessed the property of "absolute hardness."

"Die!"

Though he had only just ascended to the Upper Realm and his cultivation base was still unstable, Su Beichen had regained the invincible mindset with which he had once swept through the Lower Realm. He launched a punch skyward, charging at that terrifying creature, determined to avenge his wife’s death.

"You insignificant ant..."

The terrifying creature’s eyes flickered with contempt and mockery. It paid no heed whatsoever to the reckless, death-defying strike from this mere ant before it. Over the course of countless ages, it had seen far too many such beings who had ascended from the Lower Realm.

Not even having solidified their True Immortal realm, yet daring to challenge a peerless Immortal King?

Utterly reckless.

BOOM!!!

Yet in the very next instant, faced with Su Beichen’s furious strike, the terrifying entity’s casual downward swipe of a claw was blown apart. A vast, lingering fist-light, like a rising sun, cast a radiance that overwhelmed past and present, rolling forth to engulf and shatter it completely.

Amidst the blood splattering across the sky and excruciating pain.

The terrifying creature was enraged, filled with disbelief. It couldn’t fathom how a being who hadn’t even solidified his True Immortal realm could possibly injure him.

Just moments ago, it had toyed with him as if he were a mere insect.

"No, this fellow seems to have obtained something just now. Though it was but a fleeting glimpse, it has shaken me to my core."

In its massive, blood-red eyes, time began to rewind. The area radiating from the present moment started flowing backward. It saw clearly the scene from a few breaths ago: a streak of mystical light had sunk into the body of that ant before it. That was why he had suddenly possessed such peerless fist power.

"Those who ascend from the lower realms often possess unfathomable fortune and destiny—it is indeed true. This opportunity is now mine."

Its gaze grew greedy and intense, and its colossal, peerless true form finally emerged slowly from the boundless darkness of the cosmos.

It was a terrifying creature resembling a black bear with wings on its back. Its shattered claws began to heal, as orderly laws and principles flowed through them.

Su Beichen’s earlier punch had failed to fully wound it; the gap in their realms was simply too vast, and he was by no means a match for such a formidable opponent.

Relying on the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, his physical body had reached the absolute limit of what this realm could endure, yet he still could not hope to challenge a Immortal King when his own True Immortal cultivation was not yet firmly established.

After that punch, Su Beichen was completely exhausted. It was the strongest punch he could muster at his current peak; the fact that it managed to injure the Immortal King’s claw was already a miracle.

He was well aware of this, which is why he had to break through quickly—at least to the late True Immortal stage—before he could slay this terrifying creature.

"Qingrou, Muwan, wait for me. I’ll avenge you soon."

Gritting his teeth and his eyes red with fury, Su Beichen waved his hand, pulling Zhu Wanjun and his other wives and concubines into the inner realm. Then, without looking back, he flew toward the distant Haoyu Flying Physician.

A gentleman’s revenge can wait ten years.

Now that he possessed a fragment of the Heavenly Dao, he would eventually be able to slay this terrifying creature.

However, Su Beichen had vastly underestimated the speed of an Immortal. With a mere flick of its massive hand, the terrifying creature sent ripples through half the cosmos, tearing the entire Milky Way apart. Even after fleeing billions of miles, it still caught up to him.

“You insignificant worm, where do you think you’re running?”

"From the west of the Yu Hua Universe to the very edge of the frontier, this is all my territory. I hunt and feed here—I won’t tolerate anyone blocking my path."

"Do you perhaps think you can tear the cosmos apart and flee into the Vast Expanse? Haha, even an Immortal Emperor must tread cautiously when crossing that realm. With your cultivation, you aren’t even qualified to approach its shores—a single wave would be enough to obliterate your body and soul ten thousand times over."

The terrifying creature, shaped like a black bear, boomed with a voice like a great bell, shaking the eight corners of the world. It transformed into a rainbow-colored beam of light, overshadowing the starry realm as it rapidly closed in. Simultaneously, it opened its mouth and sucked in, instantly forming a black hole that caused the surrounding matter to collapse ceaselessly.

Su Beichen was sucked in uncontrollably, his body on the verge of being swallowed whole.

At the critical moment, his eyes flashed with determination. He bit through his tongue, igniting his primordial essence, energy, and spirit. Flames of the Dao erupted from his entire body, and cracks began to appear in his still-unstable Dao Fruit. Fortunately, his speed of escape increased once more, and he quickly broke free from the black hole’s grasp.

Thanks to the secret techniques he had discovered while in the Lower Realm—techniques that delved into the Dao of supreme speed—he was able to unleash their full potential even in this Upper Realm.

Meanwhile, that terrifying, black-bear-like creature was thoroughly enraged. As an Immortal King, to be unable to capture even such a tiny insect—if word of this got out, he would surely be mocked by the other Immortal Kings of the Yu Hua Universe.

It opened its mouth and roared, unleashing a vast, surging black light that resembled a starry river. Wherever it swept, all the stars exploded, and a clearly visible fissure, resembling an abyss, was torn open in the cosmos.

Su Beichen felt a chill run through his entire body; there was simply no way to evade it. No matter how fast he moved, he could never outpace this strike.

As the black light descended, he suddenly felt the most desperate agony he had ever experienced in his life. His body seemed to be exploding; destructive laws coiled around his flesh, intent on extinguishing all life force.

Buzz!!!

Yet in the very next instant, a faint golden light surged forth, swiftly enveloping Su Beichen’s body. His flesh, which had been on the verge of shattering, miraculously healed.

"This is..."

"The innate ability granted to me by the Fragment of the Heavenly Dao—Absolute Fortitude—has taken effect. My physical body has now reached the level of an Indestructible Golden Body. Even if shattered by a force so terrifying it’s nearly unbearable, it will heal and return to its original state the very next moment, achieving the purpose of Absolute Fortitude."

Though Su Beichen was stunned, having cultivated for nearly ten thousand years, he quickly grasped the situation.

His heart instantly settled. Having only just obtained the Fragment of the Heavenly Dao, he hadn’t yet fully grasped its nature. With such a heaven-defying endowment within him, it was no wonder he remained undefeated.

"What? That guy clearly shattered and exploded—how did he recover so quickly?"

"He hasn’t even solidified his True Immortal realm. How could he possibly achieve rebirth from a single drop of blood under my power? If he possessed such strength, he wouldn’t have had to flee in the first place."

"Could it be the strange power bestowed upon him by that mysterious speck of light?"

The terrifying, black-bear-like creature’s eyes burned like a blood-red moon. At first, it was stunned, but then it quickly snapped out of it, its breathing growing increasingly ragged. Such an opportunity—if it could seize it—would make breaking through to the Immortal Emperor realm, or even becoming a Dao Realm existence, a mere formality.

It went mad, determined to catch up to Su Beichen at any cost and devour him whole.

"This monster must have guessed the transformation within me. I must flee, find a place to recover, and then break through..."

Su Beichen immediately fled into the distance. After his physical body had been shattered, his divine soul had also sustained massive damage. Moreover, unlike his physical body, the injuries to his divine soul could not be healed directly. He feared that the monster might sense this; if it were to employ divine soul techniques, he would be doomed.

In the blink of an eye, Su Beichen streaked through a star system with a whoosh. Ahead, he sensed a dense aura of life surging toward him. Clearly, a civilization dwelled there—a life-bearing star system teeming with sentient beings, numbering well over hundreds of billions.

His eyes darted back at the terrifying black bear relentlessly pursuing him. Without a moment’s hesitation, he headed straight for the cluster of life-bearing stars.

“Who dares trespass upon the territory of the Tengmu Clan?”

Before Su Beichen could even draw near, a thunderous shout rang out, shaking the surrounding star system. A sky-filling surge of green life energy shot toward the heavens, and a colossal figure—resembling a god of life—materialized there, its Dharma Body soaring over ten thousand zhang in height, radiating an aura that sent shivers down the spine.

This was a being that had transcended the realm of ordinary True Immortals; it was likely not far from the level of an Immortal King.

Su Beichen’s eyes flashed with ferocity. Without a word, relying on his indestructible physical form, he charged directly toward that emerald-green, lush Life Star.

"You're asking for death."

The being, its green light piercing the heavens, was taken aback at first, then erupted in fury. Vast, ocean-like life energy surged, transforming into countless sharp spears coiled with boundless killing intent, piercing toward Su Beichen.

He showed no fear, allowing these spears to pierce him through, letting bloody holes open in his body, all to plunge downward.

It was only then that the creature that had attacked seemed to realize what was happening. Its expression shifted repeatedly as it sensed a terrifying, overwhelming pressure descending from afar—it was unmistakably the aura of an Immortal King.

The figure that had just charged through appeared to be fleeing its pursuit.

"Greetings, Immortal King Xiongdi."

It hurriedly bowed in respect. In the Western Cosmos, there was only this one Immortal King; their clan’s territory, along with their homeland, fell within his domain. Every thousand years, their clan was required to pay homage and present tribute.

“Get out of my way.”

However, it received no response. The terrifying creature named Xiongdi, its eyes now bloodshot, swung its massive claws toward him, tearing through space and time, leaving a trail of countless shards of cosmic laws in its wake.

Its expression was cold and furious. It never expected Su Beichen to have evaded it for so long. More importantly, the opportunity Su Beichen possessed must not be known to any other beings.

BOOM!!!

A deafening explosion echoed across the heavens. The True Immortal of the Tengmu Clan was instantly and gruesomely killed by Xiongdi’s single claw, exploding on the spot, his body and soul utterly annihilated.

The other True Immortals clearly lacked Su Beichen’s extraordinary physical constitution; even their divine souls were instantly obliterated.

The chasm between Immortal Kings and True Immortals was even greater than the gap between Enlightened Ones and True Immortals.

The Yu Hua Universe is rich in primordial energy, and while True Immortals are numerous, there are only a handful of Immortal Kings.

"Ancestor Teng San..."

Green blood rained down from the sky, accompanied by wails and sobs. Within the Teng Clan’s territory, countless clan members witnessed this scene with their own eyes, trembling with rage.

This was clearly a calamity that had come out of nowhere. They had no idea what was happening; the Ancestor who had stepped forward to intervene had met a gruesome end.

Seizing this opportunity, Su Beichen caught his breath and immediately cast the secret technique he had acquired in the Lower Realm. He altered his aura, as well as his appearance and form, and swiftly descended onto that Life Star. During his time in the Lower Realm, he had faced repeated assassination attempts by his enemies. Through countless escapes, he had mastered the art of disguise—even his divine soul’s aura and the very essence of his life could be concealed.

As long as he could evade them for just a moment, he would have a chance to strike back.

"I’m sorry, I didn’t mean to divert the danger elsewhere."

"Once I’ve recovered, I’ll definitely help you avenge yourselves."

Su Beichen landed in a vast, ancient forest. Glancing back at the surging, overwhelming killing intent in the depths of space, a fleeting trace of regret flashed in his eyes as he murmured to himself.

At that very moment, throughout the entire territory of the Vine-Wood Clan, a chorus of furious, enraged voices echoed. Deep within the ancestral temple, several stone tablets shattered, and from the cracks emerged vines as thick as mountains, as if capable of tearing apart the heavens and the cosmos at any moment.

"Xidi Immortal King, what do you mean by this? Do you think the Vinewood Clan has no ruler?"

A hoarse, aged voice roared in fury.

Amidst surging green light reaching toward the heavens, like a sea of life, an elderly man with snow-white hair and deeply wrinkled skin appeared. He still carried the scent of earth, and a faint green glow shone in his eyes; his aura was unmistakably that of a True Immortal at the peak of his power.

...

"Back in the day, our Tengmu Clan was also led by a Quasi-King. Had we not failed at that critical juncture, how could we have fallen to this state of being bullied at will?"

"Please summon the shell left behind by our Provisional King ancestor."

Another voice rang out, equally aged, belonging to yet another white-haired elder, though his expression was sharp and icy.

They were already aware of what had just transpired. Though they knew this was a case of someone diverting blame, the fact that this Bear Enemy Immortal King had crushed their clan’s Third Elder to death without even granting a chance for an explanation was simply too much.

Amidst the mournful cries and ritual chants of all the Vine Clan members, within a towering hall erected at the center of their ancestral lands, a gentle green light began to bloom.It was a piece of withered wood, less than half a person’s height, bearing human-like skin patterns. As the various sounds gathered there, a dazzling, brilliant light gradually emerged, as if a towering figure were slowly rising to sit.

A terrifying aura of a quasi-Immortal King surged high above the Vinewood Clan. The withered stump rose into the air, rising and falling like a rising sun.

"You seek death. Since you dare to block my path, this King shall annihilate your clan today and track down that little thief."

Xiongdi Immortal King’s eyes were crimson and icy. Having attained the Dao a million years ago, he had never once regarded the Tengmu Clan with any respect. To him, all beings below the rank of Immortal King were mere ants; in the entire Xixi Hua Universe, there were scarcely a handful who could stand on equal footing with him.

To him, annihilating this clan was as easy as casually destroying a mountain.

“That fluctuation of energy just now—it seems to be a battle between powerful beings in this universe?”

In a barren star system not far from the Tengmu Clan’s territory, Mo Tong suddenly raised his gaze toward the distance, sensing something.

"Wasn’t the strongest being this world has ever produced merely an Immortal Emperor? In this current era, the highest rank is an Immortal King—so why am I encountering a battle between Immortal Kings right off the bat?" Mu Yan looked astonished.

Mo Tong said nothing. She waved her hand, and the Hundred Million Souls Banner in her grasp suddenly began to tremble gently. Banner inscriptions, as if etched in scripture, bloomed with a clear radiance, billowing gray mist.

"The Hundred Million Souls Banner is reacting. These are the fluctuations of a fragment of the Heavenly Dao."

“There are fragments of the Heavenly Dao in that place.”

Her gaze fell upon the area as she stated with certainty.

Mu Yan’s face lit up with joy. “We found it so easily. Since only Immortal Kings exist in this world, won’t this fragment of the Heavenly Dao be a piece of cake for the two of us?”

At the same time, within the barrier of space where the Yuhua Universe met the Vast Expanse of the outside world, layer upon layer of viscous, pitch-black substance flowed silently. Tentacles emerged from the gaps, gradually enveloping and devouring everything below, as if intent on devouring the vast universe beneath.

Thud, thud, thud...

A series of surging, startling heartbeats echoed ceaselessly, causing Feng Yuqiu, Lin Busi, and the others standing outside the barrier to look on with expressions of shock and uncertainty.

"Senior Brother Li, what exactly are we doing here?" Luo Wuyan couldn’t help but ask.

"Wait for it to awaken."

In the vision, high above the barrier of the Yu Hua Universe, droplets of viscous liquid were falling. Tentacles spread out, constantly intertwining and wrapping around one another, forming a nest-like structure that enveloped a massive, beating heart. Within that heart, a fragment of the Heavenly Dao emitted a misty, strange glow.
