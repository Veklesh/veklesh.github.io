The so-called Heavenly Dao Realm—whether in the spacetime of the old cosmic order, the spacetime of the new cosmic order, or the newly emerging spacetime where the old and new orders are merging—all point directly to the final path.

In the end, one’s very self may become the Heavenly Dao—the Heavenly Dao in its purest sense.

This has transcended the very essence of being a sentient being; not even a state of chaos remains—it is, in fact, nothing but pure will.

Will is immortal and indestructible, existing since time immemorial. With a single thought, heaven and earth are born; with another, all things come into being. Time and space are molded at will, and life and death hold no meaning whatsoever.

Within the realm governed by the Heavenly Dao, one is an omnipotent being.

"The essence of the Heavenly Dao Realm lies in transforming one’s very nature—a metamorphosis."

At this moment, on that battlefield of the Heavenly Dao Realm, various terrifying orders intertwined and collided. As two paths emerged, all manner of extraordinary phenomena descended, a sight that was truly shocking to behold.

Further on, the collisions gave birth to a vast river of Heavenly Dao laws as majestic as a starry galaxy. Of course, this was the spectacle witnessed by all living beings outside the Heavenly Dao battlefield—it appeared as though two paths were colliding.

In reality, the battlefield within was incredibly perilous. The Three-Headed Sovereign sensed the tenacity of his opponent; to be precise, he was actually contending with two fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

He had only just entered the Heavenly Dao Realm and could at best be considered a Heavenly Being of the first tier.

The boundless, colossal laws contained within the two fragments of the Heavenly Dao, along with various energies of calamity, surged mightily into Bi Wei’s body. With a single sweep of the giant elephant’s massive trunk, countless laws were shattered.

Amidst the Vast Expanse of the cosmos, Li Qingxu watched this scene unfold, his expression serene and unruffled.

Behind him, Feng Yuqiu and the others could not help but tremble with awe; after all, they were merely the fortunate recipients of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, having never before witnessed such a colossal spectacle.

It could be said that at this moment, the gaze of the Vast Expanse was focused here.

They, too, were experiencing the true weight of being the focus of billions of eyes.

BOOM!!!

On the Battlefield of the Heavenly Dao, accompanied by a terrifying explosion, boundless energy surged forth. Ultimately, Bi Wei—who possessed two fragments of the Heavenly Dao—had his body pierced by the Three-Headed Lord. His massive, decaying form slowly plummeted downward, crashing toward the Vast Expanse of the multiverse.

The Three-Headed Master was covered in blood; both of his heads had nearly been blown apart. He was gravely wounded. The collision of the two paths could be described as a pure struggle between Daos.

Clearly, that decaying, fractured path had ultimately failed to prevail over him.

Even though Bi Wei possessed two fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

His figure reappeared amidst the Vast Expanse, gazing coldly at Li Qingxu, who stood in another void. The other’s presence could not be hidden from him.

Although Bi Wei had been defeated, with the fragments of the Heavenly Dao still within him, he remained in that state of being half-alive and half-dead; Bi Wei could not completely crush him.

Mu Yan, Mo Tong, and the others were still held in Bi Wei’s grasp; he had not released them even during that terrifying collision.

“The Path of Rebirth is truly extraordinary. Even with two fragments of the Heavenly Dao, one cannot stand against it?”

"If that is the case, what about three?" Li Qingxu sighed.

He suddenly beckoned, and a hazy fragment shrouded in infinite mysteries once again appeared in the palm of his hand, seemingly containing the profound truths of the myriad heavens.

Feng Yuqiu and the others were stunned by this sight; they could not fathom just how many fragments of the Heavenly Dao Senior Brother Li possessed.

This was something countless people in the Vast Expanse had longed for in their wildest dreams.

Yet in his hands, they seemed as common as cabbages on the street—as if he had an endless supply.

“Just how many fragments of the Heavenly Dao does this enigmatic fellow possess? Could it be that he can freely harness the full power of these fragments without even needing to fully comprehend them?”

"If three aren’t enough, then four or even ten."

"This is just too insane. It’s clear now—that massive, eerie corpse is merely a vessel and puppet he uses to contain the fragments of the Heavenly Dao."

Across the Vast Expanse of the cosmos, a tremor spread, accompanied by an unimaginable sound; many beings were so startled they could not speak.

Within the Heaven Slaying Alliance, Lord of Restraint and the others wore equally grave expressions. If this were truly the case, they would have to find a way to contact Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth.

After all, there were now only a handful of them left as the vanguard of the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

As for where Gu Changge was now, they had absolutely no idea.

Although the Three-Headed Lord was a Vanguard, he was already struggling to deal with Bi Wei, who harbored two fragments of the Heavenly Dao. If he were to harbor three fragments of the Heavenly Dao...

the Three-Headed Master would inevitably suffer a collapse of his Dao.

Unlike that half-living, half-dead being, if the Three-Headed Master’s path were to collapse, he would undoubtedly perish and his Dao would vanish.

"Act now. Suppress that mysterious figure."

“He certainly isn’t a Pathfinder; we just don’t know by what means he has suddenly grasped the secret of the Heavenly Dao Fragments.”

The Lord of Restraint and the Master of Judgment struck simultaneously.

Their figures stepped out of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, traversing billions of light-years to arrive just beyond the battlefield, locking onto Li Qingxu.

"I’ll leave them in your hands for now."

"I cannot be disturbed here."

Li Qingxu’s expression remained calm as he addressed Feng Yuqiu, Lin Busi, and the others beside him, as if bringing them here from the very beginning had been for this very purpose.

“Senior Brother Li, they are all renowned True Path-level beings in the Vast Expanse…”

Both Feng Yuqiu and Lin Busi were pale-faced, their throats parched.

Only Luo Wuyan looked eager to take on the challenge; she knew her innate talents were exceptional and that she might actually be able to hold her own against True Path-level beings.

"We don’t need to fight them. We just need to help Senior Brother Li buy some time." Her eyes blazed with fighting spirit as she fixed her gaze on the female Chief Steward.

She possessed the innate talent to seek good fortune and avoid misfortune.

Her innate talent told her that this great battle wasn’t particularly dangerous; it might just be a scare with no real harm, and afterward, there would likely be great rewards.

"You don't know when to quit."

The Lord of Restraint’s gaze was icy, radiating an overwhelming aura of authority. He didn’t even consider these holders of Heavenly Dao Fragments a threat; his true target was to subdue Li Qingxu, the instigator of it all.

Feng Yuqiu glanced at Lin Busi, then gritted his teeth and charged forward.

He didn’t know what effect his innate talent would have—that taking wives and concubines would make him stronger. At this critical moment, could it truly stand up to a Supreme Shattering being?

Before obtaining the Heavenly Dao Fragment, he wouldn’t even have been able to catch a glimpse of such a figure.

A single breath from his opponent could obliterate the entire universe he inhabited.

However, Lin Busi’s innate talent was absolute immortality—perhaps in a situation like today’s, it might actually prove useful.

BOOM!!!

Soon, Luo Wuyan charged toward the female Sect Master. She was surrounded by a moon-like glow, with layers of ethereal light floating around her—an aura of profound mystery, as if she were the embodiment of another realm within the original cosmic timeline.

She cast a slightly surprised glance at this “ant.” Though she knew he possessed fragments of the Heavenly Dao, she remained perplexed.

To charge at her so fearlessly—doesn’t he fear being crushed to death by her?

She extended a hand and struck out flatly toward Luo Wuyan. This mere Dao Realm cultivator—under normal circumstances, a single glance from her would have reduced him to dust.

But now, just to be safe, her palm strike would engulf all things and obliterate the cosmos. Never mind the Dao Realm—even an End of the Path-level figure struck by this would explode on the spot, their body and Dao vanishing without a trace.

Puff…

A cloud of blood mist exploded outward, and a trace of disbelief lingered on Luo Wuyan’s otherwise unremarkable face.

She suddenly turned her head, gazing at a figure that had appeared in the nearby void. It was a strikingly beautiful woman with aloof, delicate features, clad in a blue robe. Though stunningly beautiful, her entire being exuded a chilling aura.

She raised her wrist; an ancient-looking ring on her finger was emitting a faint glow, as if it were affecting the entire world.

Witnessing this scene, the Sect Master paused for a moment, taken aback. He hadn’t expected to kill her so easily—wasn’t the possessor of a Fragment of the Heavenly Dao supposed to be nearly impossible to slay?

At that moment, the entire Vast Expanse, which had been watching the events unfold, fell into a similar silence. Many of the most powerful beings stood frozen in shock, unable to believe their own eyes.

Killed that easily?

Or was it simply because the Lord of the Realm was too powerful?

Feng Yuqiu and Lin Busi, who had been preparing to strike, also froze in place, their scalps prickling with dread. Luo Wuyan, who had been the first to attack just moments ago, was dead.

Didn’t she possess the innate ability to seek good fortune and avoid misfortune? Knowing it was a death sentence, why would she deliberately throw her life away?

Li Qingxu furrowed his brow as he looked at the woman who had suddenly appeared, his suspicions confirmed.

"It seems she is the prodigy supported by the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance. The power within her can suppress the innate talents bestowed upon you by the fragments of the Heavenly Dao."

“However, you need not worry. She has already exhausted all her current abilities suppressing Junior Sister Luo.”

His expression was impassive; he showed no concern whatsoever for Luo Wuyan’s death.

Nan Qing, who had appeared on this battlefield, raised her hand and summoned the Heavenly Dao Fragment floating within the blood mist. She drew it toward her and, in full view of the countless supreme experts of the Vast Expanse, took it away with complete confidence.

The Alliance Master naturally recognized Nan Qing. She merely nodded slightly, having guessed that it was Nan Qing’s power that had suppressed the fragment of the Heavenly Dao.

The other party’s status was somewhat unique; she shared an indescribable, ambiguous relationship with the Alliance Leader, Gu Changge.

Naturally, she did not ask any further.

“Hah. Relying on the power of a fragment of the Heavenly Dao, one is ultimately nothing more than a rootless floating weed.”

The Lord of Restraint intervened. Seeing the Master of the Bureau effortlessly kill one of them, she pointed a finger forward, and a terrifying black hole instantly appeared ahead, poised to devour Li Qingxu and the others.

Lin Busi steeled himself and charged to the forefront. In an instant, a horrifying scream echoed throughout the entire universe. His body was torn apart and swallowed whole by the black hole, his flesh and blood vanishing without a trace.

However, in the next instant, an invisible, colossal power seemed to envelop the area. His vanished flesh began to coalesce once more, and his shattered skeleton reassembled. His entire body rapidly recovered, though his face remained deathly pale and filled with terror. The scene of his death moments ago still lingered vividly in his mind.

He had truly died once.

"If I can’t kill you once, what about twice or three times? What about a billion times?"

The Lord of Restraint’s pride was deeply wounded. An ant in his eyes had somehow evaded complete annihilation. He had clearly erased all traces of causality, yet the fragments of the Heavenly Dao had forcibly resurrected him.

Was this the gift of immortality?

Puff…

As the Lord of Restraint unleashed his mental pressure, that patch of space-time warped, and Lin Busi exploded once more.

Taking advantage of this moment, Li Qingxu stepped forward, ignoring the Three-Headed Master’s obstruction. In an instant, he vanished and appeared beside Bi Wei, whose body had been severely wounded, gently placing another fragment of the Heavenly Dao inside him.

"Stand up. Fight for us once more." He spoke, his voice filled with reassurance.
