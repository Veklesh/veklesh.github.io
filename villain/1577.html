Among the Four Heavenly Seal Clans, only the Absolute Yin Tower possesses a single "anomalous object."

This was indeed a ceremonial robe.

Even the kings of various tribes, wishing to enter that place for an audience, had to prostrate themselves with every step, displaying the utmost reverence.

How, then, did this woman manage to leave?

In the Land Of Truth, no living being dares to impersonate a creature from that place.

"Carrying a foreign object, and hailing from there—this woman’s cultivation and strength aren’t particularly high, yet she dares to be so presumptuous?"

Tai Mengyi’s gaze darkened slightly; he chose not to continue the struggle—nor did he have the courage to do so.

But if this object had been bestowed by those three, then it would be no surprise.

The woman in white robes, enveloped in a faint glow, wore an expression of extreme coolness and calm.

"Yes..."

If this woman had been on a special “mission” when she left, the consequences of their interference would be unimaginable.

“In that case, the glimmer of hope we saw in the Xuan Turtle’s divination shell might lie with this woman. Since she managed to leave that place, she must have the means to escape unscathed.”

Tai Mengyi, Yan Huosun, and the others all turned their gaze toward the woman in white.

“I have no solution either.”

“This object serves only for self-defense; it has no other use,” the woman in white replied.

At first, they had all thought they were heading to the Vast Expanse to pick up bargains, and to sweep away the creatures there in the process, seeking provisions. Little did they know that they couldn’t even set foot there—they would be trapped and die here.

They could already foresee that they would certainly face punishment from the Council of Truth, but compared to being trapped here in a situation where they had no chance of survival, what did that punishment amount to?

The four acted in unison, simultaneously invoking the Heavenly Seal Summoning Charms within their bodies.

A humming resonance!!!

Four dazzling, blinding beams of light suddenly rose and fell within this vast, mist-shrouded void of annihilation. Tai Mengyi, Yan Huo, and Chong Mingxue also moved to act, each unleashing their purest power, which converged upon their respective Heavenly Seals.

The might of the Heavenly Seal was sufficient to obliterate and erase any universe, and equally capable of creating or destroying any Civilization.

BOOM!!!

These clan leaders of the Heavenly Seal clans were among the very best outside the Nine True Clans. Yet here, they were unable to withstand a single palm strike?

Yet upon closer inspection, one could discern the evolution of profound and mysterious laws—chaos, heaven and earth, sun and moon, rivers and mountains—encompassing the ancient history of Civilization, representing the ultimate, the supreme, and the peerless.

The Absolute Yin Tower even sensed that its current Heavenly Being status was on the verge of collapse, showing signs of shattering; it was simply unable to withstand the aura emanating from that place.

His other hand rested behind his back; he made no unnecessary movements, yet simply standing there, he seemed to embody the very end and limits of all things, making Zhe dare not meet his gaze.

In the chaotic cosmic eras of the past, even when anomalies that defied the rules of order emerged from the Vast Expanse, they ultimately perished at the gates leading to the Land Of Truth, never even coming into contact with that realm.

How on earth did he reach this level in the Vast Expanse, a desolate place?

Jueyin Lou knew full well that he was no match for the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

He could only hope to make him back down by demonstrating the true depth of the Land Of Truth.

Gu Changge stood motionless in the void of annihilation, casting only a casual glance at the group without uttering a single word.
