"Rise up and fight for us once more."

As Li Qingxu gently placed the fragment of the Heavenly Dao beside Bi Wei’s side,

The other two fragments of the Heavenly Dao within it also erupted with a dazzling, ethereal light, as if the profound truths of all worlds were rising and falling in fusion, as if the path of the present were being re-enacted before their eyes.

He knew full well that what he faced this time would be a terrifying pressure unlike anything he had ever encountered.

Bi Wei, possessing two fragments of the Heavenly Dao, had exhausted every means at his disposal and, relying on the Path of Rebirth, could barely hold his own. But now, Bi Wei gazed at the three fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

In a sense, Bi Wei now possessed the strength of the third level of the Heavenly Realm.

It must be noted that even Li Zu and Qiankun Laozu—the very first to become Pathfinders—could not match the power of the third tier of the Heavenly Realm at this stage.

BOOM!!!

Bi Wei’s massive trunk swung like a divine sword piercing through heaven and earth, roaring as it charged toward the Dongyue Mountains.

Compared to before, the power radiating from Bi Wei at this moment—a force capable of shaking the Vast Expanse—struck even greater fear into all living beings, illuminating the entire Land of Oblivion.

The Three-Headed Lord roared furiously. Carrying a body infinitely more massive than Wei Na’s, he charged into the battlefield beyond the heavens. Only such a lawless realm could accommodate the great battle of the pathfinders.

Otherwise, even a single wisp of energy released at this level would be more than enough to instantly crush the Hezhen Realm and the Hebao Temple.

"Three fragments of the Heavenly Dao are already the limit; even a single more would be too much for Biwei’s body to bear."

“Given the situation, where are you, Leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance? Will you not show yourself?”

Li Qingxu gazed at the great battle raging in that lawless realm beyond the heavens and murmured to himself.

He knew that Bi Wei’s condition no longer belonged to this era; he had long since perished. Yet the power within the fragments of the Heavenly Dao had allowed him to reappear in the present age to wage his final battle.

In truth, two fragments of the Heavenly Dao were already the absolute limit Bi Wei could bear, but Li Qingxu had underestimated the stability of the New Path.

The Three-Headed Master, who had only recently become a Pathfinder, had relied on the power of the Path to force a battle with Biwei to such an extent—this alone was shocking enough.

“Leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, what exactly are you trying to do? Even at this moment, you still refuse to show yourself?” Li Qingxu said.

As Bi Wei and the Three-Headed Master erupted into battle once more, two Paths belonging to different eras collided.

On this battlefield, numerous powerful figures from the Heaven Slaying Alliance also charged toward Li Qingxu and his companions.

The Lord of Restraint casually pointed a finger, piercing and shattering Lin Busi—who possessed an immortal body—only for him to reappear not far away shortly after.

This seemed to be true immortality—he was simply impossible to kill. It was as if a fragment of the Heavenly Dao carried his true soul, allowing him to maintain an indestructible form.

Unless one could completely annihilate the power of the Heavenly Dao fragment in that very instant, it was simply impossible to truly kill him.

"What a troublesome little ant."

With a flick of his finger, the Lord of Restraint unleashed an invisible domain that expanded like the rush of time itself, transforming into a prison that instantly sealed Lin Busi—who had just revived nearby—within its confines.

This was a grand formation of the Supreme Shattering tier; trapped within it, Lin Busi had absolutely no chance of breaking free.

This also rendered his immortal body incapable of functioning as it normally would.

Although Lin Busi possessed an immortal body that ensured his own survival, he was utterly helpless against this overwhelming formation. · No matter how he thrashed about, he could not tear it apart, much less break free.

"I’ll see just what kind of creature you really are."

The Lord of Restraint paid no further heed to Lin Busi, instead directing his bloodthirsty assault toward Ji Qingxu, the source of this entire situation.

He could clearly sense that, though Li Qingxu was mysterious, he was by no means an invincible foe; his aura fell far short of the level possessed by those who had gone before.

Perhaps all he relied on was that fragment of the Heavenly Dao.

BOOM!!!

The Lord of Restraint charged toward Li Qingxu.

Joining him in the attack were numerous powerful figures from the Heaven Slaying Alliance who had rushed to the scene, such as the Department Head and Gu Xihan, the First Ancestor of the Gu Clan. They all knew that this unremarkable-looking fellow was the mastermind behind it all.

"You can’t possibly hurt me. Though you are the strongest in this era of the Chaotic Heavens and Earth, without the appearance of the Alliance Leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, you would never have been able to see me."

"You don’t even have the qualifications to stand before me."

Li Qingxu’s tone was calm, devoid of any condescension; it seemed as though he were merely stating a simple truth.

With a mere wave of his hand, an insurmountable chasm suddenly appeared before the crowd, severing everything from him.

No power could reach him; he stood as if in a realm where this era—where Yao Youyu and Wang Shi did not exist—had never been.

Li Qingxu’s expression shifted slightly.

“You simply don’t understand, nor will you ever comprehend. A mayfly knows not the difference between morning and evening; a summer cicada knows not the passing of spring and autumn.”

"Your horizons are limited to this extent; momentary strength or weakness means absolutely nothing," he said calmly.

The powerful figures of the Heaven Slaying Alliance said nothing, but charged forward, unleashing a barrage of techniques.

At that moment, the Vast Expanse grew desolate, and the heavens and earth lost their luster. Endless chains of divine order and beams of cosmic law crisscrossed the void, as if intent on reshaping the entire universe.

Every wisp of energy triggered violent spatial tremors, causing vast swathes of spacetime to collapse, forming bottomless black holes.

On the other side, Nan Qing, relying on her ring, had helped the Alliance Master kill one Endowed Being, but she herself had run into trouble.

Aside from Feng Yuqiu and the others, it was clear that more members of the Alliance of Heavenly Dao had arrived on this battlefield.

With the threat from the Ancient Hidden Civilization gone and no Enforcers from the Land Of Truth present at the moment,

It could be said that the Vast Expanse was currently in a state of being beyond the Land Of Truth’s gaze; it was precisely in this state of chaos and disorder that those who had hidden themselves the deepest had emerged.

At this very moment, the ripples of this great battle were sweeping across the entire Vast Expanse of the Multiverse; even the most distant realms of the True Realm were engulfed by them.

Beyond the heavens, in the lawless realm, terrifying anomalies unfolded one after another on the battlefield.

The void was torn open by massive fissures, from which surged endless chaotic energy, as if it were devouring everything in its path.

In another realm, the zone representing the "Ugly Heaven" cycle.

The consciousnesses of the Plague Heaven, Decay Heaven, Rot Heaven, Chaos Heaven, and Lust Heaven—each a branch of the Heavenly Will—were also closely observing all this.

Does that not mean that, during a long-ago era of chaotic creation, beings similar to the Forerunners once existed?

Even the Lord of Restraint and the Command Lord—veteran Supreme Shattering and True Path-level beings—were utterly powerless to resist?
