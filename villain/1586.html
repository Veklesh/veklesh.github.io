"The Alliance of the Heavenly Dao is, at its core, a home for those who possess fragments of the Heavenly Dao. You are not required to do anything for the Alliance; however, should news or traces of a new holder of a Heavenly Dao fragment emerge in the Vast Expanse, you must ensure their safe return."

"Only by uniting all the power of the Heavenly Dao Fragments can we counter the schemes of the Heaven Slaying Alliance’s leader. He seeks to overturn history, taint the future, and erode the rules and order of all timelines. Such an entity cannot be tolerated by the multiverse," the Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance said calmly.

He sat cross-legged there, as if shrouded in an endless mist that made him invisible to all living beings—mysterious and ancient.

Feng Yuqiu’s heart gave a start. He had never imagined that the Heavenly Dao Alliance’s true purpose was to oppose the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

Even with a fragment of the Heavenly Dao in his possession, he dared not claim that he would one day be worthy of becoming a figure like the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

But soon, upon reflecting on the mysterious capabilities of the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao leader, he felt at ease once more.

"Go down now. The Martial Sovereign will introduce you to your new companions. Here, you will surely find greater peace and comfort than ever before, and you can focus on enhancing your strength with confidence."

The master of the Alliance of Heavenly Daoists waved his hand, and a blur of five-colored light swept over him, transporting Feng Yuqiu’s figure out of the tower.

When his vision cleared once more, he found himself outside, where the Martial Sovereign was looking at him with a smile.

Upon arriving here, the Martial Sovereign revealed his true form—a tall, imposing man with a somewhat menacing appearance. A fierce-looking scar ran across his face, and he was clad in pitch-black armor. Yet, as he smiled, there was a peculiar, unsettling quality to his expression.

"Senior..."

Feng Yuqiu could sense the other’s goodwill. He took a deep breath and regained his composure. He thought of his family; if he ever wanted to avenge them, he would have to rapidly elevate his strength to the End of the Path level, and then find that End of the Path-level figure named Yan Zhu.

"I’m sure the Master has already told you what needs to be said. Now, I’ll take you to meet the others."

"Here, aside from us, everyone else refers to the Master as the Alliance Leader. You needn’t be so formal with him. He has a gentle disposition and often offers guidance to others. If you have any questions about the Fragments of the Heavenly Dao, you may also seek his advice."

The Martial Sovereign nodded, then led the way, guiding Feng Yuqiu toward another area filled with palaces and towers.

Feng Yuqiu silently took note of all this.

Deep within the sea of consciousness at his brow, a mysterious fragment resembling a misty, swirling cloud seemed to float. It contained a vast array of phenomena, scattered like stars across the sky, with countless profound truths flowing through it at every moment. On its surface, it appeared as though there were millions of faint, tentacle-like filaments connecting to his Mud Pellet Palace.

A mirror, reflecting something.

It was none other than the fragments of the Heavenly Dao that countless beings in the outside world sought to vie for.

Feng Yuqiu recalled the night he had unexpectedly obtained the fragment of the Heavenly Dao. He had been resting in the courtyard, lamenting that he had no hope of attaining the Great Way in this lifetime, when suddenly a light like a falling meteor plummeted down...

"Brother Feng."

A voice tinged with laughter reached his ears. Feng Yuqiu’s thoughts snapped back to the present, and he realized that the Martial Sovereign had already brought him to the top of a flat mountain. The surroundings were serene and tranquil, dotted with ancient bamboo of various shades of green. Purple mist swirled, crimson clouds billowed, and the essence of heaven and earth permeated the air.As a silver waterfall cascaded down, it pooled into a crystal-clear lake at the base, where several figures had been waiting.

The one who spoke was a young man dressed in scholar’s robes, with a fair complexion. He wore a smile and appeared quite kind.

At his side stood two women of peerless beauty: one clutched a sword, the other cradled a zither. Their white robes fluttered gracefully; with delicate features and cloud-like hair, they resembled celestial consorts—clearly his attendants.

The other young men and women present also possessed extraordinary bearing. In particular, a young woman in a yellow robe—though not of the most striking beauty—exuded exceptional grace. With a figure as delicate as jade and skin as fair as ice, she swayed like a celestial flower. Her hair was styled in a fairy-like bun, her neck slender and graceful, and her bright eyes full of charm.adorned with a gold and a silver bracelet on her fair wrists. The ornaments shone brilliantly, as if radiating a boundless, immeasurable aura that encompassed the vast cosmos and the origin of all things.

In addition, there were members of other clans present, whom Feng Yuqiu had encountered shortly after his arrival.

“Li Qingxu, as one of the first to join the Alliance of Heavenly Daoists, you shall introduce these companions of yours to Feng Yuqiu.” The War Sovereign turned to the young man in the scholar’s robe who stood at the head of the group.

This place was clearly the innermost circle of the Alliance of Heavenly Daoists; every single person here was a possessor of a Heavenly Dao Fragment, without a single exception.

Feng Yuqiu’s gaze swept briefly over the crowd—there were easily dozens of people—and he was deeply shaken.

Over the years, the number of Heavenly Dao Fragment holders who had made a name for themselves across the Vast Expanse was actually quite small—fewer than one could count on two hands. Yet here, within the Heavenly Dao Alliance, there were so many. It was indeed as the Alliance’s master had said: an unseen force would gather them all together.

"Of course, that’s no problem. Whenever newcomers arrive, I’ll introduce them."

Li Qingxu smiled, exuding the bearing of a true leader. He stepped before Feng Yuqiu, gestured toward the various beings behind him, and began introducing their origins, identities, and the innate abilities associated with their Heavenly Dao Fragments. The beings he pointed to stepped forward a few paces, nodding in greeting with a most amiable demeanor.

"His name is Wang Zhuo, from the Qingyun Universe. His innate talent is replication; as long as he shakes hands with anyone, he can instantly replicate that person’s innate physical constitution and talents..." Li Qingxu pointed to a black-clad youth.

The black-clad youth appeared somewhat aloof, but he gave Li Qingxu the courtesy of a nod toward Feng Yuqiu.

Feng Yuqiu was stunned. Could one really replicate another’s innate physical constitution and talents simply by shaking hands?

He found he couldn’t gauge this person’s cultivation level—and it wasn’t just Wang Zhuo; the others were the same. Each seemed to possess a mysterious innate ability to conceal their true depth of cultivation.

"Her name is Luo Wuyan. She comes from the Dizhao Universe, and her innate talent is to seek good fortune and avoid misfortune..."

“His name is Fang Yu, from the Realm of Ten Thousand Laws. His innate talent is ‘Advance Draw.’ He can draw upon future strength in advance, but the price is that he must repay it through hard work afterward…”

Li Qingxu—introduced Feng Yuqiu.

The innate talents of every being left Feng Yuqi deeply shaken.

In comparison, his own innate talent—which required him to take concubines to grow stronger—seemed utterly ordinary. There was even one whose innate talent was immortality—absolute immortality, such that not even an End of the Path-level figure could kill him.

Others possessed the ability to simplify the complex; for instance, practicing the Great Sun Breathing Technique required only observing the sun’s breath to improve…

There are also those whose innate talent lies in drawing strength from others’ imagination; the stronger they appear in others’ minds, the greater the power they themselves gain.

By the time he reached the later descriptions, Feng Yuqiu was nearly numb.

It wasn’t until he heard about a fellow named Qi Sheng—whose innate talent was a “stat sheet” that allowed him to earn points through relentless cultivation, thereby breaking through his limits—that he finally felt a faint sense of superiority and breathed a sigh of relief.

If every member of the Alliance of Heavenly Dao was this incredibly gifted, he would have absolutely no presence here.

Li Qingxu had finished introducing all the members, yet he had not mentioned his own innate ability. However, Feng Yuqiu could guess that it must be terrifying—otherwise, it would be impossible for so many extraordinary individuals to hold him in such high regard, implicitly viewing him as the core leader of this small group.

"Cultivation is a mutually supportive process. If you have any needs, Brother Feng, please feel free to speak up."

"Don’t be fooled by the small number of core members in the Alliance of Heavenly Daoists. Behind each one lies immense power. For instance, if you fancy beautiful women with excellent physical constitution, all it takes is a word from any of us, and the forces behind us can easily find such women for you..."

Li Qingxu chuckled as he patted Feng Yuqiu on the shoulder.

Feng Yuqiu nodded eagerly.

In the days that followed, he stayed at the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao, getting to know its many members. Through the influence of their respective backgrounds, he secured various concubines for himself and began rapidly increasing his strength.

Outside, the events surrounding the Tuo Lin Universe had barely caused a ripple—if even that—across the Vast Expanse of the cosmos.

After months of searching, there was still no trace of Feng Yuqiu, the holder of the (cbda) Heavenly Dao Fragment. Civilizations from various True Realms and the rulers of the Super Multiverse ceased to focus on this matter and began searching for the whereabouts of the remaining Heavenly Dao Fragments.

……

"The Alliance of Heavenly Daoists?"

"That’s somewhat interesting, though I wonder how far they’ll ultimately grow."

“Is there any hope that they might pose even the slightest threat to me?”

Gu Changge watched with keen interest.

Standing atop an endless, boundless height, he observed the ebb and flow of the Port of Ling’s cosmic energy.

Apart from the Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth, no third Pathfinder had yet emerged. He had originally believed that, given the vast accumulation over the centuries, a third Pathfinder would appear, but it seemed he had overestimated the sentient beings of the Vast Expanse.

However, the emergence of the Alliance of Heavenly Daoists did come as something of a surprise to him.

Where his gaze fell, he saw the Vast Expanse of billowing qi colliding with a mysterious mass of qi emerging from the depths of the void.

That mass of cosmic energy was immense, surging at times and expanding at others. From within it, an aura distinct from the old and new cosmic orders periodically emanated, as if originating from another unknown and independent realm of time and space.

This unknown, mysterious mass of energy originated from the Ren Qing Daoist Alliance.

After the Heavenly Will disintegrated and shattered, this mysterious, unknown mass of cosmic energy suddenly broke away from the Vast Expanse of cosmic energy and began to expand at a terrifyingly rapid pace.

Like a constantly growing giant crocodile, it devoured countless qi formations of all sizes along the way, fusing them into a single entity.

Almost all of the energies it devoured were imbued with the same aura of fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

Thus, the Alliance of Heavenly Dao Walkers came into being.

As for what forces drove this alliance and how it operated, Gu Changge wasn’t really interested; he didn’t want to waste his spiritual energy investigating it.

For him, the emergence of such a colossal mass of Qi was a good thing; it proved that his method was viable and effective.

If he were to control and manage every single detail, that would deviate from his original intent. For the future trajectory he sought was one of disorder and chaos—one that would leap and transform toward higher dimensions and a grander scale.

All of this aligns with his philosophy.

"Huh, another massive energy mass has appeared?"

Gu Changge raised an eyebrow slightly.

Following his gaze, he saw that amidst the Vast Expanse of the primordial energy, a bubble-like mass suddenly rose up, rapidly expanding until it reached a hundred times its original size in an instant.

This added yet another colossal entity to the Vast Expanse.

In addition, there were energy formations representing the Five Heavens—the Plague Heaven, the Decay Heaven, the Rot Heaven, the Chaos Heaven, and the Lust Heaven—all of which were immense and vastly different from the other living beings.

Even the Qi possessed by the Lords of Power and the Ancestor of the Cosmos could not compare to them.

These streams of energy represent the orderly trajectory of the future and, to a certain extent, symbolize potential.

The current structure of the myriad realms within the Vast Expanse can almost entirely be delineated and inferred through the fluctuations of these energies.

In any case, the Five Heavens were created by Gu Changge by extracting their origins from the complete Heavenly Will of the Heavenly Dao. When it comes to potential, it naturally lies with the Lords of Power and their kind.

"It’s quite unexpected that the third to become a Pathfinder would be this fellow."

"But even so, it’s still a bit lacking..."

At this moment, the cluster of Qi that Gu Changge was observing was undergoing internal restructuring, as if undergoing billions of cycles of chaotic birth and destruction, reincarnation and renewal, giving birth to new life and continuing the path ahead.

At the edge of a vast, boundless, and unfathomable chasm, far from his infinite spacetime plane.

The figure of the Three-Headed Sovereign sat cross-legged. His hands were folded, and a fragment of the Heavenly Dao floated before his chest. Threads of true fire burned, carrying an inexplicable aura that enveloped him.

Around him, billions of dazzling, crystalline golden dragons swirled. Each golden dragon represented a solid Dao; these Daos had now materialized, completely merging into the Three-Headed Master’s body, as if to reconstruct an entirely new, celestial Dao for him.

BOOM!!!

As if power from the highest realms were pouring down, the Three-Headed Master’s first head exploded with a bang, followed by the second, the third…

Then his body itself exploded completely, transforming into dazzling streams of light, until nothing remained.

Only that fragment of the Heavenly Dao remained in place, floating in the air, as crystalline and mysterious as ever.

The area soon fell into a deathly silence and darkness. In the distance, the vast, boundless chasm seemed capable of burying countless lives, unchanged since time immemorial.

This was the edge of the Great Abyss; even beings of the End of the Path tier dared not set foot here lightly, for they would easily be claimed by all manner of unknown dangers.

"I refuse to accept this..."

Upon the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, a wisp of conscious light flickered faintly, constantly pulsing. It slowly gathered the streaks of light that had just dissipated, gradually reshaping the head of the Three-Headed Lord.

He, who once possessed three heads, now had only one remaining; the other two could not be restored.

"Why did I fail at the most critical moment? Everything else went perfectly—I was just a hair’s breadth away."

"If only I had one more chance, I would surely succeed."

He refused to accept it—the path ahead was right before his eyes.

Now that even his primordial essence was incomplete, the hope of advancing further was all but gone; indeed, merely preserving a sliver of his consciousness was already a struggle.

BOOM!!!

Just as the Three-Headed Lord was consumed by utter frustration, a deafening roar echoed through the air. He watched as the dissipating golden dragons—twisting and coiling—converged from all directions, flowing back into his body once more. The surrounding spacetime seemed to flow backward, and the dissipating source of confinement began to reform.

"Alliance Leader..."

The Three-Headed Master sensed a familiar aura, as if he could see Gu Changge waving her sleeve from a distant, endless horizon. His expression instantly filled with excitement and elation, and he was moved to tears with gratitude.

"It turns out my decision and choice back then were not wrong."

"Only by joining the Heaven Slaying Alliance can one find rebirth..."

The Three-Headed Master dared not waste this hard-won opportunity. He once again focused all his mind and spirit, using the fragments of the Heavenly Dao to seal off the path before the hall.
