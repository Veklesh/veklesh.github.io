The Feng Clan elder who had charged forward in a rage had no chance to resist; he was instantly engulfed, his very soul obliterated.

Within their own clan, such a figure represented the very foundation of their heritage—one who might only emerge during the Gray Clan’s time.

Mo Tong’s cold gaze swept over the others; he chose not to act, then turned his attention to another battlefield.

"We beseech the Ancestor to emerge and protect our clan."

"Everyone is coveting the Feng Clan’s most precious treasure—this is simply too much!"

"Please, Ancestor, stand up for us."

What had once been a joyous occasion was now drenched in blood. Many members of the Feng clan roared in grief and fury, kneeling on the ground, biting their tongues to draw blood, and chanting the ancient incantations of the Ancestor.

One by one, ancient characters—vibrant and dazzling, as if they had existed for dozens of eons—carried an air of profound antiquity as they surged deep into the heart of the Feng clan.

The mausoleums and temples built there instantly burst into boundless radiance, and a terrifying, surging energy began to stir and revive.

"The Wind Clan’s most ancient ancestor is about to emerge..."

Zhu Jinglong, who was accompanying the many members of the Liu clan, saw this scene and said through gritted teeth, his eyes filled with fury.

"Don’t worry. Once the Young Master’s battle concludes, this war will end. It’s just that the woman in gray will likely be a tough opponent."

said a pale-faced, long-bearded middle-aged man standing beside Zhu Jinglong.

The Feng Clan’s foundations pale in comparison to those of the ancient clans. That ancestor is likely at most at the Real Dao Realm level, having survived six Celestial Waning Tribulations but failing to advance to the Ancestral Dao Realm.

On the other hand, he suspected that the suddenly appearing man with the ink-black pupils was no ordinary figure. Though he didn’t appear very old, he had already attained the Dao Realm, and his background was likely no small matter.

“However, no one can stop the Liu Clan from taking the Heavenly Dao Fragment today.”

As the words fell, the middle-aged man began chanting incantations, and at the same time, a brilliant, crystalline light burst forth from the center of his brow.

The one hundred and eight great banners originally summoned from the eight directions of heaven and earth instantly erupted with boundless black light, tearing through the fabric of space and time. They continuously intertwined, eventually opening up a realm resembling a prison.

Zhu Jinglong trembled.

Mo Tong couldn’t help but frown, sensing a hint of danger.

"Our clan’s ancestors once subdued a savage beast of the primordial wilderness. This beast has devoured multiple universes; its ferocity is beyond measure. Ordinary Real Dao Realm cultivators are no match for it..."

The middle-aged man said coldly. Having come here, they must have made thorough preparations.

"Open the prison."

He pointed with both hands, and the crystal-clear drop of blood that had flown from his brow instantly vanished into the vast sky. There, a pitch-black cross-shaped fissure split open, as if tearing the entire universe apart, and an aura of boundless ferocity and malice surged forth.

Billions of living beings throughout Wuji City trembled in terror, unable to resist the urge to prostrate themselves on the ground, unable to bear the weight of this aura.

In the next instant, the cross-shaped fissure tore open, and a bloodlight so vast it seemed to engulf the heavens poured forth.

A terrifying creature, covered in black fur and resembling a black bear, burst forth from within. Its massive form filled the sky; its crimson eyes alone shone like a blood-red sun, causing every living being it gazed upon to tremble.

There was no doubt about it: this was an existence of boundless power. At the peak of its might, even a being from a major universe would not be enough for it to devour.

Now, as this being broke free, it let out a deafening roar and charged deep into the Wind Clan’s territory.

Endless gale-force winds tore through the air; space and time shattered like bubbles, unable to withstand the shockwaves.

"What kind of vile beast dares to act so brazenly?"

In the deepest tombs of the Wind Clan, countless structures crumbled. An ancient coffin burst open, and a figure emerged from within.

The moment the Wind Clan’s ancestor awoke, he learned what had transpired from the prayers of his people.

He appeared as a gaunt, withered old man, yet his entire body now erupted with chaotic light. He extended a massive hand and struck out horizontally. The entire sky over Wuji City turned black, completely obscured; the sun and moon dimmed, their light utterly swallowed up.

BOOM!!!

But in the next instant, accompanied by a agonized scream, blood spattered.

The terrifying creature, resembling a black bear, opened its massive maw—like a black hole, filled with sharp fangs—and lunged directly at the Wind Clan Ancestor’s hand. A vast, pitch-black mass of flesh plummeted toward the vast cosmos, and blood spattered in all directions like a cascading starry waterfall.

The entire world was bathed in crimson, like a torrential downpour, an utterly terrifying sight.

Yet in that very first clash, the Wind Clan ancestor suffered a severe setback—half his arm had been torn to shreds.

"You're asking for death..."

Enraged, he roared, his figure instantly soaring to a height that seemed to crush the heavens and earth. At the site of his severed arm, time itself appeared to fracture; all the blood surged back, as if bridging the gap between past and present.

After countless years of seclusion, upon waking for the first time, he faced such a situation. Could Ling Fengjia’s Wu Ou Ru’a possibly refuse?

He revealed his true form, piercing through Wuji City and sinking into the entire star domain. Before him, the so-called stars were as insignificant as dust.

This boundless form and the overwhelming aura of the Dao caused countless living beings to tremble in fear.

The formation patterns originally arranged around Wuji City shattered in an instant. The many beings who had already intended to flee did so without hesitation, scattering like birds and beasts toward the distant starry sky, not daring to linger for a moment.

"That old man has been in seclusion all this time; he’s actually preparing to endure the Seventh Celestial Waning Tribulation. Once he survives it, he’ll reach the Ancestral Dao Realm."

"I never expected him to break through the grand formation..."

The expression of the middle-aged man standing beside Zhu Jinglong shifted, revealing surprise and shock.

According to intelligence gathered by the Liu family, the Feng family’s ancestor had not yet survived the sixth Celestial Waning Tribulation—they never imagined the information was false.

Zhu Jinglong seethed with anger. He himself had only survived three Celestial Waning Tribulations and was simply no match for a battle of this magnitude.

In the past, the disparity in strength between the Feng and Zhu clans had not been significant. In his view, the root cause of all these changes lay in that fragment of the Heavenly Dao.

With the emergence of the Heavenly Dao Fragment, the entire Vast Expanse plunged into a frenzy of fierce competition and slaughter. It was as if boiling magma had been poured into a pond, causing all living beings to be unable to remain still, constantly leaping out of the water.

This has also caused the development of the Vast Expanse to accelerate at an unprecedented pace.

In a single great universe, it used to be no small feat for a single being to reach the Dao Realm within an entire eon. But now, with the descent of the Heavenly Dao Fragment, even an ordinary person could attain the Dao Realm within a hundred years.

Among the fragments of the Heavenly Dao that Zhu Jinglong knew of, there were even those possessing such mystical and wondrous properties that they could allow cultivators to attain cultivation levels out of thin air.

In the chaotic realm beyond the world, the ancestors of the Feng clan engaged in a fierce battle with that terrifying being; the scale of this clash was even more terrifying than the battle between Liu Hongyun and Feng Yuqiu.

In an instant, the heavens and earth were thrown into turmoil; the sky turned dark, and the sun, moon, and stars shone simultaneously, constantly shifting. Wailing ghosts and howling gods and demons filled the air, while a terrifying gale swept across the land, even causing the Sea of Time and Space to wither over a vast expanse.

Ordinary True Path-level beings could never generate such fluctuations; this was nearly on par with the might of the Ancestral Dao Realm.

"It won’t be so easy to vie for the fragments of the Heavenly Dao."

Witnessing this scene, Mo Tong could only frown. Watching from the shadows, she had originally planned to strike suddenly and seize the fragment while Liu Hongyun and Feng Yuqiu were engaged in battle, but now it seemed the difficulty was far greater than she had anticipated.

No living being could resist the temptation of a Heavenly Dao Fragment.

Although the Ancient Liu Clan has sealed off the news for now, the other supreme beings in this universe will likely soon catch wind of it and rush over.

The later it gets, the harder it will be to secure a fragment of the Heavenly Dao.

In fact, eventually, even beings at the End of the Path level might make an appearance.

"The fragments of the Heavenly Dao are not so easily obtained..."

"But with this banner in my possession, there must still be other opportunities."

Mo Tong pondered this, already considering retreat.

Just as she was about to slip away quietly,

The young mistress celebrating her wedding today—the bride in her phoenix crown and wedding robes—ran up to her with eyes full of longing and hope, then dropped to her knees with a thud.

"Please, Senior, take me with you."

Tears streamed down the woman’s face, making her look pitiful and pitiable.

Mo Tong frowned. She had merely saved the girl out of the goodness of her heart; there was no connection between them.

"Please, Senior, take me away from here. I’ll serve you as a cow or a horse—I’ll have no complaints..."

The emergence of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao was clearly a disgrace brought upon the Jingyu Alliance by Mao Gu Changge.

Perhaps this struggle over the fragments of the Heavenly Dao might be a trial of some sort for him.

BOOM!!!

A cold, indifferent voice rang out, piercing the heavens. Whether it was the Feng clan ancestor and the terrifying colossus locked in combat, or Feng Yuqiu and the Liu clan’s young master, Liu Hongyun, on another battlefield—all were swept up in its wake, about to be crushed beneath its weight.
