"Cultivating by absorbing the essence of creation and the essence of immortality?"

Yue Mingkong had now reached the End of the Path cultivation level. After all, she had inherited the Dream Lord’s Dao Fruit. Although she was still some distance from the Supreme Shattering Realm, with her natural talent and Gu Changge’s assistance, advancing further would pose no problem.

She carefully pored over this profound cultivation manual. It contained the concepts of Great Freedom, Great Transcendence, Great Mystery, Great Supremacy, and Great Highest—representing immortality and the ultimate—and was far beyond the comprehension of ordinary people.

Even if one were to have the Lord of Power and others peruse it, they would immediately become bewildered and eventually go mad.

However, thanks to Gu Changge’s guidance in helping her make sense of it, she gradually came to understand it, though the process was arduous.

In a sense, the substances of Creation and Immortality were far beyond what she could comprehend or even touch at her current stage.

Even after transcending the Eternal Heaven Realm, one would still be unable to fully comprehend or master them.

"I once spread the 'Calling Sutra' across the Vast Expanse. This cultivation method can really only be considered a prototype, an experiment. Various ethereal forces—such as the power of faith, the power of destiny, and the power of the mind—can actually be condensed through certain methods, though sentient beings are currently unable to access or master them."

"And this cultivation method, which utilizes Immortal Substance and Creative Substance, is actually a technique that transcends the Sutra of Summoning. Once a cultivator reaches a certain level, they can naturally condense these two substances on their own; absorbing and cultivating them is merely an instinct."

"For now, I am simply allowing you to master this instinct ahead of time by presenting it in the form of a cultivation technique," Gu Changge explained.

"What is the name of this cultivation method?" Yue Mingkong was both curious and astonished; he could scarcely imagine a cultivation method that utilized immortal substances and primordial substances.

Compared to the common practice of competing for fragments of the Heavenly Dao to advance along the Third Path, this method was simply on a whole different level.

Of course, she could understand why. After all, the quantities of Creation Substance and Immortal Substance were limited; it was impossible to disseminate this cultivation method so that everyone could practice it.

The fact that Gu Changge was willing to pass it on to her spoke volumes about his selflessness.

"It has no name, but if one must give it one, perhaps it could be called 'Endless,'" Gu Changge said after a moment’s thought.

“The Endless Technique?”

Yue Mingkong nodded.

“The current supply of Immortal Substances and Creative Substances is insufficient for all sentient beings to cultivate, so I am imparting this cultivation method solely to you. As for the Vast Expanse of all worlds, they serve merely as the fertile soil that nurtures these substances.”

Gu Changge’s gaze swept across the various realms of time and space. As the struggle for fragments of the Heavenly Dao intensified and the various Children of Destiny and protagonists of the Epochs were born, the power of destiny—or the power of faith—would only grow ever more dense and potent. The Creative Substance and Immortal Substance he had refined using the Cauldron of the World would also increase in abundance.

Of course, he hoped even more that beings capable of transcending the Eternal Heaven Realm would emerge sooner rather than later.

Only after reaching the Eternal Heaven Realm could cultivators, in a certain sense, break free from the nurturing ground of the Vast Expanse, achieve self-sufficiency, and then, in turn, nourish the Land of Wang Yan.

Only then would the combined power of the Vast Expanse rise to new heights and truly be of assistance to him.

"I understand. This cultivation technique is of the utmost importance; it must not be disclosed or publicized."

"Not even to my most trusted subordinates. It seems I’ll have to conceal my cultivation level from now on and not reveal it lightly." Yue Mingkong quickly grasped the implications.

Gu Changge smiled, wrapped his arm around her slender waist, and asked, “Do you think I’m too cold-hearted?”

"No, to me, you’ve never been cold-hearted." Yue Mingkong leaned gently against his chest, her thoughts drifting to the various aspects of the cultivation technique.

In the days that followed, the competition across the Vast Expanse grew increasingly fierce and brutal. As traces of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao began to surface, a series of earth-shattering battles erupted.

Many powerful figures from the Heaven Slaying Alliance and the Palace of Defiance were also drawn into the fray.

The Lord of the Bureau had originally planned to journey into the Great Abyss to comprehend the true essence of the former Heavenly Dao and thereby advance on his path, but now he too was forced to vie for the fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

The Lord of Restraint, Gu Xihan—the First Ancestor of the Gu Clan—the Forgetful Immortal Elder, and the Three-Headed Lord also emerged one after another.

With the threat and pressure from the Land Of Truth temporarily lifted, the various True Realms and Super-Great Universes—which had previously formed a united front—were now forced to clash over the fragments of the Dao.

The Nightmare Demon Realm was no exception.

Time flew by, and a hundred years passed in the blink of an eye.

After Yue Mingkong went into seclusion, Gu Changge met with Ling Yuling once, entrusted her with the many matters concerning the future of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, and then departed from the alliance.

Ling Yuling had regained her former cultivation level. With Gu Changge refining her fundamental essence and purifying her with the Substance of Creation and the Substance of Immortality, she began preparing to break through to the Supreme Shattering Realm.

During this time, Gu Changge observed the changes unfolding across the Vast Expanse of all realms.

The Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth had journeyed to that battlefield beyond the boundaries of space and time to undergo trials; as of now, no third Pioneer had yet emerged.

As for the inconceivable universe that transcends the super-multiverse, it has not yet come into being.

This requires a catalyst; for an Inconceivable Universe to form, it must have at least one Heavenly Realm being—that is, a Pioneer—to hold the fort.

For an ordinary super-multiverse to ascend to an Unthinkable Universe is virtually impossible.

Another hundred years passed.

Prosperous Dao True Realm, Qing Shan Village.

On a patch of fertile land, the air was refreshingly crisp with the scent of rain.

Gu Changge, dressed in coarse cloth, was using a sickle to harvest the lush green chives. Beside him, on the newly cultivated land, Su Qingge was helping him sow new chive seeds and then watering them, her face beaming with a happy smile.

"You’ve always loved growing chives. Year after year, you harvest them without ever tiring..." Su Qingge brought him a cup of sweet mountain spring water.

"Only the diligent can truly appreciate the joy and fulfillment of the harvest. Do you understand that now?"

Gu Changge shook his head gently, a smile playing on his lips, and drained the bowl of spring water in one gulp.

He looked at the lush green patches of chive fields before him and felt a sense of satisfaction. Perhaps it was an innate fondness that made him feel a special affection for chives.

At this stage of his life, there were very few things that brought him joy.

Only when harvesting chives could he truly feel that original emotion from the beginning.

“Are you really no longer considering the path of cultivation?”

After finishing his work, Gu Changge sat lazily in a wicker chair outside the Bamboo Pavilion, the aroma of tea wafting nearby.

Su Qingge came to his side, picked up the robe she hadn’t finished embroidering, and said softly, “I’m already very content now. Every year when the chives grow lush and green, you come to see me…”

Hearing this, Gu Changge said nothing. He reached out and took her wrist, altering the essence of her life. An immortal fruit—invisible to Su Qingge—coalesced within it, glowing brilliantly.

Su Qingge knew what Gu Changge was doing; she smiled slightly.

Immortality and eternal life were no longer what she sought; peace of mind was enough. Wherever he was, that was her true home.

Still, she did not refuse Gu Changge’s kindness.

With the fruit of immortality within her, the world may change beyond recognition, yet she remains unchanged; thunder and lightning may fade, yet she remains immortal.

After leaving the Prosperous Dao True Realm, Gu Changge once again observed the changes across the Vast Expanse as he always did. This time, however, his observation led to an unexpected discovery.

Gu Changge looked on with keen interest and immediately spotted a figure he hadn’t seen in a long time within a vast universe.

“Was the news of a fragment of the Heavenly Dao here something you deliberately leaked?”

A majestic middle-aged man, his form surging like raging waves, stood tall beneath the starry sky. His Dharma Body was boundless in size, radiating the aura of the Real Dao Realm.

“It was me.”

A figure with gray hair and eyes, and a complexion as pale as ice and snow, stood silently before him, radiating a chilling aura of murderous intent. Serene ripples spread out like a spider’s web, sealing off this vast universe completely.

Compared to the first time Gu Changge had seen her in the duel arena, she had matured—tall and aloof, as if carved from ice and snow, radiating an aura that warned all to keep their distance.

"You're asking for death."

“Since you don’t possess a fragment of the Heavenly Dao, how dare you lure me here?”

The middle-aged man at the Real Dao Realm was furious. This gray-robed woman, whose aura was merely at the Void Dao Realm, dared to provoke him so brazenly and had even deliberately spread rumors about the fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

Unsurprisingly, a fierce battle erupted between them almost immediately.

After all, this was a clash between cultivators of the Dao Realm; the energy fluctuations were intense, swiftly tearing through this vast universe and spilling into the outer realms, where stars shattered and chaotic energy surged.

Although the commotion and energy fluctuations drew many powerful beings to the scene, none dared to intervene.

After a grueling battle, the woman in gray, relying on a slight advantage, triumphed against a stronger opponent and slew the middle-aged man. Holding a Banner of a Hundred Million Souls from which a chilling wind emanated, she stood there proudly, her body drenched in blood, with the Source of Slaughter faintly visible.

Finally, she sat cross-legged amidst the cosmos and began to refine the primordial essence of that Real Dao Realm being using the Hundred-Million-Soul Banner in her hand. The source of slaughter behind her grew increasingly dense and pure.

Throughout this process, not a single living being dared to approach and disturb her; the killing intent radiating from this woman in gray was simply too terrifying, enough to strike fear into anyone’s heart.

As she refined the essence, her own Path of Slaughter also solidified completely, showing faint signs of a breakthrough.

Once she weathered the next Celestial Waning Tribulation, she would be able to firmly establish herself at her current level.

Suddenly, Gu Changge’s figure emerged from the void.

The woman in gray braced herself as if facing a formidable enemy, her body tense and her killing intent radiating outward. But the moment she recognized Gu Changge’s face, she froze, utterly at a loss.

"Leader... Leader..."

The woman in gray was none other than Mo Tong.

The young girl in gray who had been taken away by Gu Changge in the arena long ago had, with the passage of time, grown into this form.

“Is this the Way of Slaughter passed down to you by the White Bone Ancestral King?”

Gu Changge stepped beside her and waved his hand casually. All of Mo Tong’s injuries healed instantly; even the traces on her primordial essence began to mend. Her life force surged with renewed vigor, and her aura soared to new heights.

"This is what my Master taught me before he passed away. I intend to carry on his legacy." Mo Tong’s eyes darkened slightly as she clenched her fists and said, "I want to bring my Master back to life."

During the great war against the Ancient Hidden Civilization, many high-ranking figures—including the Skeleton Ancestral King—had perished. Afterward, the Lord of Power and others had searched for their divine souls, but found no trace of them.

Existences such as Emperor Huang, Hun Yuanjun, and the Fire God Zhu Rong had also perished.

Only Chu Heng had a single thread of his soul remaining; through gradual nurturing, he eventually recovered.

Many leaders and high-ranking members of the other clans had also perished.

Gu Changge said nothing, merely gazing at Mo Tong, then at the Banner of a Hundred Million Souls in her hand. This object carried a faint trace of the Heavenly Dao’s essence; it seemed Mo Tong had encountered quite a few fortuitous opportunities over the years.

"I’ve heard that once one becomes a Pathfinder, ascends to the Heavenly Realm, and enters the Eternal Heaven Realm, one gains the power to create things out of nothing and bring the dead back to life—even if the very essence of life and death has been extinguished, one can still revive them."

“Although my Master was strict with me, he was the kindest person to me in this world. I want to bring him back to life.” Mo Tong’s eyes dimmed slightly.

She recalled the many experiences she’d had while training under the White Bone Ancestral King. Though harsh and arduous, they were unforgettable.

Originally, she had been a death-row prisoner awaiting execution in the arena.

Had it not been for Gu Changge, she would have long since perished in the fray against her opponents.

"To revive the Bone Ancestor King, your current cultivation is still too weak." Gu Changge shook his head.

“But I will strive for it,” Mo Tong declared resolutely.

Gu Changge suddenly smiled and said, “In that case, I’ll lend you a hand.”

“Thank you, Alliance Leader.”

Mo Tong looked up at him abruptly, his eyes sparkling with light.

Gu Changge reached out and grabbed the Handkerchief of a Hundred Million Souls, which Mo Tong held in his hand; it instantly flew toward him, beyond his control.

As Gu Changge casually traced a line through the vast void, a dazzling radiance gathered from all directions. Within it flowed a viscous substance that surged madly into the Hundred-Million-Soul Banner. Earth, water, wind, and fire roared in unison; chaotic mist overflowed, erupting with a terrifying commotion, as if it were forging the ancient, forging the heavens, and forging all living things.

"Sacrifice."

Finally, Gu Changge gave a light wave of his hand, and the Banner of a Hundred Million Souls became plain and unadorned, returning once more to Mo Tong’s grasp.

"This..." Mo Tong sensed an inexplicable change in the Banner of a Hundred Million Souls, but she could not pinpoint exactly what had changed.

"I have re-forged this banner. It can now sense the presence of fragments of the Heavenly Dao. If you wish to grow, you must inevitably engage in fierce battles with the many Children of Destiny and Children of the Epoch. This banner will grow alongside you, and one day, it may reach the level you desire," Gu Changge said.

Mo Tong clutched the Banner of a Hundred Million Souls tightly and nodded solemnly.

"Kill. The Way of Slaughter is meant to be this way."

A faint smile played on Gu Changge’s lips as ripples appeared before him. He then took a step forward and vanished.

All living beings have their own destinies, and Gu Changge did not wish to interfere unduly. For now, he merely intended to serve as an observer, watching the unfolding of these events from the highest vantage point.

"I will not disappoint the Alliance Leader."

After Gu Changge departed, Mo Tong took a deep breath to regain his composure. He then focused his mind on sensing the changes in the Hundred Million Souls Banner, quickly grasping the many profound subtleties within it—particularly his understanding of the Way of Slaughter, which had deepened significantly.

"Hmm, the Hundred Million Souls Banner has undergone a subtle change. Could it be that fragments of the Heavenly Dao truly exist near this great universe?"

Several days later, just as Mo Tong was about to leave.

She suddenly froze, furrowed her brow, and sensed the area carefully for a moment, confirming that her perception was not mistaken.

Originally, she had merely spread the rumor of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao to lure cultivators here, intending to hunt them down using the grand formation she had previously set up. She never expected that there would actually be a lead on the fragments of the Heavenly Dao.
