In the Land Of Truth, aside from the elders of the Council of Truth, the great Heavenly Seal Clans are the most powerful forces—without exception. This has been the case for countless ages, like an immutable law since time immemorial.

Therefore, the four of them had never imagined that one day, aside from the Council of Truth, anyone else could strip them of their control over the Heavenly Seal Symbols.

"I should have realized this sooner..."

"The fact that you’re here means you’ve successfully broken free from the entanglements of the Heavenly Will—and perhaps even triumphed over it."

"Such an existence is far beyond what we could ever hope to oppose or even imagine."

"Even if the senior elders of the Council of Truth were to come, they would meet the same fate."

"Who exactly are you?"

Jueyin Lou spoke with bitter resignation. Without the Heavenly Seal, he was nothing more than a being who had repeatedly broken through the limits of the End of the Path realm—at best, comparable to an existence at the level of the Supreme Shattering.

It was as if he had fallen from the Nine Heavens into the dust.

Yan Huojun, Chong Mingxue, and the others were equally grim and despairing; they had long since lost the will to resist. Of course, even if they had the will to resist, it would not have made the slightest difference.

The outcome would not change in the slightest.

The powerful figures from the other ancient clans—such as the Moon Demon Clan and the Ashes Clan—trembled uncontrollably, watching the scene with utter dread.

If not for seeing it with their own eyes, who could possibly believe it?

Even the Heavenly Seal, bestowed by the three True Ancestors, was stripped away.

What a colossal and inconceivable feat—beyond the limits of their comprehension.

Gu Changge paid no heed to these despairing figures. He clasped his hands together, and the primordial substance flowing through his palms and fingers instantly vanished.

He glanced at the white-robed woman, who was enveloped in a dazzling radiance and whose expression was equally filled with shock and awe. From her, he sensed the shared bloodline of the three clans of the Lai Zhi, Wang Ji, and Chan.

With just a fleeting sense of her, Gu Changge knew her origins.

Without a word, he flicked his sleeve. Before the woman in white could even utter a sound, she was enveloped by a transparent bubble marked “310,” instantly shrinking in size as he tucked her into his sleeve.

The mystical bead that had just repelled the attacks of the Absolute Yin Tower’s men still rippled, radiating a transcendent power as it attempted to resist, but to no avail; the ripples dissipated the moment they began to spread.

Suddenly, Gu Changge realized something and raised his hand to summon it.

The mysterious bead immediately flew out and landed in the palm of his hand. At the same time, a stream of thoughts from the same source surged into his consciousness like a torrent.

A moment later, Gu Changge’s gaze grew deep as he tucked the mysterious bead away.

"There wasn't much of a reaction. It seems my initial guess was wrong."

Gu Changge pondered briefly.

There was no doubt that this “foreign object,” the Transcendent Pearl, originated from the True Ancestor’s Temple, and the mental signal it contained also stemmed from the “True.”

Otherwise, the woman in white—who was none other than Ji Xuanyi—would never have been able to escape from the True Ancestor’s Temple.

It was no easy feat.

It was as if even a dragon’s casual roll could stir up colossal waves across the vast ocean.

Gu Changge had always believed that among the three True Ancestors, aside from himself, both “Xuan” and “Shen” had, due to their respective philosophies, left their mark in the Vast Expanse of the ages.

Whether it was the blueprints for the treasures of Civilization revealed earlier or the Transcendence Compendium, these all emerged only in this era of the Chaotic Heavens and Earth.

In the chaotic cosmic eras of the past, the vast waters of the world remained calm.

Even those beings that had survived from even older Chaos Realm eras kept a low profile, scraping by in silence, afraid to make the slightest sound, lest they be detected during the final reckoning of a particular Chaos Realm era.

Take, for example, the legacy of the Jutian Sect that Gu Xian’er is currently receiving—as Gu Changge knows—which clearly belongs to the previous era of the chaotic cosmos, yet it has now begun to manifest.

The so-called "old cosmic order" and "new cosmic order" have both existed in past Chaos Eons; this is not a theory that emerged only in this current Chaos Eon.

In the depths of the unknown, a mighty hand is deliberately concealing something, following a certain trajectory to ensure everything unfolds exactly as it wishes.

It was precisely for this reason that when Gu Changge first arrived in the Vast Expanse from the Prosperous Dao Realm and attempted to establish the Heaven Slaying Alliance, the Will of Heaven struck a blow of its own, clearing away some obstacles in his path.

At the time, Gu Changge believed this was likely a trap set by the other two of the Three True Ancestors.

Now, however, Gu Changge has a general understanding of what is happening.

"If you were to kill them outright, it could lead to major complications down the line. The most important thing right now is to buy as much time as possible for the Vast Expanse, rather than engaging in a decisive battle with the Land Of Truth."

Suddenly, a voice rang out behind Gu Changge.

The vast mist expanded, coalescing into a deep vortex. From within it, a slender, smiling figure emerged, walking slowly toward him, completely unaffected by the fragment of the Annihilation Realm.

Gu Changge’s expression remained calm, showing little emotion, as if he wasn’t the least bit surprised.

"Whether I kill them or not makes no difference to me."

Gu Changge said casually, her words betraying no concern for the lives of Jueyin Tower and the others.

Jueyin Lou and the others, however, stared at the woman who had suddenly appeared before them with trembling shock.

They could not fathom the fluctuations of the aura emanating from this woman, nor did they know her origins—how she had managed to reach this Passage C, which connected the Vast Waters to the Real World.

The depths of the Vast Expanse were far greater than they had imagined; aside from the Heaven Slaying Alliance Master, there existed such a mysterious being.

“In that case, I’ll spare their lives for now. I have an idea.”

The woman who had stepped forward laughed with a voice as clear as a silver bell. Her hair was styled in a cloud-like bun, her figure graceful and slender, her skin as fair as ice and her bones as delicate as snow. She wore embroidered snow-white robes and cloud-patterned shoes, her gait light and elegant.

Gu Changge stared at this woman, who looked almost exactly like Wang Zijing—even their life essence and vital energy were indistinguishable.

Under his scrutiny, even the slightest detail was indistinguishable.

One could say the two were one and the same.

Yet he knew clearly that the woman before him was not the Wang Zijing he knew. Perhaps they shared similar, shared memories, but their personalities and life experiences would be entirely different.

“What’s your plan?” Gu Changge asked.

“Since the path ahead in the Vast Expanse has already been restored, why not spare their lives and transform this place into a battlefield beyond the boundaries—perhaps we could call it the Field of Oblivion—to temper those who journey along the path in the Vast Expanse.”

The woman offered her suggestion with a serious expression.

“That’s not a bad idea,” Gu Changge replied casually, nodding.

The woman smiled, her expression bright and captivating. “You don’t seem the least bit curious about my origins, nor do you find it strange at all that I’m here. Your reaction is all too natural. I had prepared an explanation, but now I don’t even know how to begin.”

"It seems there really is nothing in the Vast Expanse that can be hidden from you."

Gu Changge had long been aware of the relationship between Wang Zijing and the Western Saint—that is, the master of the Holy Maiden Xi.

Ever since he met the Piao Miao Holy Ancestor in the Piao Miao Holy City, he had pieced together many clues from the causal threads connecting the two.

Of course, Wang Zijing had also sought him out and mentioned certain things, such as the strange, eerie dreams she had been having.

Unlike Gu Changge’s artificial “time traveler” status, in his view, Wang Zijing’s crossing of time was, on the contrary, a truly uninterfered-with “anomaly.”

He wasn’t surprised that Wang Zijing had come here to find him; of course, for now, he shouldn’t address her as Wang Zijing, but rather as the Western Saint.

In this chaotic era of the cosmos, the blueprints for the primordial Civilization’s treasure—used to seal and restrain the Lord of Heaven—had been passed down from her very hands.

Furthermore, the other leaders who fought against the Black Calamity—such as the Eastern Sovereign, the Central Lord, the Southern Venerable, and the Northern Emperor—had all united under her guidance.

She was the most enigmatic figure in the vast expanse before the Black Calamity.

"Forgive me for only coming to find you now to confess all this. After all, I dared not take the risk—the Vast Expanse is far deeper than I ever imagined. But I don’t think you’ll mind. After all, in a certain sense, I am your woman too. Surely you wouldn’t make things difficult for your own woman?"

Western Saint smiled as she looked at Gu Changge, completely unconcerned by the presence of the Absolute Yin Tower and other beings nearby, speaking as if to herself.

Judging by this slightly frivolous demeanor, she bore a certain resemblance to Wang Zijing.

"You are you, and Wang Zijing is Wang Zijing. I certainly wouldn’t put her in a difficult position, but you might." Gu Changge said indifferently.

Western Saint remained all smiles as he said, “What a cold-hearted person. I am her, and she is me. I possess her experiences and memories. The years she spent by your side—wasn’t it actually me accompanying you all along…?”

However, before she could finish her sentence, the smile on her face froze.

A slender, pale hand clamped around her neck. With a slight squeeze, the very source of her life seemed on the verge of shattering.

Western Saint raised her eyes, clear and luminous as a still lake, only to meet Gu Changge’s calm, unruffled gaze. She saw not a trace of warmth or affection in them, only indifference.

"Though your true form is not here, believe me—with a single thought of mine, no matter where you hide, you will die." Gu Changge spoke with the same detached tone.

The Western Saint sighed, his hand still resting on Yi Yi’s neck.

“Enough with the jokes. You must know that the Vast Expanse easily attracts the attention of the Land Of Truth, so often, we dare not make too much of a commotion. Though the Heavenly Will is no longer at its former peak, its power is still beyond the reach of a mere nobody like me. Until your entanglement with the Heavenly Will has drawn to a close, I dare not show myself lightly.”

"I imagine, given your nature, you must have guessed my existence long ago. Ever since before the Heaven Slaying Alliance was even established, I’ve been waiting for you."

Her tone gradually grew serious.

Gu Changge opened his palm and said, “What is your intention in seeking me out now? Do you wish to join forces with me to oppose the Land Of Truth?”

As he spoke, the corners of his mouth curled into a smile that was impossible to discern—whether it was indifference or mockery.

The Western Saint shook her head. “I don’t mean to dampen your spirits, but the existence of the Land Of Truth is not something the living beings of today can shake. Only in the chaos of the future might a sliver of possibility be forged. That possibility does not exist now; it exists only in the vast, endless future.”

“You are strong—so strong that I cannot fathom you, cannot fathom your depths. But you are certainly no match for the Land Of Truth, let alone a force capable of standing against the Three True Ancestors…”

Gu Changge, however, did not refute her words.

Just how powerful the three True Ancestors truly were was something only he himself knew; all other beings could only speculate and imagine.

"I have come to seek you out, solely to ensure the stability of the existing orderly layers of the cosmos."

"In a certain sense, the fate of the Vast Expanse is inextricably linked to the existence of our clan. If the Vast Expanse were to fall into eternal silence and turn to ash, all the predetermined, ordered futures would collapse and shatter, and our clan would vanish completely, ceasing to exist." The Western Saint explained.

Gu Changge gazed at her with a profound look, saying nothing more.

The Western Saint continued, “Our race was born in a world that exists in a state of neither existence nor non-existence, neither illusory nor real. We call this world the Realm of the Heart.”

“As for the birth of the Realm of the Heart, it was entirely accidental. One could say that a single thought of a sentient being is the source of creation. The birth of the power of the mind is quite mysterious and impossible to fully comprehend. To give an example, when an ordinary person constructs an imaginary world in their mind, that world actually exists in a state that is neither illusory nor real. Once certain conditions and forces are met, it can transform the illusory into the real, bringing it into being.”

"This is the premise for the birth of the Realm of the Heart, and the overarching premise for all of this is that the Vast Expanse is in the process of evolving toward an ordered future..."

"The Mind Realm is forged in the future, and all beings within it exist in a state of being or non-being, akin to a stream of information. The Mind Realm is a vast network that encompasses all beings across the Vast Expanse. As long as the power of thought is born, it can connect with the Mind Realm. When the ordered future of the Vast Expanse collapses, the thread upon which the Mind Realm was born will also be severed."

"Only if the Vast Expanse does not fall into eternal silence will the Realm of the Heart stabilize, evolving along a trajectory that is both real and illusory. Only then will that thread of the future persist. I have come from the future precisely to do my utmost to maintain this trajectory."

Having finished his explanation, the Western Saint fixed his gaze intently on Gu Changge, as if to gauge his reaction.

However, Gu Changge merely appeared deep in thought.

Clearly, the Western Saint had only provided a general explanation of the origin of the Realm of the Heart; he had not revealed much else.

As for why she had come to the Vast Expanse, or why a Prince Jin who looked exactly like her had appeared, she had offered no explanation from start to finish.

"To maintain the orderly development of the future, if we follow this logic, the creation and birth of the Heart Realm in the future actually lies within the observable trajectory."

Gu Changge glanced at the Western Saint, his gaze seeming to pierce through her very essence.

Since it is an observable future, then it is a change in the established order.

And within this, there is one crucial point that cannot be avoided… namely, the gaze of the Three True Ancestors.

Western Saint was not merely a being of the Heart Realm; she possessed another identity—one she might not even be aware of herself.

"The future Heart Realm faces a fate of depletion and collapse. To reshape the timeline, we must stabilize the past and reorder the chaos."

“I can tell you directly that in the future, a third path does exist—beings may even transcend the Heavenly Seal Realm.” The Western Saint spoke again, revealing what she considered to be a truly shocking revelation.

Of course, in the future, the Heaven Slaying Alliance is destined to fail in its conquest of Heaven. Even the existence of the Alliance Leader himself has never been recorded; he is as fleeting as a dream or a bubble, as if he had never existed at all.

There are no records of him, nor could any ever be left behind; it is as if he were erased entirely.

However, the Vast Expanse did indeed witness an era known as the “Heaven Slaying Era.” Like so many taboos of the past, this era eventually sank into boundless darkness, vanishing amidst repeated purges.

Yet this era, deemed a taboo, did indeed bring about a complete transformation to the Vast Expanse.

After the fusion of the old and new cosmic orders, a third path emerged. This third path raised the ceiling of the Vast Expanse, causing it to expand once more, becoming chaotic and infinitely vast.

It is even possible that, during a certain era, the Vast Expanse came into contact with a spacetime akin to the "Multiverse Sea" of the Founders, devouring and fusing with it to undergo yet another transformation.

It was through these various orderly transformations that the collective consciousness of sentient beings ultimately reached an unimaginable level, giving rise to the Realm of the Mind.

Following the birth of the Realm of the Mind, a cultivation method beyond common sense emerged. Though still in its embryonic form, it already possessed various extraordinary and unimaginable powers.

As long as a cultivator’s imagination was sufficiently powerful, they could endow themselves with nearly limitless might.

Even the Western Saint did not know how to explain this to the cultivators of the present age; even she did not understand the principles behind this method of cultivation. It was as if, in some future time and space, ordinary people knew that a substance called “uranium” could be used to create weapons capable of intimidating the world, yet the knowledge of how to manufacture them remained in the hands of only a select few.

This method of cultivating mental power operates on the same principle.

Such a future—prosperous and dazzling—is beyond the comprehension of the beings of this present age.

Of course, to sustain such a future, the foundation of the present is essential; one could even say that those incredible cultivation methods were born precisely because of the present.
