After the Western Saint had explained to him the existence of this ordered future, Gu Changge closed his eyes briefly; in no time at all, he realized the entity that might be connected to the Western Saint.

The orderly evolution of the future could never, from beginning to end, escape the gaze of the Three True Ancestors.

Therefore, the part of the future mentioned by the Western Saint has, in fact, always been under the watchful eyes of the Three True Ancestors.

Her arrival in the present world from the future Realm of the Heart might seem like an accident to her—perhaps a mere coincidence, or perhaps the depletion of the Realm of the Heart, causing its instinctive self-preservation to send her to the present.

Regardless of which possibility it is, none can escape the True Ancestors’ gaze.

The Western Saint is merely an observed subject; the vast, boundless, and endless shadow looming behind her is something she cannot see.

Of course, she might not even be aware of it herself; perhaps her own will has long been eroded and corrupted without her knowing it, leaving her completely oblivious.

"So, what do you want me to do?"

Gu Changge did not press further about the Western Saint’s origins, but asked casually.

"I don’t want you to do anything. I’m just saying this so you don’t go charging headlong into a hopeless battle. Now that the third path has emerged and the predetermined future is becoming clear, everything is becoming possible."

"I have a way to get the Land Of Truth to temporarily delay the reckoning of this Chaotic Heaven and Earth Era. Of course, this is on the condition that you do not disturb those three. Only then will there be a sliver of hope for the Vast Expanse to evolve to a higher plane, and for the essence of all living beings to undergo a metamorphosis."

"If you truly wish to engage in Heaven Slaying, this is your chance," said the Western Saint.

At this point, she smiled again, a strange expression on her face, and continued, “But I suspect your motives are not so simple. Is it truly for the sake of the Vast Expanse that you seek to oppose the Land Of Truth…?”

She shook her head and said no more.

Gu Changge, of course, had no reason to explain himself to her; in truth, his current objective had already been largely achieved.

He had never intended to rely on the power of the Vast Expanse to oppose the Land Of Truth.

Heaven Slaying was never his true objective.

Even if he were to harness the full power of the Vast Expanse, he could never ascend to a level comparable to that of a True Ancestor.

Therefore, for Gu Changge, the Vast Expanse was merely a fertile ground.

The three True Ancestors each held their own philosophies. In a sense, Gu Changge was merely conducting an “observation”—only the object of that observation was himself.

"This Realm of Oblivion will remain open for the next few hundred, or even a thousand years. The races and beings from the Land Of Truth will all become trials within this place."

Gu Changge cast his gaze upon figures like the Absolute Yin Tower, Chong Mingxue, and Yan Huojun. Having lost the Heavenly Seal Talisman, their strength, though surpassing the End of the Path tier, remained merely at the level of the Supreme Shattering—still no match for true Pathfinders.

Therefore, this place was indeed suitable as a trial ground.

Not to mention the other races from the Land Of Truth—such as the Moon Demon Clan and the Ashen Clan—whose beings far surpassed ordinary beings of the End of the Path in power.

With a wave of his hand, the primordial substance—previously as lustrous and crystalline as ointment—reappeared. As it flowed and spread, it transformed into layer upon layer of restrictive power, permeated with an aura surpassing the End of the Path realm, sealing off this space in concentric layers.

Furthermore, as the radiance within surged, earth, water, wind, and fire manifested simultaneously. Chaotic energy surged, and the might of the Primordial Source overflowed. These forces expanded and contracted, surging like the creation of the heavens and earth, until they ultimately coalesced into a series of radiant divine artifacts: banners, flags, towers, mirrors, sacred trees, and steles...The memories of cultivation experiences and various inherited knowledge from the Heavenly Seal Clans—including the Absolute Yin Tower—constantly flew forth, eventually merging into these divine artifacts and scattering throughout this space.

Watching Gu Changge’s actions, the Western Saint showed no surprise.

These methods, which seemed akin to the creation of the world, were displayed by Gu Changge in a way that did not surprise anyone in the least; after all, he was capable of suppressing even the Heavenly Will.

"Now, in the Vast Expanse, freed from the constraints of the Heavenly Will, an unimaginable transformation is about to unfold—it will be a revolutionary upheaval." She murmured to herself.

Then, the Western Saint turned her gaze toward the vast, eerie mist shrouding this desolate void.

Her figure flickered slightly as she stepped forward, and she soon vanished from sight. The mist that could trap Heavenly Beings of the Heavenly Seal Realm was as insubstantial as a phantom before her.

As the Western Saint vanished, the Heavenly Seal Talisman—which had hung high above the heads of the Absolute Yin Tower group—seemed to respond to an unseen summons. It trembled slightly, bursting into a dazzling radiance that radiated supreme majesty, effortlessly tearing through the void of annihilation.

Immediately afterward, four beams of light, as bright as blazing suns, flashed away in the direction from which Jueyin Tower and the others had come.

The Elder’s Jade Tablet, which Jueyin Lou had been holding, dimmed and transformed into an ordinary sheet of paper. It drifted to the ground, where it burst into flames with a loud crack, burning to ashes.

Seeing this, Gu Changge made no move to intervene; the Heavenly Seal, summoned by him, had no effect whatsoever.

He cast a glance at Jueyin Lou and the others trapped there, his gaze indifferent. The void split open, and his figure transformed into a streak of light, vanishing into it.

At the same time, in the Land Of Truth, above the He Xu Abyss.

The once-majestic Ancient Altar of Reckoning erupted with an overwhelming, thunderous pressure as mighty as a mountain’s roar and a sea’s roar. Endless streams of light swirled backward, resembling a vast starry river capable of engulfing the cosmos, obscuring the ages.

All living beings in the vicinity trembled uncontrollably; their knees buckled, and they prostrated themselves in worship. Immediately afterward, four talismans shrouded in radiant light burst forth from the gateway.

Within the Council of Truth, the senior elders who were closely observing this scene were all shaken to the core. They rose to their feet in unison, unable to believe their eyes.

A massive earthquake shook the Land Of Truth, causing the space-time fabric to rumble and tremble.

"The Heavenly Seal Symbols have returned of their own accord. It seems trouble has befallen the great clans."

Elder Xuan Sanliu was deeply shaken. Immediately, he reached out a massive hand from a distant realm and brought the four Heavenly Seal Summonings back into the Hall of Truth.

"What on earth is happening?"

The elders of the other major True Clans were unable to remain calm. Within the four Heavenly Seal Summoning Charms, they could not sense the auras of the Absolute Yin Tower or the others; they were even unable to trace back the events.

The Heavenly Seal Summoning Charms were completely empty; the vast reserves of magical power they had accumulated had dissipated entirely, leaving not a trace behind.

This was a situation they had never encountered before.

The elders of each clan did not dare to delay; they immediately reported the matter to the "Kings" of their respective clans, who were slumbering in the deepest recesses, refining their magical power.

However, news that shook the elders to their core soon followed: a certain King had produced a mysterious summons and personally descended to the Council of Truth.

His true form was as vast as eternal night, ancient and boundless, with no end in sight. Standing silently high above the assembly, a single sweep of his gaze was enough to shatter and obliterate any order, no matter how refined.

The elders of the Council of Truth could barely even breathe steadily in his presence.

This King was among the mightiest beings of the Land Of Truth and one of the first to receive the Heavenly Seal Summoning.

"There is no need to delve deeply into the affairs of the Four Great Clans—Absolute Yin, Loathing Calamity, Profound Abyss, and Great Veil. Next, we shall re-elect the Heavenly Seal Clans."

"The matter of reckoning has been arranged separately; do not presume to act on your own initiative."

The King’s cold voice rang out as he reverently held a page of the edict, shimmering with radiant light.

"Yes."

All the elders were utterly submissive, prostrating themselves on the ground and kneeling in silence, not daring to ask for any explanation.

For within that mysterious edict flowed a profound, vast, and supremely majestic aura—one belonging to a True Ancestor.

As for the rise and fall of clans like the Absolute Yin and the Calamity-Avoiding, they cared not a whit.

Soon, a hurricane once again swept through the Land Of Truth, triggering a massive earthquake. The Council of Truth decided to reinstate the Heavenly Seal Clans and bestowed upon them the Heavenly Seal Summoning Symbol.

Due to the exceptional nature of the situation, this era of the Pure Celebration Land would give birth to four entirely new Culinary Celebration Clans.

News of this spread.

The entire Land Of Truth went into a frenzy; no living beings paid attention to the clans heading toward the Vast Expanse anymore, instead rushing to engage in new pursuits and competitions.

Every ancient being who had broken through the limits of the End of the Path multiple times made an appearance; even becoming the “lackeys” of the great True Clans did not bother them in the least.

The members of the Absolute Yin, Loathing Calamity, and Profound Abyss clans—who had originally remained in the Land Of Truth—stared in stunned silence, still unable to believe what was happening.

"Father..."

In the territory of the Absolute Yin Clan, Absolute Yin Ming growled in anger, filled with resentment. His cultivation and magical power were bound to the Heavenly Seal Talisman. As the new Heavenly Seal Talisman chose its master, his cultivation and magical power would inevitably be absorbed by it.

He was furious and deeply disheartened. Although he had suspected that the Truth Council was sending the Four Great Heavenly Seal Clans to serve as cannon fodder and die, he never imagined their attitude would be so cold-hearted.

They had begun selecting the new Heavenly Seal Clans so quickly.

Similar scenes were unfolding on the territories of the Yan-Huo, Chong-Ming, and other clans. It could be said that the cruelty of the Land Of Truth surpassed even that of the Vast Expanse.

Yet faced with this situation, they were all powerless; their fate was already sealed.

……

Vast Expanse。

In the deepest, most boundless depths of time and space—a realm where almost no living being could ever set foot—Gu Changge’s figure arrived here silently and without a trace.

Before him, a luminous golden cocoon was constantly expanding and contracting, radiating a dazzling brilliance. Its light shone as brilliantly as the sun, illuminating every corner of the realm. At its edges, one could discern thread-like tendrils—like tentacles—piercing through the void and extending into the deeper, more distant realms of spacetime.

Each tendril seemed capable of connecting to a separate super-multiverse. Countless tendrils spread out from this region, stretching endlessly into the void—too numerous to count.

Fate, causality, time, and space—any concept or substance—seemed as fleeting as bubbles in a stream before these tendrils, evaporating at the slightest touch.

Within that golden cocoon, a succession of shifting faces contorted and materialized, while the voices of all beings under heaven, countless races, and infinite civilizations hummed and resounded.

This was none other than the true Heavenly Will of the Heavenly Dao, governing the Vast Expanse of all worlds.

Yet now, the Heavenly Will conveyed a sense of fear and dread, unwilling to face the figure standing before it.

In this figure, it instinctively sensed the terrifying scene from its faintest memories—much like when it had faced those three unknown entities. They transcended and towered above all else; the so-called Great Dao and the Heavenly Will were as mere sustenance to them. The Vast Expanse of will that had forged the Chaos Will and the Heavenly Will was being devoured by them.

Gu Changge sensed a faint thread of fear emanating from the Heavenly Will, but paid it no mind.

Even though it had been confined by the three True Ancestors, the passage of countless ages had nonetheless allowed it to develop a certain instinct and consciousness.

Perhaps in time, the Heavenly Will would no longer be content to remain a puppet or a tool; it would seek liberation, the restoration of its self-awareness, or perhaps even the birth of a wholly new being.

But that day would never come.

BOOM!!!

He raised his palm and gave a light tap. Instantly, the Heavenly Will before him was torn apart, revealing countless dense cracks. Even the purest essence of the Heavenly Will split open, scattering into countless crystalline particles that became mottled fragments.

The entire Vast Expanse seemed to be struck by thunder; in every realm of space and time, blood rain, black lightning, and eerie hurricanes appeared. Snowflakes as thick as goose feathers, resembling blood, drifted through the heavens and earth, blanketing every corner of the universe.

The heavens seemed to wail in agony, weeping.

Such a colossal upheaval shook the entire Vast Expanse; nearly all beings at the End of the Path realm sensed this tremor.

This aura was no less than that of the creation of heaven and earth, far more astonishing than the birth or demise of any ancient universe.

Ordinary beings could not yet perceive it clearly, but the Five Heavens—who shared the same origin as the Heavenly Will—detected it immediately and discerned the cause.

"The dissipation of the Heavenly Will... the constraints and suppression it once imposed upon us are weakening..."

"Perhaps that entanglement and struggle, invisible to us, has already come to an end."

All the supreme beings gazed upward toward the boundless, ethereal heights, where terrifying spectacles unfolded. The Vast Expanse of the multiverse erupted in thunderous tremors; time, fate, and causality swelled rapidly, surpassing their previous scale by billions of times in the blink of an eye.

Finally, a colossal roar—greater than any heard throughout the ages—echoed across the cosmos. A blinding, boundless light, resembling the primordial radiance that emerged after the creation of the universe, pierced through the void, reaching far and wide, sweeping across the Vast Expanse.

Immediately afterward, countless particles began to drift down from the heavens, like seeds sown in spring, each seeking suitable soil to take root.

These particles were pure, immaculate, and profound, embodying the deepest essence of the Heavenly Dao.

They appeared as fragments of a shattered, reconstituted mystery, yet they contained the most vast breath of the Heavenly Dao.

These fragments of the Heavenly Dao, like fine snow, blanketed the vast, bewildered world.

In an instant, countless living beings were driven mad and stirred into action; even those who had reached the End of the Path could not suppress that madness and fervor.

"The source of the Heavenly Dao has fractured, transforming into countless fragments of the Heavenly Dao..."

"This is the purest essence of the Heavenly Dao."

Within the Realm of Divine Truth, Su En, the host of the Plague Heaven, acted immediately. He extended countless tentacles, rooting them into the areas where the fragments of the Heavenly Dao were falling, attempting to seize them.

In other realms, Decaying Heaven, Rotten Heaven, Chaotic Heaven, and Lustful Heaven all moved to join the fray, vying for the fragments.

For them, these fragments containing the purest essence of the Heavenly Dao were the only path to their ascension and metamorphosis.

In fact, it wasn’t just the Five Heavens; other supreme beings were also vying for them. Though they didn’t yet fully grasp what this represented or what it signified, they understood it was a colossal opportunity, one that concealed the very source of the Heavenly Dao.

However, there were simply too many fragments of the Heavenly Dao, and since they were formed from the shards of the Heavenly Will, their inherent level was extremely high—far beyond the reach of the supreme beings of the Vast Expanse.
