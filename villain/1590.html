Even if he’s truly in trouble due to the entanglements of the Heavenly Will, you’re not someone that disciple can match.

He operates from behind the scenes, seemingly scattering seeds at random, but there are bound to be one or two seedlings he cares about. As long as we keep a close eye on those few seedlings, he will naturally reveal himself.”

"You’ve spoken too much today. I know nothing of what you’re saying; I am merely the Earth Ancestor’s servant," the old man in black robes said coldly.

“If the Heavenly Will is the Heavenly Ancestor, then let me ask: what exactly is the Earth Ancestor?”

“What exactly does He intend to do? Is there any danger involved?”

The leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance turned away, gazing up at the boundless, profound void, as if muttering to himself.

The old man in black seemed to stiffen and grow cold all over.

"Brother Feng, do you have time to accompany me on a trip?"

Li Qingxu arrived at a picturesque mountain peak. Here, the peaks were verdant and the valleys lush, with birds singing and flowers in bloom. Halfway up the mountain, pavilions and towers had been constructed, and numerous servants bustled about, attending to the many wives Feng Yuqiu had taken in recent times.

The woman’s name was Luo Wuyan; she had an unremarkable face and wore a pale blue robe. She possessed the innate ability to attract good fortune and ward off misfortune.

The man was named Lin Busi. He had bronze-colored skin and a very sturdy build. One of his arms was covered in fine, pale golden downy hair; he possessed the bloodline of the Sha Hu tribe, and his innate talent was absolute invincibility and immense vitality.

Both Luo Wuyan and Lin Busi held Li Qingxu in high regard and were more than willing to undertake this mission.

"Brother Li, is it just the three of us? Shouldn’t we call upon Senior Bingzun as well?"

Before leaving this mysterious realm, Feng Yuqiu asked with some curiosity.

He hadn’t seen the Martial Sovereign who had accompanied them on the way there; after all, the Martial Sovereign was a figure of the End of the Path tier. With his protection, who would dare covet the fragments of the Heavenly Dao on their persons?

After all, each of them carried a fragment of the Heavenly Dao; once targeted, the consequences would be dire.

Upon hearing this, Li Qingxu merely smiled faintly, offering no explanation.

He strolled ahead, his robes fluttering gracefully, his demeanor serene and detached. He looked every inch the scholar, yet he was constantly accompanied by two maidservants—one carrying a sword, the other holding a zither—as if he were on his way to take an imperial examination.

“Brother Feng, you’ve only just joined the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao, so you may not know this yet. As long as Senior Brother Li is here, no one can take the fragments of the Heavenly Dao from us. If even Senior Brother Li can’t stop them, then even the Sovereign of War would be powerless.”

Luo Wuyan spoke, his tone brimming with admiration for Li Qingxu.

Lin Busi added, “Senior Sister Luo is right. If even Senior Brother Li can’t stop it, then even if the War Sovereign were here, it would be of no use.”

Li Qingxu’s strength was indeed unfathomable and beyond imagination.

Judging by this, he might even surpass the End of the Path realm—could it be that he, too, is a Supreme Shattering?

He’s certainly kept it well hidden—I’ve never even heard of such a powerful being existing in the Vast Expanse before.

If combined with Li Qingxu’s innate talent, might his true strength surpass even that of a Licheng Shattered Sovereign?

At this thought, Feng Yuqiu was secretly shaken once more; the depth of the Alliance of Heavenly Daoists’ resources was truly not to be underestimated.
