"System, I’ve now achieved my goal—I’ve taken on three apprentices and won their loyalty. Does that mean all I need to do is make a splash at next month’s grand competition to attain the Dao Realm?" Zhang Yang asked.

Cultivation at the Dao Realm would be enough to look down upon the entire Eastern Region. Not only that, but in this universe, there were very few who possessed such power.

Even within the Qianqiu Academy where he currently resided, only Elder Qing possessed such cultivation.

Just thinking of that Elder Qing, Zhang Yang felt an inexplicable surge of excitement.

She was a renowned powerhouse throughout the entire Eastern Realm—not only young, but also strikingly beautiful and enigmatic, often clad in a long blue robe that concealed her divine features.

However, due to her overwhelmingly terrifying power, the number of beings who dared to covet her beauty was few and far between.

"Once I reach the Dao Realm, I’ll naturally have the qualifications to pursue Elder Qing. By then, winning her favor won’t be impossible."

"Besides, I have this mysterious system."

"According to the rumors, this must be that fragment of the Heavenly Dao. It seems I am the true Chosen One, favored by Heaven."

"Hehe..."

Zhang Yang had a secret known to no one: he was indeed the master of the "Lihuo’s Strongest Master" system.

All he had to do was complete the system’s tasks, follow the steps, take on disciples, and pass down the teachings to earn numerous rewards.

Originally, he was just an ordinary scholar with a bit of talent for painting. He made a living by using his silver tongue to charm the daughters of wealthy families, swindling them out of money and seducing them.

Previously, he had offended the daughter of a prominent family. While painting for her, he secretly drugged her and, taking advantage of a maid’s inattention, defiled her. That was why he had been forced to flee.

Little did he know that while on the run, he unexpectedly acquired a system.

The system assigned him a mission: to enter an academy and become Elder Bing Qichen.

And that is how he came to be at the Qianqiu Academy.

"Complete the system’s mission to attain early-stage Immortal Emperor cultivation."

Zhang Yang listened to the cold, mechanical voice in his mind and a smile spread across his face.

At the same time, a colossal energy surged into his body as if being poured in. A series of crackling sounds erupted, resembling explosions, rising in waves like a tide.

The overwhelming cultivation of an Immortal Emperor manifested within Zhang Yang’s body, and countless profound and powerful insights flooded his mind.

"So this is the cultivation of an Immortal Emperor. It’s simply marvelous."

Zhang Yang closed his eyes to savor the sensation, feeling as though he could traverse the cosmos and tear apart the heavens and earth with a single hand—truly boundless in power.

Suddenly, it was as if he sensed some kind of presence.

Zhang Yang snapped his eyes open, furrowing his brow as he watched the figure that had suddenly appeared before him with caution.

"Who are you?"

he asked in a deep voice.

At the front of the courtyard, a figure clad in a blue robe appeared, staring at him with a slightly strange gaze. Through the sheer veil covering their face, one could make out a faint outline.

"Elder Qing?"

Zhang Yang guessed the visitor’s identity—it was likely the renowned Elder Qing of Ganqiu Academy. She was a Dao Realm cultivator; could it be that the earlier fluctuations in energy had alerted her?

Just as various thoughts were swirling through Zhang Yang’s mind, the System’s cold, mechanical voice rang out: “Mission issued: Deceive the woman before you. Do not reveal the existence of this System. Mission failure results in the Host’s death.”

Zhang Yang was taken aback.

Apart from the mission to recruit disciples, this was the first time the System had issued such a task. Deceive the woman before him—how exactly was he supposed to do that? Should he conceal his origins and identity, or the changes in his cultivation level?

If he failed to deceive her, would he die?

But deceiving people was his specialty—he was a master at it.

With that thought, a smile spread across Zhang Yang’s face. “A late-night visit—I wonder what brings Elder Qing here? Had I known you were coming, I would have prepared some fresh tea.”

“When did you obtain the Fragment of the Heavenly Dao?”

Nan Qing paid no heed to his warmth, but asked coolly.

Zhang Yang’s body stiffened once more, but he kept the smile on his face, feigning ignorance as he asked, “What do you mean by that, Elder Qing? I don’t know what you’re talking about when you say ‘fragment of the Heavenly Dao’…”

“Since you don’t know, then never mind. I’ll kill you, and naturally, I’ll find out everything.”

Nan Qing didn’t want to waste any time. She had finally tracked down the whereabouts of the Fragment of the Heavenly Dao and had no intention of letting it slip away.

Zhang Yang’s expression changed drastically. He was about to summon the cultivation he’d acquired to flee, but suddenly realized that the surrounding space-time seemed to be locked down—he couldn’t move at all.

"System, System, give me more powerful cultivation power quickly, or I’ll die here!"

he screamed inwardly.

However, his System did not respond. As if sensing danger instinctively, a streak of light enveloping a crystalline fragment was about to shoot out from between Zhang Yang’s eyebrows.

Nan Qing reached out his hand. The simple, ancient ring he wore began to glow with a gentle, clear light. A force of suppression radiated from it, as if capable of subduing heaven and earth and suppressing the ages—no living being or force in the present or past could escape it.

As expected, the crystalline shard was instantly drawn in and vanished into the ring.

Having lost the system, the surging power within Zhang Yang began to run amok. His face instantly turned a deep purple, and immediately afterward, amid his defiant roar, there was a loud bang as he exploded on the spot, turning into a cloud of blood mist.

"Is this the Fragment of the Heavenly Dao?"

Nan Qing, meanwhile, examined the Heavenly Dao Fragment within the Treasure of Plunder. Having lost its radiance, it resembled an ordinary shard of glass, its edges glowing with an indescribable hue, as if capable of encompassing all things.

As for Zhang Yang’s death, she couldn’t care less.

"Hmm, someone’s spying?"

Suddenly, Nan Qing sensed a gaze. Her figure vanished from where she stood, reappearing outside this miniature universe—within a boundless expanse of the cosmos.

She looked toward a spacetime node ahead, flickered her slender, snow-white wrist, and struck out with a palm. It was an unremarkable strike, yet it possessed the vast, overwhelming power to tear through the ages and shatter the heavens and earth.

This was the might of the End of the Path—no being within the Dao Realm could withstand it.

Yet, an unexpected turn of events occurred. A muffled groan emanated from the node, accompanied by a spatter of blood. A figure shrouded in a hazy glow swiftly retreated, vanishing into the distant reaches of spacetime in an instant.

"The Treasure of Plunder is emitting fluctuations—it’s the aura of another Heavenly Dao Fragment."

“Another possessor of a Heavenly Dao Fragment?”

Nan Qing’s eyes narrowed with resolve. Without a moment’s hesitation, she gave chase.

As everyone knows, holders of fragments of the Heavenly Dao are rare. Having finally encountered one, she would naturally not let the other party escape.

"Don’t push your luck."

A voice rang out, laced with coldness, followed immediately by a colossal burst of fist-light. A cacophony of sounds and tremors erupted, as if intent on shattering the heavens and earth. The figure had been caught up with; clearly, their cultivation was not at the End of the Path level. Even having unleashed such a terrifying strike, they were still no match for Nan Qing.

"Hand over the Heavenly Dao Fragment."

Nan Qing flickered his palm, and a vortex appeared before him. The figure was instantly sucked in, unable to resist, and was about to be trapped there.

Only then did the figure reveal itself—to everyone’s surprise, it was a woman of striking beauty, bearing six pairs of wings, each pair a different color, radiating the six primal elements of earth, water, wind, and fire.

"You killed one of our comrades. You deserve to die a thousand deaths."

The woman glared at Nan Qing with icy fury, her intent to kill palpable.

“A companion?”

Nan Qing raised an eyebrow.

She hadn’t expected that Zhang Yang had any companions—did that mean all of them were possessors of fragments of the Heavenly Dao?

"Sacrifice Exemption."

Just as Nan Qing was pondering this, the woman suddenly let out a low growl. A pair of wings behind her burst into flames, radiating a dazzling light, while the aura emanating from her body surged and swelled.

Her Heavenly Dao Fragment granted her a special gift: by sacrificing something of herself, she could gain tenfold or even a hundredfold in return…

In her youth, she had offered her Sacred Primordial Dao Bone to a cousin of her clan, thereby obtaining a superior Eternal Life Dao Bone. Later, she sacrificed her Gaze of the Void True Eye to a younger cousin, gaining the superior Dihuan Immortal Eye… Through these repeated offerings, she had grown to her current level.

For this, the fragments of the Heavenly Dao bestowed upon her the innate ability to be exempt from the consequences of sacrifice; she could even offer parts of herself to her future self, thereby exchanging them for even greater power in the present.

In that instant, the woman’s cultivation soared to the Ancestral Dao Realm—and beyond… She continued her sacrifice, her wings bursting into flames.

BOOM!!!

"Infinite and boundless, the sun and moon know no measure; the universe has its end, yet I alone am supreme."

The woman possessed terrifying power beyond this era; six pairs of wings radiated a terrifying glow as they burned and consumed themselves, and she had suddenly reached a level comparable to Nan Qing’s “End of the Path” realm.

"The Fragments of the Heavenly Dao—who would have thought they held such terrifying power?"

Watching this scene, Nan Qing’s expression grew slightly grave. Without the Plunder Treasure, she would truly struggle to contend with such an enemy.

"However, the Treasure of Plunder exists by nature to seize fragments of the Heavenly Dao. Your tactics are useless against me."

"Dust to dust, ashes to ashes."

Nan Qing raised her slender wrist, and the ancient ring once again burst into radiance. This time, the suppressing force coalesced into a massive sphere, enveloping all directions as it bore down upon the woman. The sphere’s perimeter contracted relentlessly, trapping her within and rendering her completely immobilized.

"What kind of power is this?"

The woman’s expression turned ashen. She felt the connection between herself and the fragments of the Heavenly Dao severed instantly, and she could no longer access that previous sense of omnipotence.

Nan Qing’s expression remained cold as he manipulated the Treasure of Plunder, forcing the woman deep into the ring.

She was determined to obtain the fragments of the Heavenly Dao from her opponent.

"Once I plunder the Heavenly Dao Fragment from her, I will possess two. By fully refining them into my own power, I might even break through to the End of the Path Ascension stage in a very short time—and perhaps even challenge the Supreme Shattering."

Nan Qing sensed the myriad secrets and energies contained within the Heavenly Dao fragment.

She finally understood why even those at the "End of the Path" stage kept stepping forward, desperate to seize the fragments of the Dao for healing.

The energy contained within was simply too incredible. Even with the Treasure of Plunder at her disposal, she couldn’t refine a single Heavenly Dao Fragment in a short period of time.

If she were to successfully refine a single Heavenly Dao Fragment and absorb its power, to what level would her cultivation soar?

"The marvel of the Plunder Treasure lies in its ability to use fragments of the Heavenly Dao as nourishment; no matter how unique one’s innate talent, it can refine them and make them its own."

Nan Qing pondered this for a moment before quickly leaving the area.

Since this woman had companions, it meant there were likely many others who possessed fragments of the Heavenly Dao. If they came looking for her, she wasn’t confident she could handle them.

For now, the Treasure of Plunder could only temporarily suppress the innate talents of a single possessor of a Heavenly Dao Fragment.

Zhang Yang’s strength was far too weak; even with a fragment of the Heavenly Dao, he posed no real threat to her.

As for whether Zhang Yang’s death would lead to any future trouble, she didn’t care in the slightest.

"The immediate priority is to find a safe place to refine this power."

Nan Qing left.

It shimmered with radiant brilliance, dazzling and crystalline, as if the very essence of the Heavenly Dao’s myriad laws, reflecting the end of the cosmos and the rebirth of countless laws—it was indeed a fragment of the Heavenly Dao.

He was emaciated, his eye sockets sunken, his head completely bald, devoid of even a single strand of hair, and his entire being exuded an aura of utter decay.

Even a single wisp of that putrid aura was enough to cause the surrounding spacetime and matter to twist and disintegrate, vanishing into nothingness—it could even cause a cultivator’s Dao Fruit to rot away.
