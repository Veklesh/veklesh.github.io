"Why do I suddenly have a bad feeling—it seems this journey won’t go smoothly."

The vast and mighty beings of the Land Of Truth emerged from the torrential gateway beyond the Void Abyss, vanishing into the boundless, dazzling path of light.

The vast and boundless Ancient Altar of Reckoning materialized, reflecting at the very edge of the highest heavens. Endless, dense mists swirled and lingered, drifting down in wisps—each strand capable of reducing an ancient, vast world to ruin.

The Heavenly Lord of Absolute Darkness—who was also the Absolute Darkness Tower—frowned slightly. Holding the Elder’s Jade Tablet, he stood suspended in the air upon the majestic high platform.

“What is Father worried about? Could it be that even now, with the Four Great Heavenly Seal Clans acting in unison—breaking a precedent unseen since time immemorial—there is still a chance of failure?”

Jueyin Ming, dressed in ethereal white, stood beside Jueyin Lou, watching the vast throng of beings vanish into the passage ahead, a look of puzzlement on his face.

“This premonition is more intense than ever before.”

Jueyin Lou’s expression was as still as water.

Jueyin Ming’s heart skipped a beat. As the son most cherished by Jueyin Lou, having accompanied him for countless years, he knew Jueyin Lou’s character well—he would never speak without cause.

Especially since Jueyin Lou possessed an "artifact"—one that possessed the trait of "absolute foresight."

"When I went to the Vast Expanse to preside over the grand ritual, the Tower of Absolute Darkness didn’t think much of it, so I didn’t bring that object with me."

“Could it be that object that gave Father the warning?” Jueyin Ming couldn’t help but ask in a low voice.

Jueyin Lou nodded and said in a voice only the two of them could hear, “Ming’er, you must stay here this time. If anything happens to me, the Heavenly Seal Talisman will return on its own. You must strive to gain the support of Elder Xuan Sanliu at the Truth Council.”

Jueyin Ming’s heart skipped a beat. “Father, I want to go with you…”

However, before he could finish, Jueyin Lou waved his hand to cut him off.

“You possess my original bloodline. Only if you remain here will I have peace of mind. No matter what unexpected events arise, the Jueyin Clan will not perish.” A rare look of gentleness flashed across his face.

Jueyin Ming took a deep breath and nodded slowly.

Soon, Jueyin Lou resumed his usual cold demeanor. He casually issued orders, leaving behind a number of his followers, including Ju Ling and Jueyin Kui—Heavenly Beings possessing the power of two Chaos Heaven and Earth Epochs. These individuals were essentially the most elite and core warriors of the Jueyin Clan.

"Jueyin Lou is being this cautious—he must truly fear that something unexpected might happen on this journey."

Noticing Jueyin Lou’s actions, Tai Meng Yi, the clan leader of the Tai Meng Clan, also felt a slight chill.

He appeared as a blond youth, exceptionally tall and broad-shouldered, with long arms and eyes like brilliant golden suns. Within them lay traces of ten thousand paths of annihilation and the abyss of fate, causing one’s heart to tremble and making it impossible to meet his gaze.

At his side, Yan Huo Jun, the clan leader of the Yan Huo Clan, and Chong Ming Xue, the clan leader of the Chong Ming Clan, also frowned slightly.

“The Absolute Yin Tower holds the Elder’s Jade Warrant. If we defy his orders, it would be tantamount to defying the Council of Truth. The consequences are unimaginable. Given his ruthlessness, he knew full well that this mission was fraught with danger and yet insisted on dragging us along, leaving only our offspring behind. Could it be that he intends to use this mission to eliminate us?”

Though Yan Huojun appeared refined and scholarly, his eyes held a terrifying, malevolent aura capable of plunging the ages into ruin.

“We must leave some men here. I know that the Absolute Yin Tower possesses an artifact capable of piercing the unknown, revealing unseen realms, and untangling the threads of fate shrouded in mist,” said Chong Mingxue.

Though they were all beings who had reached the Heavenly Seal Realm, reputed to possess the might of the Heavenly Dao, in the Land Of Truth, they were still unable to grasp their own destinies—or even discern the destinies of any living being. The Three Cuts had all been obscured and shattered.

Under the shadow of the supreme power of the three True Ancestors, so-called fate and causality had long since shattered, becoming things beyond comprehension or control.

Yet the “strange object” in Jueyin Lou’s possession possessed a portion of the power to shatter this very rule.

It was precisely for this reason that the Absolute Yin Tower had incurred the wrath of the heir to the King of the True Three Clans.

"Then let us follow Jueyin Lou and keep the majority of our clan here. Even if something goes wrong on this journey, we can ensure our lineage continues."

Tai Mengyi said, then quickly issued orders to leave the majority of their elite forces behind.

Standing atop the high platform, Jueyin Lou also noticed the group’s actions, though he paid them little heed.

Before him, a magnificent, dazzling light seemed to overshadow the ancient heavens, transforming into an endless, hazy path of light, with countless beings surging into it.

For the entire Land Of Truth, this was a rare opportunity to reach the Vast Expanse beyond. Even beings from distant realms, upon hearing the news, rushed here at the first opportunity, hoping to claim their share of the “harvest” during this time of abundance.

In addition to the Four Heavenly Seal Clans, other ancient and venerable clans followed in their wake, such as the Moon Demon Clan, the Dao Immortal Clan, the Three Mountains Clan, and the Ashes Clan.

These clans have been rooted in the Land Of Truth for a long time. Though they have never received the Heavenly Seal Summoning Symbol, they are not lacking in Dao Realm experts. Their current clan leaders are all beings at the End of the Path level, having broken through their limits many times, with strength no weaker than that of the Supreme Shattering.

As the vast tide of beings surged forward, several imposing figures within the Truth Assembly turned their gaze toward the gathering.

The Land Of Truth is a place of strict hierarchy and extreme cruelty. To the other clans, the Heavenly Seal Clans appear infinitely powerful, yet in the eyes of the Nine True Clans, they are nothing more than a pack of dogs kept in captivity.

The Great Clans of the Heavenly Seal exist by virtue of the Heavenly Seal Symbols, and all of this is bestowed by the Council of Truth.

They have the power to create the Great Clans of the Heavenly Seal, and they can also annihilate these clans with a single thought.

BOOM!!!

Within the Path of Heavenly Light, bones lay heavy and mist hung sparse, as if composed of fragments of shattered cosmic debris. There, they floated, forming a vast sea of blood, while countless living beings marched ceaselessly along the islands formed by these cosmic shards.

But soon, a colossal fog engulfed the area. All the cosmic debris began to rise and fall, colliding and erupting with energy fluctuations comparable to the destruction of a great world.

Yet the beings who had dared to descend from the Land Of Truth were all at least at the Dao Realm, and among them were no shortage of figures at the End of the Path level.

Apart from those initially engulfed, the remaining beings quickly regained their footing, standing firm upon the fragmented islands formed from cosmic debris, then gazed into the distance with trembling eyes.

The passage that had once connected the Land Of Truth to the Vast Expanse was now shrouded in an inexplicable fog. They could not see the path ahead, nor did they know what lay beyond, leaving them no choice but to remain where they stood.

The source of this thick fog was unknown; no one knew from where it blew. Even beings of the End of the Path realm were unable to see through it.

The Vast Expanse and the Land Of Truth exist independently, separated by a "membrane" substance akin to an atmosphere. Even beings at the Heavenly Seal Realm cannot tear through that "membrane" to descend at will; they can only pierce the barrier between the two by activating the Ancient Altar of Reckoning.

Yet now, even with the Ancient Altar of Reckoning suspended high, it can no longer illuminate the path ahead. The fog sweeps in, obscuring everything, and all beings have lost their way.

The members of the Four Heavenly Seal Clans were deeply shaken.

“Heavenly Lord Absolute Yin, what is happening?”

“When you went to that side to preside over the grand ritual, did you encounter this deadly fog back then?”

Tai Mengyi, Chong Mingxue, and the others asked. Though they were Heavenly Beings of the Heavenly Seal Realm, they too were at a loss regarding this fog that had suddenly appeared.

It contained an aura that made them feel a sense of danger and dread; they dared not approach.

"When I returned to the Land Of Truth, this passage was clear of such things. This thick fog only appeared afterward."

Jue Yin Lou’s expression was as still as water; he too stood on an island formed from cosmic debris.

“If that’s the case, does that mean some being on the other side of the Vast Expanse deliberately obscured the passage to prevent us from descending?” Tai Mengyi asked.

Jue Yinlou nodded. With a wave of his hand, a dazzling, ancient celestial realm materialized—like a boundless black hole where infinite cosmic principles were in flux, poised to swallow the thick fog whole.

But in the very next moment, his expression changed. He hurriedly intervened, halting the process.

The ancient celestial realm shattered and vanished without a sound, utterly unable to withstand even a single wisp of the thick fog.

"This mist contains the pure essence of the Heavenly Dao. This is a power that transcends the End of the Path. Could it be that the Heavenly Will from the other side is blocking our way?" Chong Mingxue’s expression darkened. After a brief sense of the situation, he had already formed this conjecture.

They had not even reached the other side of the Vast Expanse yet, and already they were met with such an eerie force.

This filled all of them with a sense of unease.

"The Truth Council has likely guessed that something has happened on the other side of the Vast Expanse, yet they are unwilling to send their own people to investigate. Instead, they’re using us as pioneers and cannon fodder." Yan Huosuo sneered, having guessed the Truth Council’s intentions.

But there was no point in saying anything now. Even if this mist was troublesome, they had to find a way to cross through it.

Jueyin Lou said nothing more. Holding the Elder’s Jade Tablet in his hand, a dazzling light of order suddenly erupted from it—like the first ray of Dao light tearing through the heavens, piercing past, present, and future. Brilliant and blazing, it pierced through the vast, impenetrable fog ahead.

Within it, supreme laws transcending the End of the Path were at work, and even the passage before them was instantly torn asunder.

However, in the blink of an eye, his expression changed.

"What is that?"

Tai Mengyi, Chong Mingxue, Yan Huosuo, and the others also showed a flicker of change in their expressions—some were shaken, others stunned.

As bearers of the Heavenly Seal Summoning Symbol, their magical power was profound and surging; in that instant, they too caught a glimpse of the scene deep within the fog.

There, clearly visible, was a massive, deep-green halberd planted at an angle in the ground. A sparse yet dense mist was billowing out from around it, accompanied by deep, terrifying whirlpools, as if it were the source.

From that pale-green halberd, they sensed a chilling, spine-tingling dread.

"A weapon of such ferocity that it surpasses the Heavenly Being tier—perhaps even the Heavenly Lord tier, possibly reaching the level of the Heavenly Emperor..."

The group was utterly stunned.

After all, in the Land Of Truth today, only the Nine Great True Clans possessed powerhouses of this caliber—and yet here they were, face to face with such a fearsome weapon.

No wonder the Supreme Heavenly Lord of Absolute Yin had failed.

Had they presided over the grand ritual, they would likely have met a fate of death and the annihilation of their Dao; even if they had sacrificed their own magical power, it would have been difficult to return to the Land Of Truth.

"It is the weapon of the Alliance Leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance."

“How did it end up here? Could it be that the Heavenly Will has been suppressed by the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance?”

Jueyin Lou’s expression darkened. In truth, he had already guessed it—across the Vast Expanse of all realms, only that one possessed the means to block their path.

Back then, it was precisely with this fearsome weapon that Yue Mingkong had wounded him.

"What should we do?"

"Should we advance or retreat?"

Yan Huojun, Tai Mengyi, and the others all turned to look at Jue Yinlou. They weren’t foolish enough to provoke that fearsome weapon; if they forced their way through this thick fog, they feared they would be instantly crushed by it, their bodies and souls utterly annihilated.

“The fog is getting thicker.”

Suddenly, a panicked voice rang out from the distant island.

"Now even our retreat route has vanished."

Jueyin Lou turned to look behind him. The vast fog was blowing in, obscuring even the other end of the light path. Now, the surroundings were a vast, featureless expanse; nothing could be seen.

Even if they wanted to retrace their steps, it was impossible—they would be trapped within it.

"Could it be that the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance intends to trap us here? But if this goes on long enough, the Council of Truth will surely sense something amiss. If the kings of the various clans are alerted, this won’t end with just the Great Reckoning of the Era."

"If we were merely trapped here, it wouldn’t be so bad, but…"

Jueyin Lou’s expression darkened. He suddenly produced a tortoise shell the size of a palm. The object looked unremarkable, its surface bearing the marks of time and covered in cracks, yet an indescribable aura swirled within it.

This aura existed entirely apart from the Great Dao, as if belonging to another, unknown system—distinct and separate.

The tortoise shell radiated a clear glow, but it quickly dimmed. A torrent of blood surged forth, and bones were obliterated. Ultimately, Jue Yin Lou witnessed a scene where his very form and spirit were annihilated, reduced to dust.

This was a vision of the future.

Of course, it was merely one possibility.

Jueyin Lou continued his divination and calculations. The scenes unfolding within the tortoise shell shifted constantly; in nine out of ten instances, he met a direct and immediate death. In the final vision, he glimpsed a vague, slender figure, appearing and disappearing amidst the thick fog. The figure cast a single glance his way, and he died instantly.

"Ten deaths, no survival."

Jue Yinlou’s face grew pale, and cold sweat beaded on his forehead. Unwilling to accept this, he continued divining, constantly draining his primordial essence and blood, until on the hundredth attempt, he finally glimpsed a glimmer of hope.

But he could not make out what that glimmer of hope actually was. He spat out a mouthful of blood, his face turning completely pale, his breath weak and labored, and he collapsed face-first onto the ground.

Had his clan members not supported him, he likely would not have been able to stand.

"Is there no chance of survival?"

Tai Mengyi, Chong Mingxue, and the others stood nearby, awaiting the decision of the Absolute Yin Tower. But seeing his face so pale and his expression so despairing and dejected, a shadow of gloom fell over everyone’s hearts.

They hadn’t even reached the other side of the Vast Expanse yet—could it be that everyone would die here?

Jue Yinlou took a deep breath, swallowed some of his followers’ primordial essence to barely regain his composure, and said, “There is a sliver of hope, but I haven’t seen it clearly. It is one of only three possibilities.”

“Only a sliver of hope?”

A deeper fog settled over everyone’s hearts. Even if there was a sliver of hope, how could they possibly seize it?

“We must try, no matter what.”

Yan Huojun swept his gaze over the beings of the other clans. Without a moment’s hesitation, he swept his arm wide, unleashing a mighty storm of the Dao. Instantly, a vast number of beings were swept forward, only to be caught in a tangle of inexplicable rules and order—vast and blood-red as a sea—where they were torn apart. In an instant, those beings vanished like melting snowflakes.

Seeing this, the remaining beings felt a chill run down their spines, yet they dared not utter a word of protest.

However, Tai Mengyi and his companions paid it no mind. They too were taking action, constantly using the creatures of the other clans to scout the path and test the dangers. As vast numbers of creatures vanished, instantly melted away by that inexplicable order, their expressions grew increasingly grave.

Later on, even some beings on the verge of reaching the End of the Path tier were seized by the group and used as scouts.

The fog was thick and impenetrable, with no end in sight. Relying on such methods, the group’s sense of unease only deepened.

"Ah..."

Suddenly, a panicked, delicate cry rang out, accompanied by a thud—the void itself was shattered, leaving a gaping hole.

A hazy, pure glow blossomed, unfolding slowly like a lotus, enveloping a petite, slender figure like a luminous shield.

She wore a pure white robe and a headscarf; her eyes, clearly visible, shone with a crystalline clarity that stood out sharply against the vast, heavy fog.

Chong Mingxue raised an eyebrow slightly and withdrew the hand she had extended. It was that very hand she had just casually waved, sending a group of creatures nearby to scout ahead.

But she hadn’t expected an accident to occur.

Jueyin Lou, Tai Mengyi, Yan Huojun, and the others were also somewhat surprised. They could clearly sense that the fluctuations of energy emanating from this woman were not strong—she was merely at the ordinary Dao Realm level, not even having reached the End of the Path.

How had she managed to withstand this vast fog without being swallowed up?

“It’s the fluctuation of a foreign object. Does this woman also possess a foreign object?”

Suddenly, Jue Yinlou sensed a familiar aura. He narrowed his eyes, reached out with a massive hand, and was about to snatch the woman—who was still shrouded in the light barrier—back.

Tai Mengyi, Yan Huosuo, and the others reacted just as quickly, moving immediately to seize the woman.

Zhong Mingxue inwardly lamented her lack of foresight—she had missed such a rare opportunity.

After all, “foreign entities” were rare; they represented the “unconventional” and possessed an inexplicable power independent of the Heavenly Dao.

This woman clearly possessed such an entity, which was why she was able to withstand the thick fog of this place.
