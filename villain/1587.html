I don’t know how much time had passed.

This time, the Three-Headed Master seemed to be completely enveloped by a brilliant golden cocoon.

Having failed once, he was now much more cautious. Countless golden dragons, each stretching for tens of thousands of miles, coiled around him, constantly shifting between solid and ethereal forms. Finally, with a loud, piercing roar, they merged into the Three-Headed Master’s body.

His brow, cheekbones, forehead, and crown—every part of him—began to glow, as an aura transcending the End of the Path took shape within him.

With a thunderous crash, the various bottlenecks and constraints within the Three-Headed Master’s body shattered, and his aura surged upward in waves, surging like a tide.

Finally, he opened his eyes. Deep within his brow, it seemed as though a new Heavenly Dao was coalescing, blazing as brightly as the scorching sun.

He opened his mouth and swallowed that fragment of the Heavenly Dao, and at the same moment, a vast and mighty power surged upward, swirling within him like the sun and moon, like heaven and earth. His aura had suddenly reached a level comparable to that of the Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Cosmos.

The First Tier of the Heavenly Realm: a Heavenly Being.

The Vast Expanse gained yet another pioneer.

In the depths of the cosmos, a colossal mass of cosmic energy surged, resonating from afar with the Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth.

"Thank you, Alliance Leader."

The Three-Headed Lord expressed his heartfelt gratitude.

Gu Changge withdrew his gaze.

Had the Three-Headed Master been unable to preserve even a single spark of consciousness, he would not have wasted his time.

Judging by the Three-Headed Master’s experience, becoming a Forerunner is exceedingly difficult—even more arduous than the step of Supreme Shattering. It is all too easy to burn oneself to ashes in the process, leaving nothing behind.

Even a primordial being of chaos like the Three-Headed Master had failed on his first attempt.

It must be noted that the Three-Headed Lord had personally witnessed the process of the Lord of Power stepping onto the Path Ahead, and he possessed fragments of the Heavenly Dao to aid him in fathoming its profound mysteries.

"For the sentient beings of the Vast Expanse, the Path Ahead is still far too difficult..."

"But this is the only chance for advancement."

Gu Changge fell deep in thought.

Everything he was doing now was akin to sowing the seeds of giant whales in an ocean where they had never existed before. Yet for these seeds to grow into giant whales, they would require an immense amount of time to develop.

As for exactly how long this process would take, even Gu Changge himself had no idea.

"When a whale falls, all things are born."

Yet to that colossal whale, figures like the current Lord of Power, the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth, and the Three-Headed Master were still no different from seedlings.

"Even with the impetus of fragments of the Heavenly Dao, the birth of a single 'End of the Path' being would take at least an entire eon—let alone becoming a 'Pathfinder'."

"Even after becoming a Pathfinder, one has only just set foot in the Celestial Realm; the path to becoming a colossal whale is still as distant as ever."

For now, Gu Changge could only set his sights on a far more distant future.

With a flick of his figure, he departed from this boundless, endless expanse of the heavens. To him, time and space were mere substances that collapsed in the blink of an eye; the ages and the laws all feared him and fled from him. After the Heavenly Will had completely collapsed, the Vast Expanse of the multiverse was nothing more than a plaything in Gu Changge’s palm.

In the next instant, his figure appeared within the Nightmare Demon Realm.

It could be said that this was the first time in countless ages that Gu Changge’s true form had set foot here once more. Standing high in the sky, all the dark matter seemed to come alive, erupting in a roar of jubilant cheers like the roar of mountains and the crash of the sea, converging toward him and swarming around him.

The entire Nightmare Demon Realm trembled.

Within the Hall of the Celestial Regent, every pillar of the palace trembled, and the Coffin of the World’s Demise rumbled with a thunderous roar. The coffin lid burst open, and a dazzling, ethereal light surged forth—like an inverted galaxy—casting a shadow of darkness that obscured the ages upon the heavens.

That was the malevolent essence of the Lord of Heaven, which had once manifested at the time of the downfall of the Ancient Hidden Civilization.

But at that moment, as Gu Changge opened his mouth and drew it in, that dark figure instantly transformed into a stream of dark light, surging toward where he stood and vanishing directly into his mouth.

By now, his identity as the Lord of Heaven was no longer a secret.

"I pay my respects to the Alliance Leader."

The spirit of the Gate of Eternal Life—none other than Jing Tianyuan—had once come to the Nightmare Demon Realm at Gu Changge’s command.

It had always served before the Hall of the Celestial Regent, and after the collapse of the Ancient Hidden Civilization, it had returned here.

Now, sensing Gu Changge’s presence, Jing Tianyuan rushed to the scene at once.

Countless beings of the Nightmare Demon Realm, sensing the deepest and most ancient source of power, emerged from every mountain peak and region, like a vast, dark ocean, prostrating themselves on the ground.

"The Master has returned."

Before the Hall of the Celestial Mandate, the High Priestess Wuyue no longer bore her usual aloofness; her voice was thick with delight and an uncontrollable tremor.

Her figure remained shrouded in a faint mist, her features indistinct, yet she strode forward swiftly, drawing a thick, surging mist in her wake.

The four High Priests followed behind her, like attendants.

Gu Changge took a step forward, landing directly before the Hall of the Heavenly Mandate.

As he looked at Wuyue, who was so overcome with emotion she could barely contain herself, his mind drifted back to when she was still a demon beast. In this moment, just as in that one, time seemed to freeze in that blizzard-swept moment of the Three Abhorrences.

"I'm back." Gu Changge nodded slightly and reached out to help Wuyue up as she made a move to kneel.

“Wuyue has finally waited for this day once more.”

“I’ve finally waited for the day my Master returns.”

Two pale blue tears fell down Wuyue’s crystal-clear face, shrouded in mist, her voice trembling slightly.

"You did well. You didn’t let me have to heal you."

Gu Changge’s tone remained as gentle as ever.

Wuyue said nothing more, but simply bent down slightly, rubbing her soft cheek affectionately against his palm.

When Gu Changge first found her,

she had been nothing more than a frail, sickly little beast, abandoned by her own demon clan. She lay prostrate on the barren, lifeless ice plain, calmly awaiting her fate as food for other beasts.

That day, snow and wind swirled through the sky, and the world was blanketed in pristine white. All around the ice plain, many beasts had been drawn by her scent, circling her like wolves, staring intently as they waited for her to draw her last breath.

The Nightmare Clan is a race that feeds on nightmares; they possess naturally powerful demonic energy and rarely stray from their clan’s realm to fall into the Demon Realm.

Yet to ordinary beasts, even an unremarkable young member of the Yan Clan—a race of such immense power—possessed terrifying demonic energy.

As her breath grew fainter and fainter,

The demonic energy emanating from her body grew ever more abundant, as magnificent and ethereal as drifting purple mist, shrouding the icy sky.

Just as her life had been born of the heavens and earth, so too would her death return them to their origins.

The wind and snow grew fiercer, and the gathering demon beasts grew ever more numerous.

All were waiting for this feast to begin.

She didn’t know how Gu Changge had come to that snowy plain, nor why he had noticed her lying prostrate on the frozen ground.

He simply walked over, picked her up, and said, “From now on, you’ll come with me…”

From that day forward, three demonic beasts never left the side of the Lord of Heaven.

The Dark Clan, led by Wuyue, was one of the few truly powerful factions in the Vast Expanse. Before the Heaven Slaying Alliance was established, even the mightiest realms shunned them like venomous snakes.

Even the super-multiverse, with an being of the End of the Path stationed there, dared not provoke the Dark Clan lightly.

Although Wuyue had not yet reached the stage of Supreme Shattering, through the accumulation of countless years, her cultivation and power had reached a terrifying level, and she was just a hair’s breadth away from the End of the Path Ascension.

Gu Changge’s visit to the Nightmare Demon Realm had another purpose besides seeing Wu Yue: to meet someone who, in a certain sense, was his direct blood relative.

Inside the Hall of Heaven’s Vocation, having calmed her emotions, Wuyue regained her usual serene composure.

She instructed the other chief priests to summon Nan Qing.

After the Heavenly Lord of Absolute Yin fled in defeat, Nan Qing had also left the Nightmare Demon Realm.

However, as his master, Wuyue still had the means to contact Nan Qing.

In truth, Nan Qing’s birth was something Wuyue had neither wanted nor anticipated.

Otherwise, she would not have imprisoned Southern Venerable Nan Zun to coerce Nan Qing into serving her.

Of course, fundamentally speaking, Nan Qing’s veins carried Gu Changge’s bloodline; no matter how much Wuyue disliked him, she could never bring herself to eradicate him.

Not much time had passed.

Upon receiving word from Wuyue, Nan Qing returned to the Nightmare Demon Realm under the guidance of a High Priest.

Inside the Hall of Divine Representation, Nan Qing saw Gu Changge for the first time in the true sense of the word.

The two simply gazed at each other, neither speaking.

She was dressed in blue, her figure slender and graceful, her eyes lustrous, and her features exquisitely beautiful, exuding an air of noble elegance and otherworldly grace. She was so stunning that one could hardly look directly at her, yet upon closer inspection, one could discern a certain resemblance between her and Gu Changge in their facial features.

As for her character, she was, in fact, somewhat ruthless.

Back then, to uncover the whereabouts of the Civilization’s Treasure, the Pool of Creation, she did not hesitate to annihilate the Pan Clan. Later, posing as the “Green Fairy” on the Floating Continent under the guise of a descendant of the Southern Venerable, she ordered her men to raise Zhu Xuan—the last surviving bloodline of the Pan Clan—and used him to pinpoint the location of the Pool of Creation.

She also once went by the alias Qing Nan, accompanying Gu Xian’er and others to the Nine Heavens, where she left coordinates that summoned the Dark Clans, nearly plunging the Nine Heavens into chaos…

All in all, one can indeed see a strong resemblance to Gu Changge in Nan Qing.

Even Gu Changge herself does not deny this.

"How should I address you?"

Nan Qing spoke first, her expression far more composed than she had imagined.

She had imagined the scene of actually meeting Gu Changge countless times—feeling anxious, uneasy, reverent, hopeful, hesitant, and afraid… She never imagined it would be like this today, so utterly calm.

"How do you think I should be addressed?" Gu Changge seemed genuinely interested.

Yue Mingkong had once wanted to bear him a child, but for various reasons, he felt it wasn’t appropriate. So even he was surprised by Nan Qing’s appearance.

“Perhaps… I should still address you as Alliance Leader. Any other title might not be quite appropriate,” Nan Qing replied after a moment’s thought.

Although Gu Changge’s direct bloodline flowed through her veins, she was not, after all, a child conceived in the traditional sense, so calling him “Father” would not be appropriate.

“Heh…”

“That’s up to you; a title is just a label.” Gu Changge smiled, seemingly unconcerned.

He greatly admired Nan Qing’s composure and rationality. While she did indeed bear a faint resemblance to his former self, perhaps it was also because a thread of his blood flowed through her veins.

If Gu Changge wanted his woman to bear him an heir, it would undoubtedly be an extremely arduous process.

Yet here before him stood a true descendant—a feeling that left him with an indescribable, inexplicable sensation.

Nan Qing let out a slight sigh of relief, her expression growing even more serene. Although this was her first real encounter with Gu Changge, she could indeed feel that sense of kinship emanating from the depths of their shared bloodline.

“All these years, I’ve been watching over you…”

But the next moment, Gu Changge’s words made her freeze slightly. If that were the case, didn’t it mean he knew everything she had done?

For some reason, Nan Qing felt a slight sense of panic, as if she’d done something wrong or caused trouble and been caught by her parents.

But upon closer reflection, given Gu Changge’s omniscience and power, how could he possibly be unaware that a direct descendant of his bloodline existed in this world?

So, it was only natural that Gu Changge was aware of her many actions.

Seeing Nan Qing suddenly become somewhat self-conscious, Gu Changge was actually a bit taken aback. He shook his head and said, “Although your growth has exceeded my expectations, it is ultimately a source of comfort. You haven’t done anything wrong; to you, all of this is just a minor hiccup in the process of growing up. There’s no need to dwell on it.”

……

“Thank you for your understanding.”

Nan Qing exhaled softly, choosing her words carefully.

In front of Gu Changge, she simply couldn’t be as carefree as she used to be.

“You’re being too formal. To be honest, sometimes I envy those ordinary, mundane fathers and daughters—at least their relationship isn’t so strained.” Gu Changge let out a soft sigh; his tone truly sounded like that of a helpless old father.

Hearing this, Nan Qing couldn’t help but feel a pang of helpless bitterness. At first, she had simply believed her father to be the Lord of Heaven, never once associating him with the Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

Now, the two identities had merged into one.

Who could possibly accept all of this with a clear conscience?

A being so powerful as to defy comprehension—one capable of shattering the very Heavenly Will—even as his own offspring, who could truly stand before him with ease and composure without a foundation of spiritual armor?

After all, even when Gu Changge simply stood there, everything around him retreated in awe.

All the Dao of the world feared him and shied away from him. No matter what living being it was, the moment they laid eyes on him, they would know his invincibility and power—he was unbeatable.

It was as if Gu Changge knew exactly what Nan Qing was thinking.

He still held great expectations for this young man, who carried his direct bloodline—which was precisely why he had come to meet Nan Qing.

“Affection truly accumulates only through time spent together. It’s only natural that you fear me now,” he said in a gentle voice.

Back when he first met A Man—that is, Chu Xiuyan—hadn’t A Man, too, harbored feelings of fear and trepidation toward him?

“I’ll do my best to control these feelings,” Nan Qing nodded.

“From now on, you will stay by my side,” Gu Changge said.

Nan Qing was taken aback, but didn’t ask any further questions. To walk alongside the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance was a dream come true for countless beings; even pioneers like Lord of Power or the Qiankun Ancestor would likely envy such an opportunity.

"Since this is our first meeting, I don’t really have a gift to give you."

“I am now but a hair’s breadth away from the End of the Path. Since that is the case…”

Gu Changge looked at Nan Qing and raised her palm slightly. Instantly, a torrent of power surged forth, boiling and erupting with countless explosions as it violently surged into Nan Qing’s body, causing her cultivation to undergo a massive transformation.

It was as if, in the vast void, a speck of light had fallen and collided—like the birth and death of chaos, the creation of heaven and earth, nurturing all spirits.

In Nan Qing’s mind, countless insights and truths suddenly emerged. Though she had previously been at the Ancestral Dao Realm, the traces of the nine Celestial Waning Tribulations were all shattered in the explosion, transforming into streams of energy as vast as the ocean, which converged into the deepest recesses of her soul.

Her Dao Realm foundation was being reshaped.

Her Ancestral Dao Realm cultivation was also directly shattered.

But soon, her cultivation began to coalesce anew, even more vast and terrifying than before.

Void Dao Realm……

First tier, second tier, third tier.

Real Dao Realm……

First tier, second tier, third tier.

Ancestral Dao Realm……

Ultimately, a power surpassing the primordial chaos of the universe gathered within her. It was the fluctuation of the End of the Path itself; every move Nan Qing made contained the vast traces of the End of the Path, capable of triggering changes across the super-multiverse. Indeed, if she so desired, she could carve out a brand-new super-multiverse from the chaos and void.
