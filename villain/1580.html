Countless fragments of the Heavenly Dao, as if driven by some higher purpose, plummeted from the boundless depths of the heavens and settled across the realms of the cosmos, finally coming to rest. Even the mightiest of beings, if they sought to seize them, could find no trace of their presence.

Though the exact whereabouts of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao remained uncertain, this scene—shaking the Vast Expanse to its core—still drove countless beings into a frenzy.

"It’s hard to believe—these are actually fragments of the Heavenly Dao..."

Within the Heaven Slaying Alliance, the Lord of Power, now a Pathfinder himself, furrowed his brow. In the palm of his hand, a mysterious fragment—neither real nor illusory, neither profound nor obscure—floated and sank. It shone with a brilliant golden light, yet seemed to encompass the very origins of all things in heaven and earth. Even having walked the Path, he could scarcely fathom its true essence.

"It is indeed a fragment of the Heavenly Dao."

Another Pathfinder, the Ancestor of the Cosmos, also held a fragment of the Heavenly Dao in his palm.

The remaining experts of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, however, lacked such prowess. In that moment that had shaken the Vast Expanse, they had failed to intercept the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, and could only watch as they scattered across the land.

"With the Heavenly Will no longer present, it has transformed into countless fragments to nourish the heavens and earth. This may well be the opportunity of the future."

The Lord of Power closed his eyes slightly, sensing a profound and mysterious meaning within, and finally speculated.

He believed that these countless fragments of the Heavenly Dao would bring unimaginable changes to the entire Vast Expanse. Now, as they continued on their path, they had once again encountered these fragments—a clear sign that the limits of the Vast Expanse were about to be pushed even further.

“It must be the Alliance Leader’s doing.”

The Lord of Power spoke with certainty, his tone tinged with rare joy and excitement.

"Every fragment of the Heavenly Dao contains a glimmer of opportunity to ascend the path ahead. In the future, this will give rise to countless pioneers, and the existence of these pioneers will drive the development of the entire Vast Expanse."

"That must be the Alliance Leader’s true purpose," the Ancestor of Heaven and Earth added.

"Indeed. The path forward has been revealed. The Heavenly Dao is merciless, treating all things as mere fodder. After the destruction of Heaven, all sentient beings must use their bodies as seeds and become Heaven themselves."

"This is the third path."

At that moment, a voice rang out within the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

"Alliance Leader."

The Lord of Power, the Lord of Restraint, the Lord of Judgment, the Elder of Forgotten Immortals, and others all bowed in that direction, their expressions filled with excitement and elation.

"Changge." Yue Mingkong’s eyes, too, were filled with radiance.

"Greetings, Alliance Master."

Although the Ancestor of the Cosmos was the oldest being in this era of the Chaotic Heavens and Earth, he still held Gu Changge in high esteem. Without Gu Changge’s assistance, he would never have been able to break through the barriers ahead and reach this point.

Outside the grand hall, the dazzling radiance faded, and a slender, ethereal, and refined figure walked forward—none other than Gu Changge of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, who had been missing for quite some time.

Gu Changge’s reappearance, coupled with the disappearance of the Heavenly Will, was clear proof that he had emerged victorious from his struggle against it.

This was, of course, thrilling news for the entire Heaven Slaying Alliance.

Soon, the news of the Alliance Leader’s reappearance swept through like a hurricane. All the high-ranking members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance rushed to the scene, and the news began to spread across the Vast Expanse.

The appearance of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao had already aroused deep suspicion among many supreme beings, who speculated that it was connected to Gu Changge.

Now, with so many unanswered questions, they were eager for clarification.

Gu Changge smiled slightly and nodded, his gaze sweeping over the Lord of Power, the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth, and the others before turning to the rest of the assembly.

“Our assault on the Ancient Hidden Civilization drew the Reckoning Envoys from the Land Of Truth. Though we emerged victorious, we suffered significant losses—some familiar faces are no longer with us.” His tone carried a hint of regret.

Lord of Power and the others fell silent.

This was inevitable; great battles inevitably entail casualties. The Heaven Slaying Alliance’s overall strength was, in truth, not much greater than that of the Ancient Hidden Civilization. Had it not been for the contingency plan left behind by Gu Changge, the outcome would have been far from certain.

As for the Reckoning Envoys from the Land Of Truth, it was only by relying on the deadly weapons left behind by Gu Changge that they were able to drive them back.

"However, the fallen beings and departed heroic souls may not be without a chance of resurrection. Now that the path ahead has been revealed, once one’s cultivation surpasses the Eternal Heaven Realm, shatters the eternal truths, and creates a process from nothingness to existence, perhaps it will be possible to revive the heroic souls who fell in battle," Gu Changge said.

Lord of Power, the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth, and the others were all taken aback. Although they had stepped onto the path ahead, they still did not fully understand the system that lay before them. They only knew that the Heavenly Dao Realm corresponded to the Heretic Dao Realm, and that there were actually many layers. According to the theory of the old cosmic order’s spacetime, the Heavenly Dao Realm had nine layers, culminating in the Eternal Heaven.

But now, beyond this realm, the Heavenly Dao Realm had been extended by the three True Ancestors. Even Ju was unsure exactly how many layers there were.

"We humbly ask the Alliance Leader to enlighten us."

Everyone present spoke respectfully, eager to understand the structure of the path ahead.

Gu Changge, with one hand clasped behind his back, walked slowly to the center of the hall.

"Now that the path ahead has reappeared, I should naturally inform you of the system governing it."

"Whether it be the Heavenly Dao Realm, the Heretic Dao Realm, or the third path—where one uses one’s body as a seed and oneself as heaven—all fall within the framework of the Heavenly Realm." "Jin Zhong."

"Those who walk the path ahead enter the Heavenly Realm. Within the spacetime of the old cosmic order, they comprehend the Heavenly Dao and break through to the ninth layer. According to the classifications of the Land Of Truth, these can be divided into three titles: Heavenly Being, Heavenly Lord, and Heavenly Emperor—corresponding to the nine layers of the Heavenly Dao."

"Upon transcending the nine layers of the Heavenly Dao Realm, one reaches the so-called Eternal Heaven Realm, which is also known as the Heavenly Ancestor in the Land Of Truth. This process involves comprehending the Heavenly Dao from its birth to its destruction, and the process of creating all things."

"After the Eternal Heaven Realm, there is little distinction between a cultivator’s essence and the Heavenly Dao. One’s self becomes a realm; indeed, every cell is a realm. In the Dao Realm, a cultivator can carve out a world from the Chaos, essentially borrowing the power and matter within it—a process of creation from existence to existence."

"Upon reaching the Eternal Heaven Realm, one comprehends the true essence of eternity, thereby shattering the immutable laws of the Heavenly Dao and breaking the chains of causality, energy, and matter that have bound existence throughout the ages. Thus, one creates from nothingness—fabricating out of thin air, bringing life from death. In this process, the cultivator abandons the external world and no longer relies on it to obtain energy; a single thought is enough to open up a world and create all things."

"After reaching the Eternal Heaven Realm, cultivators thoroughly explore their own selves, transforming themselves into a realm—or transforming an object into a realm. They themselves become the world. At that stage, there is no longer any physical substance to be added."

"Of course, if we apply the theoretical framework of the old cosmic order’s spacetime, the Eternal Heaven Realm could, in a certain sense, be considered the tenth layer of the Heavenly Realm—though it did not exist within that original order and was subsequently created and extended..."

Hearing this theoretical framework, everyone in the grand hall fell into deep contemplation, their hearts deeply shaken.

The Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth could not help but gasp in astonishment. In an entire era of the Chaos Heaven and Earth, only one pioneer had barely emerged.

The fact that the two of them existed in the same era was, in itself, an exceptional occurrence—something that had never happened before in all of history.

And yet, the two of them had only just reached the very first tier of the Heavenly Realm; they could only be considered ordinary practitioners.

"The emergence of fragments of the Heavenly Dao is, in fact, the very catalyst for advancing through the Heavenly Realm."

At that moment, the two realized that if they relied solely on their own cultivation, they had no idea how long it would take to break through to the second layer of the Heavenly Realm—there was simply no hope in sight. Not to mention the reckoning from the Land Of Truth, which could descend at any moment.

If they were to vie for the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, perhaps by comprehending the primordial essence of the Heavenly Dao within them, they could complete their own Heavenly Dao and continue to break through.

"The Eternal Heaven Realm seems a distant prospect, but I suspect the power of the Heavenly Will far surpasses even that realm."

"The Alliance Master’s might is truly beyond imagination."

The members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance now vaguely sensed the realm in which Gu Changge stood—a realm far beyond anything they could currently fathom.

"In the process to come, the Vast Expanse will expand and evolve once more, giving birth to all manner of unimaginable universes that transcend even the Super Multiverse. The fragments of the Heavenly Dao serve as catalysts; all cultivators may vie for them. This is also a destiny of your own making."

"At least for the time being, things will remain quiet over in the Land Of Truth," Gu Changge added.

He then turned his gaze to Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth.

"Above the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization lies a battlefield beyond the boundaries of this realm. It contains sentient beings trapped there by the Land Of Truth. Those who have just set foot on the path ahead may go there to train and deepen their understanding of the Renqing Realm."

“Understood.”

The Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth were stunned. They vaguely suspected that, likely without their knowledge, Gu Changge had already taken action, intercepting and suppressing the invading beings from the Land Of Truth there, turning it into a place of trial for them.

Soon, under Gu Changge’s deliberate instructions, news of the events within the Heaven Slaying Alliance spread rapidly across the Vast Expanse.

Information regarding the path ahead was also disseminated through the members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance across the major Supreme True Realms and super-multiverses, causing a massive uproar.

Now that the suppression of the Heavenly Will had been lifted, cultivation in the Vast Expanse was no longer as arduous as in past eras. With the accumulation of various resources and divine blessings, a single era might now see the emergence of a Dao Realm cultivator.

Now, with the era brimming with blessings and fragments of the Heavenly Dao falling to nourish individual universes or worlds, prodigies and heroes emerge in endless succession. Even the "Sons of the Universe" and the protagonists of the era have begun to spring up like bamboo shoots after rain.

What reaches its peak must decline.

When decline reaches its peak, prosperity follows.

The Vast Expanse has ushered in its most prosperous golden age, a dazzling era of splendor.

The struggle for fragments of the Heavenly Dao has grown increasingly brutal. Whenever even the slightest rumor of a Heavenly Dao fragment surfaces, that region is inevitably plunged into a bloodbath, with beings of all levels rushing in to seize it.

Among these, the struggle for the Plague Heaven, Chaos Heaven, Decay Heaven, Rot Heaven, and Lust Heaven was the most frenzied, having reached a state bordering on madness.

However, divine objects tend to conceal themselves, and the fragments of the Heavenly Dao are no exception. Once tainted by the mundane world, these fragments undergo a transformation—including a change in their very essence—which gives rise to all manner of strange and wondrous opportunities.

When a fragment of the Heavenly Dao falls into a ruin, that ruin begins to revive, developing a self-awareness. It then deliberately spreads news of various opportunities and treasures to attract cultivators, only to devour them.

Furthermore, various beings who obtain fragments of the Heavenly Dao begin to develop mysterious abilities: some acquire systems, others gain immortal bodies capable of traversing time and space, some possess the talent to halt time, while others possess the innate ability to travel between realms to trade goods. Still others gain access to communication groups or auction houses that connect various realms and timelines.

Some fragments of the Heavenly Dao, upon being tainted by certain blood, developed a will of their own. Like the Five Heavens, they began actively seeking hosts, seeking to devour other fragments of the Heavenly Dao to become the "Bing Wei" as prophesied.

In short, the once-Vast Expanse was like a still lake, but now, with the descent of the Path of the Heavenly Dao and the scattering of its fragments, it has begun to boil. Even the deepest-hidden fish can no longer contain themselves.

Gu Changge, his robes as fresh as new, stood there in silence. With a wave of his sleeve, the woman in white, who had been enveloped and protected by a brilliant sphere of light, suddenly flew out from within it.

The woman in white robes looked slightly bewildered. After being taken away by Gu Changge, she had no idea why, and her heart had been filled with anxiety, fearing the worst.

"Where is this…?"

Ji Xiangmiao, Ji Ziyi, and the others—who had previously been puzzled as to why Gu Changge had come to the Ji Chan clan—stared wide-eyed upon recognizing the woman in white. Their expressions quickly filled with joy, excitement, and disbelief.

After countless years, they had actually seen their eldest sister, Ji Xuan’yi, who had once been sacrificed to the Ancient Hidden Civilization for the sake of their ninth sister.

"Second Sister, Third Sister... you..." As Ji Xuan’yi came to her senses, tears welled up in her eyes.

Ji Ziyi stepped forward and embraced Ji Xuanyi tightly.

The esteemed elders of the Ji Chan clan were also utterly stunned; they had never imagined they would see the eldest princess again.

Could it be that Gu Changge had personally traveled to Zhenmaifei?

Ji Qingyi’s eyes were brimming with tears; her voice was filled with joy, yet also tinged with self-reproach and guilt. If it hadn’t been for her decision to take her place back then, how could her eldest sister have become a sacrifice to the Ancient Hidden Civilization?

“Little Nine.”

Ji Qingyi clasped her hands, her voice equally filled with joy and delight. This reunion had come so unexpectedly that her mind was still a bit fuzzy; it felt like a dream.

Gu Changge stood nearby, holding back tears as she watched the long-awaited reunion.

"Thank you, Alliance Leader, for bringing my sister back to the clan."

A moment later, Ji Xiangmiao approached Gu Changge, curtsied gracefully, and expressed her deep gratitude.

“I happened to run into her, so I brought her back along.”

Gu Changge nodded slightly, not making much of it.

The entire Ji Chan clan was immersed in the joy of this reunion.

Ji Xuan’yi recounted her experiences in the Land Of Truth over the years to her sisters, while Ji Zou’miao and the others shared the events that had unfolded within the Ji-Chan clans during that time.

She was quite surprised, but she soon realized why Gu Changge had intervened to bring her back from that realm of oblivion.

It turned out he had done so out of respect for her.

The Ji Chan clan wished to host a grand banquet to express their gratitude to Gu Changge, but he declined, citing other commitments.

Although Ji Qingyi had much she wished to say to him, now that she had reunited with her eldest sister, she felt it would be inappropriate to leave.
