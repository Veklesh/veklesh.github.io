Whoosh!!!

A beam of light that spanned the entire cosmos engulfed the Vast Expanse, and a being of the End of the Path realm appeared within this realm of time and space.

With a longbow slung across his back and dark-gold arrows in hand, he was none other than the Archer.

"The aura of this entity is so strange that it likely does not belong to this Chaos Heaven and Earth Era; it may trace back to the previous Chaos Heaven and Earth Era, or even further still."

The Archer spoke with a solemn expression, gazing intently at the vast and boundless "Biwei" before him. He could clearly sense the dense aura of death emanating from it—this was not a living being.

Some force beyond their comprehension had reversed its state, allowing this entity to rise from the dead.

"How did a being from the previous Chaos Heaven and Earth Era manage to survive? After the end of an era, isn’t everything supposed to turn to ashes?"

The figure, radiating boundless light, approached the Archer Lord and asked in puzzlement. It was none other than Yuan Zhu, another being of the End of the Path tier.

The Tathagata Buddha replied, “The previous era of chaos lies too far in the past to trace. But if there were a way to escape the great reckoning of the Land Of Truth, does that not imply that in even more ancient times, there were actually beings surviving in this world in forms unknown to us?”

His words prompted deep reflection among those present; clearly, this was not a baseless notion.

"Even with the power of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, it might not be enough."

Soon, other figures at the End of the Path appeared in this place, and even some powerful members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance had arrived.

In this vast and desolate Vast Expanse, aside from the Five Celestial Powers, the Heaven Slaying Alliance reigned supreme. Even though the emergence of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao had given rise to a mysterious Alliance of Daoists, the Heaven Slaying Alliance still did not regard them as a threat.

In the eyes of the Heaven Slaying Alliance’s high-ranking members, though the Vast Expanse held many secrets, as long as their Alliance Master, Gu Changge, remained, they had nothing to fear.

The only thing they need to worry about is the reckoning from the Land Of Truth.

"Kill them."

Li Qingxu stood atop the highest peak, hands clasped behind his back, with Luo Wuyan, Feng Yuqiu, and Lin Busi behind him.

He spoke calmly, “Today, if the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance does not show himself, then let the entire Vast Expanse know that the existence of our Alliance of Heavenly Daoists is already sufficient to rival them—and perhaps even surpass them.”

As his order was given, that terrifying, fearsome figure suddenly extended another massive hand covered in black hair, obscuring the vast expanse of time and space, and reached out to seize the Arrow Lord, the Primordial Lord, and the Tathagata Buddha Lord as they rushed toward him.

This peerless power, rivalling that of the Heavenly Dao Realm, instantly shattered the space-time of that region. Unlike his previous attempts to capture Mu Yan and the others, Li Qingxu’s command this time was to kill them.

He was very measured in his actions, knowing that these End of the Path-level beings had not been by the Alliance Leader’s side from the very beginning; at most, they had joined forces with the Heaven Slaying Alliance after its establishment to wage war against the Ju-Zang Civilization.

The Alliance of Heavenly Daoists had always remained hidden behind the scenes, known at most to a few True Path-level figures.

Today, however, he needed to make the entire Vast Expanse aware of the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao’s existence—for the age to come belongs to the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao.

The emergence of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao was the choice of the Heavenly Will; only on this unknown path could the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao follow the most correct course.

They represent the Heavenly Will, and they also represent the correct path.

Even if, in the future, the Reckoner of the Land Of Truth were to descend once more, as the chosen ones of the Heavenly Will, they would be fully capable of evading the final reckoning.

Thus, this will avert the ultimate catastrophe of this Era of Chaos and Heaven and Earth, ensuring it does not end in eternal silence and oblivion like the previous Era of Chaos and Heaven and Earth.

"This is destined to be a path that is not understood, yet it is the correct one."

"The only obstacle now is the Heaven Slaying Alliance—specifically, its leader, that unpredictable anomaly."

Li Qingxu’s expression remained calm as scenes from the distant past flashed through his mind.

His former homeland, his past joys and sorrows—everything he once knew had been buried in that bygone era.

He did not belong to this era, yet for the sake of his own convictions and path—and for the sake of his Master’s ideals—he chose to save this era, thereby proving that their methods were correct.

Bi Wei moved to act, obeying Li Qingxu’s command. All the laws ahead crumbled into ruins; no living being could resist. It was the very scene of the Great Annihilation of all worlds.

The entire Vast Expanse trembled in unison; countless true realms and great universes roared. The Tathagata Buddha, the Primordial Sovereign, and others all turned pale with shock.

“We can’t even escape?”

Their scalps prickled with dread. They had only encountered beings of this caliber during the last time the Enforcer of Reckoning descended upon the Land Of Truth.

Whoosh!!!

At that moment, a blinding beam of light pierced through the profound Vast Expanse, shooting forth from the Heaven Slaying Alliance and illuminating the boundless void.

Two surging forces collided, tearing apart the massive, black-haired hand. Terrifying ripples swept across the Vast Expanse like a tidal wave.

Countless universes and worlds collided and churned there like bubbles.

A figure with three heads appeared here—none other than the Three-Headed Sovereign, the third of the Three Paths of Enlightenment, following the Lord of Power and the Ancestor of Heaven and Earth.

Rather than immediately heading to the realm of trials beyond the Vast Expanse to temper himself, he had remained within the Heaven Slaying Alliance to consolidate his cultivation and contemplate his own "Heavenly Dao."

Consequently, he sensed the fluctuations here immediately.

"This aura is stronger than mine, yet this is not a true Pathfinder. Could it truly be relying on the power of a fragment of the Heavenly Dao?"

The Three-Headed Master ordered the Buddha of the Present and the others to step back. His gaze swept over Mu Yan and the others, who had been captured by Bi Wei, and his heart sank.

Before arriving at the Heaven Slaying Alliance, he had already researched its current members. It was said that Mu Yan, Mo Tong, and the others had been among the earliest to follow Alliance Leader Gu Changge.

Though their cultivation levels were not high, their seniority within the Heaven Slaying Alliance was unquestionably the greatest.

"By targeting them, are they trying to force the Alliance Leader to step forward and intervene personally?"

The owner of the Three-Headed Inn had guessed the other party’s true intentions.

“I may not know which era you come from or what your true intentions are, but the Alliance Master is not someone you can test.”

The Three-Headed Master spoke coldly. Since becoming a Pathfinder, he had never once clashed with a being of the same caliber as the Alliance Master.

Therefore, he also wanted to know just how far his own strength had progressed.

Although the opponent relied on a fragment of the Heavenly Dao to possess the strength of the second level of the Heavenly Dao Realm, it was, after all, merely an inanimate object.

Li Qingxu ordered calmly, “Bi Wei, subdue it.”

He knew the Three-Headed Master was a genuine Pathfinder, but Bi Wei possessed two fragments of the Heavenly Dao—fragments that contained the complete mysteries of the Heavenly Dao, encompassing the path of the Heavenly Dao within the spacetime of the old cosmic order.

If the two were to clash, the outcome was a foregone conclusion.

BOOM!!!

The Three-Headed Master’s expression darkened. Given the other’s brazen disregard, he likely had long anticipated this outcome—which was precisely why he remained utterly unconcerned.

"You're asking for death."

He, too, was enraged. Gu Changge had helped him break through and pulled him back from the brink of death; in a sense, without Gu Changge, he would have died long ago.

Though he didn’t know if Gu Changge was watching all this, it was indeed time for him to act.

At first, when the Heaven Slaying Alliance clashed with the Ancient Hidden Civilization, he had not intervened, merely observing from the sidelines. While there was nothing wrong with that—indeed, it was the most prudent choice—

But ultimately, it lacked a pledge of loyalty.

Here, an unprecedented great war had erupted, threatening to overturn the entire Vast Expanse—a clash between beings at the Heavenly Dao Realm level, a sight rarely witnessed throughout history.

Countless True Realms and universes were caught in the midst of it, as fragile as withered leaves in a storm, on the verge of being swept away at any moment.

Countless eyes were fixed on this scene. Not only the beings at the End of the Path, but even True Path-level figures were trembling with fear, deeply experiencing the true meaning of “The Slaughter of the Path Ahead.”

Even in the deepest recesses of the Vast Expanse, within the region known as the Great Abyss, a pair of dark, piercing eyes opened, gazing intently in this direction.

If a Dao Realm being can already effortlessly tear apart a vast primordial world,

then an being at the End of the Path could pierce through the Multiverse.

The casual fluctuations from the Supreme Shattering and True Path-level beings alone could stir up terrifying waves across the Multiversal Sea, composed of the Multiverse.

These are the strongest beings the Vast Expanse has ever produced.

This is the absolute limit that the Vast Expanse can accommodate.

Now, the battle between the being known as Biwei and the Three-Headed Sovereign has sent the entire Vast Expanse into turmoil; even the most distant universes have been affected.

Terrifying beams of light and chains of divine order pierce through space and time, spanning the heavens and earth, as if two boundless, surging suns were colliding. This radiant upheaval illuminates all the heavens.

Indeed, even in some distant, hazy realms of time and space, fragments of time were stirred, ceaselessly reflecting this battle.

Ultimately, they carved out a battlefield belonging to the Heavenly Dao Realm within the depths of endless spacetime; only such a void of spacetime could bear the weight of their auras.

As the battle raged and their auras collided—a clash representing the paths of the Heavenly Dao—two indistinct paths emerged from the infinite heights of the Vast Expanse, under the stunned gaze of countless beings across the vast multiverse.

One path exuded decay and decline, streaked with fractures and permeated by a thick, putrid stench.

The other path radiated the light of rebirth, brimming with vitality and representing boundless hope.

As these two paths collided, from the very first spark, billions of strands of the laws of the Dao rained down. As they intertwined, they continuously exploded, eventually dispersing into various divine lights that showered down upon the Vast Expanse.

Chaotic light particles rise and swirl; born from the collision of palms and fingers, they scatter, forming the embryonic forms of countless small worlds.

This was, in the truest sense, a clash of paths: the paths of the old cosmic order’s spacetime, and the new path of this present age—where the self serves as the seed and the self embodies the Heavenly Dao—constantly colliding.

In the Vast Expanse, countless beings watched all this unfold with trembling excitement.

Those of exceptional talent even gained insights from it, sensing profound cosmic transformations within the cascading streams of light.

In a certain sense, this could truly be called a clash of the Heavenly Dao.

The Heaven Slaying Alliance—including the Lord of Judgment, the Lord of Restraint, Gu Xihan, the First Ancestor of the Gu Clan, the Elder of Forgotten Immortals, and others—also watched intently, their expressions fixed in a state that seemed both pensive and enlightened.

Within the five distinct realms, the wills of Decaying Heaven, Rotten Heaven, and Chaotic Heaven also grasped an unfathomable truth.

It seemed they had grasped the true nature of the Heavenly Dao.

It was an extremely pure manifestation.

It was as if the outer shell had been peeled away, revealing the inner essence clearly to them, thereby allowing them to grasp the path that lay ahead.
