The leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance looked straight at Gu Changge without a trace of fear, his words ringing with unwavering conviction.

At his level, he had long since cast aside so-called emotions; he felt neither fear nor dread, and his words came straight from the heart.

Gu Changge remained silent, seemingly deep in thought.

"But I am different from you. I am a native being of this realm, born and raised here. Under my guidance, the ancient civilizations of yore flourished immensely. I even personally challenged the authority of the Heavenly Dao, briefly capturing and imprisoning it, thereby uncovering many of its secrets."

The leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance once again revealed a startling truth.

Of course, just as tides rise and fall and the sun and moon rise and set, the authority of the Heavenly Dao also has its moments of strength and decline. Relying on the limits of human capability and the limits of an entire era’s Civilization, he had once, when the authority of the Heavenly Dao was at its weakest, successfully captured and imprisoned it, gaining insight into many of its secrets.

Consequently, his disciple, Li Qingxu, was able to fully analyze the fragments of the Heavenly Dao after obtaining them, harnessing their power through a puppet body to unleash it to its fullest extent, thereby creating an entity comparable to the predecessors.

All of this was a great achievement of their era.

The authority of the Heavenly Dao—what a terrifying and vast power it is, like the boundless depths of the ocean; human strength pales in comparison, as insignificant as grains of sand.

Gu Changge looked at the leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance before him, his thoughts momentarily wandering.

He was aware of the Heavenly Dao Alliance’s existence, but he paid it no mind. In the Vast Expanse of the cosmos, no matter how deep the waters might be, he truly did not care.

He merely wished to watch everything he had set in motion unfold along its predetermined course. Just as the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance had said, he intended to remain a behind-the-scenes observer, interfering with none of it.

Clearing the path and creating the Pathblazers was, at its core, a decision he made to raise the ceiling of the entire Vast Expanse.

Otherwise, no matter how the Vast Expanse might develop, it would be impossible for a Pathfinder to emerge.

The path to the Celestial Realm had already been forcibly severed and blocked by the Three True Ancestors at the very dawn of the Vast Expanse, leaving no way forward for those who came after.

As for the current Alliance Master of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, he had merely uncovered the secrets of the Heavenly Dao through a clever ruse.

His own power is utterly unworthy of even touching the authority of the Heavenly Dao.

“So what? What significance does any of this hold for me?”

"This is not the reason you provoked me or tested whether I exist." Gu Changge said indifferently.

As his words fell, a boundless, unknown power suddenly engulfed the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance before him.

It wasn’t just the Alliance Leader; even Li Qingxu, Lin Busi, Feng Yuqiu, and the rest of the Heavenly Dao Alliance members were swept away as if by a violent storm blowing in from some unknown dimension.

They were unable to move, as if their very souls had been frozen solid. Cracks began to spread across their bodies, turning each of them into porcelain vessels that seemed ready to shatter into dust at any moment.

Not even the Heavenly Dao Alliance Leader was spared. Under Gu Changge’s gaze, his entire body began to crack. These fissures carried a rotten quality, poised to devour him whole until he vanished into thin air.

This was a power beyond their comprehension; even the fragments of the Heavenly Dao trembled, as if they might be crushed by Gu Changge’s gaze.

"Of course it has meaning."

The leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance spoke without a flicker of fear, his tone unwavering as he met Gu Changge’s gaze. “You wish to elevate the entire Vast Expanse of Worlds, but your current method is far too slow. Even after tens of millions of years, there would be little significant change. Yet you can see those countless possibilities—knowing the outcome, you still wish to try, because you cannot be certain how the future will ultimately unfold.”

“All your observations stem from your own attempts and your refusal to accept the inevitable. After all, even you have no certainty that the Vast Expanse will ultimately unfold exactly as you envision.”

Gu Changge fixed his gaze on him, his eyes devoid of any emotional ripples. “And?”

The Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance said, “Rather than letting the future unfold this way, why can’t you give me a chance? After all, all you care about is the result; you don’t care about the process.”

“Give me this one chance, and I’ll give you the future you want to see.”

After speaking these words, the Alliance Master of the Yan Dao Alliance gazed calmly at Gu Changge.

Gu Changge seemed to fall into deep thought as well.

Clearly, in all this time, this was the first time anyone had ever offered him such a deal—though strictly speaking, it wasn’t a deal at all, since the Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance had no standing to negotiate with him.

At most, this was a bold attempt—like a mortal making a vow to a deity, or placing a wager with a god.

Of course, for Gu Changge, such a wager carried no risk whatsoever.

"Quite interesting."

A moment later, a faint smile touched Gu Changge’s lips, and he suddenly took a keen interest in the leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

As things stood, the leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance possessed far greater intellectual depth and strategic vision than figures like the Lord of Power, the Three-Headed Master, or the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth.

Perhaps in matters of building and managing civilizations, he himself fell far short of this man. After all, without relying on sheer brute force, Gu Changge could not have conceived of a better method to use a force as minute as a grain of sand to leverage an entire ocean, temporarily capturing and imprisoning it to uncover its secrets.

Moreover, the existence of the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance has, to this day, remained unknown to the Land Of Truth.

He has hidden himself deeply enough and possesses sufficient means—this is the truth that has been revealed.

"Your words just now have succeeded in convincing me, so you have lived." Gu Changge smiled faintly.

As his words fell, the entire Vast Expanse seemed to be caressed by a spring breeze; ice and snow melted away, and all traces of calamity dissipated. The wounds on the Alliance Leader and his members’ bodies also vanished and healed.

Everyone felt as though they had returned from the brink of death, their bodies drenched in cold sweat.

At this moment, the expression of the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance had clearly changed. Since he had dared to reveal himself, he was, in fact, certain that Gu Changge would not kill him.

As for the unfathomable and boundless power Gu Changge had just displayed, he knew full well he could not possibly contend with it and harbored no thoughts of resistance.

Facing Gu Changge felt to him like facing the boundless Heavenly Will of the Heavenly Dao itself—like a single firefly’s light confronting an endless, pitch-black abyss.

At a time like this, resistance would only be a laughingstock.

However, with a power like Gu Changge’s to assist him, perhaps many of his own ideas and theories could be put to the test.

"Alliance Leader..."

Seeing that Gu Changge had casually spared Li Qingxu and the others, who had caused such a commotion,

The Lord of Restraint, the Chief of Administration, and others approached, looking somewhat puzzled.

"The Vast Expanse is vast enough to accommodate the Heaven Slaying Alliance’s adversaries. The Heavenly Dao Alliance is no match for us; our true adversary lies in the Land Of Truth on the other side."

Gu Changge remarked offhandedly.

The Alliance Leader’s gaze shifted. He had long suspected that Gu Changge was actually an entity from the Land Of Truth, but he had no evidence to support it. Although he had indeed sneaked back from that side and personally sensed its aura,

Yet even after seeing Gu Changge with his own eyes, he still could not be certain.

He possessed astonishing audacity; he had once dared to capture and imprison the Heavenly Will when it was weakened, so naturally, he also dared to take advantage of the end of a Heaven and Earth Era to pass through that gateway, following the Enforcer of Reckoning to the Land Of Truth.

Of course, all of this was predicated on the fact that he possessed absolute confidence and a solid foundation.

"I won’t let you down," said the leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance.

In the end, the voices of Gu Changge and the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao conversing went unheard by the others. Although the high-ranking members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance were puzzled, they deferred entirely to Gu Changge.

Since Gu Changge had chosen to spare the Alliance Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, they would not say a word against it.

The other members of the Heavenly Dao Alliance—such as Feng Yuqiu and Lin Busi—all let out a collective sigh of relief. They had no desire to go to war with the Heaven Slaying Alliance; for many of them, the events of the day were still somewhat baffling.

The Heavenly Dao Alliance’s primary objective was still to gather the many holders of Heavenly Dao Fragments and form their own alliance.

Of course, the current situation looks promising. The Heaven Slaying Alliance and the Heavenly Dao Alliance are not enemies, but they can certainly be rivals.

In other words, regarding the competition for the Heavenly Dao Fragments, Gu Changge, the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, would not intervene.

Mo Tong, Mu Yan, and the others still felt a bit resentful. After all, they had been used as bait and had nearly lost their lives, yet they never expected the matter to be settled so easily.

However, the Heavenly Dao Alliance had also suffered significant casualties during the recent battle.

Nan Qing, who had rushed to the scene, used the ring Gu Changge had given her to seize a Heavenly Dao Fragment.

Her gains were substantial, and she was in high spirits; she did not consider the battle to have been in vain.

As for the conversation between Gu Changge and the leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance, she paid it no mind, knew nothing of it, and cared even less.

Her goal was to break through to a higher realm, step into the Celestial Realm, and become a "Jing Qianlu Ni."

How the Vast Expanse would develop and evolve was not something a being like her could even contemplate.

"Master..."

Li Qingxu was finally able to speak. His back was completely soaked through; he had truly walked the edge of death. He knew that if his Master hadn’t ultimately persuaded Gu Changge, he would likely have been utterly annihilated—body and soul—and erased from the multiverse by now.

The Three-Headed Lord’s injuries were significant, but as a Pathfinder who embodies the Heavenly Dao, such wounds would have no lasting effect; he would recover in short order.

On the other hand, the Five Celestial Will, observing this scene from the Vast Expanse beyond, sensed that the situation was growing increasingly unfavorable.

Although the Heavenly Dao Alliance’s probe had achieved nothing, it had caused Gu Changge, the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance who had been absent for a long time, to reappear.

This made it abundantly clear that even if the Heavenly Will were shattered into countless fragments, Gu Changge would remain largely unscathed.

If he so desired, he could easily use the fragments of the Heavenly Dao to recreate an existence similar to the Five Heavenly Will.

This instilled an even greater sense of urgency and crisis in the Five Heavenly Will.

Gu Changge’s decision to let them grow was, at its core, a calculated ploy—much like a fisherman releasing a few fry into a pond and letting them grow, knowing that the timing of their harvest would depend entirely on his own whim.

To Gu Changge, the Heavenly Dao Alliance’s probing was nothing more than a farce.

Compared to the many valuable insights he gained from the Heavenly Dao Alliance Leader, forgiving the Heavenly Dao Alliance’s disrespect was, to him, a matter of no consequence.

Moreover, as he observed the Heavenly Dao Alliance Leader, he did indeed sense a glimmer of opportunity.

It was a glimmer of opportunity he had never sensed before.

This discovery only piqued Gu Changge’s interest even further.

"I once conducted an experiment."

"I once conducted an experiment." "I placed two worlds of the same tier within a space of absolute darkness—a 'black box'—creating a sealed environment. The beings inhabiting these two worlds could not see one another, yet they were aware of each other’s existence. Beings outside the black box dared not enter it lightly, for they did not know just how powerful the beings within the black box truly were."

"Meanwhile, the beings inside the black box dared not leave easily, nor did they dare make too much noise, for fear of being detected by the beings outside."

"Though the beings of both worlds were roughly equal in strength, the existence of the black box left them uncertain of one another, deterring them from testing each other’s limits."

"Until one day, within one of these Black Box realms, I artificially dispelled the darkness and brought in light, allowing them to see another boundary—and thus all the conflicts and upheavals began..."

On a nameless mountain peak, the Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance and Gu Changge sat facing each other. Before them stood a stone table bearing fragrant tea; the delicate aroma and mist of the tea drifted far into the distance.

The Leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance recounted the many things he had done in the past to understand the true nature of the Vast Expanse.

The Black Box Experiment was merely one of them.

He believed that the Vast Expanse, stretching endlessly without end, was in fact a space akin to a black box, and that the Heavenly Will and the Will of Chaos were the overseers within this black box space.

The existence of the Land Of Truth arose when certain powers gained insight into the overseers’ capabilities, overthrew them, and thereby gave birth to a second world that does not transcend the confines of the Black Box.

The leader of the Heavenly Dao Alliance believes that as the myriad realms of the Vast Expanse continue to expand, they will one day encounter other black-box worlds similar to the Vast Expanse.

Within these worlds, there are also overseers akin to the Heavenly Will and the Will of Chaos, maintaining order within the black box space.

Unfortunately, his power is insufficient to leave the Vast Expanse. To be precise, the Vast Expanse has no boundaries; no one knows where its limits lie.

The Vast Expanse has been expanding continuously, never ceasing.

Unless, one day, he could obtain authority akin to that of a regulator, or truly comprehend the true nature of Cangmang.

"Once you view the entire Vast Expanse as a black-box space, it becomes quite easy to understand the significance of the birth of the Heavenly Will and the Will of Chaos..." said the leader of the Alliance of Heaven.

Gu Changge actually found himself in some agreement with the Heavenly Dao Alliance Leader’s words.

However, he only agreed with part of it. The reason the Vast Expanse has been expanding all along is that it is growing, and this growth is disorderly and chaotic.

Essentially, the Vast Expanse is not a single world or universe; it is the vessel that contains all cosmic spacetime and the Multiverse Sea, within which lie countless voids and forms of matter.

From its very inception to the present day, the Vast Expanse has expanded by a factor of billions.
