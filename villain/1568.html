The seas of reincarnation were in constant turmoil, and a dense silver light surged forth. From the depths of countless spacetime dimensions, countless points of light converged and rained down.

Many figures stood there, their intense gazes sweeping across the stretches of exposed tidal flats and deserts.

No one knows the origin of the Sea of Reincarnation; its creation remains a mystery. Some say it is, in fact, the greatest miracle in the universe.

Upon death, all living beings return here in spirit, washing away their past lives to become the purest soul-light, merging into the Sea of Reincarnation to await the opportunity for rebirth.

The Mother Tree of the Eon takes root within it, drawing upon that pure soul light to condense the Seeds of the World, giving birth to countless small worlds. These soul lights then become the living beings born within those worlds.

However, now that the Mother Tree of the Eon has vanished, though the soul-lights continue to converge, there is no one to guide them. They gather in the depths of the Sea of Reincarnation, coalescing into swirling masses that rise and fall unpredictably.

The soul light there surges with a terrifying power of reincarnation.

Even figures at the End of the Path level dare not venture there lightly, for this touches upon the most profound Dao of Reincarnation—a realm where the slightest misstep can obliterate one’s true spirit, causing the soul to be lost forever.

No one knows whether a being reborn after the cycle of reincarnation is still the same being as before. Even the Lord of Reincarnation, who once wielded the authority over reincarnation, did not he vanish completely?

"Will the sapling of the Epoch Mother Tree truly appear here?"

"Could the calculations be wrong?"

The imposing figure standing upon the reef sighed softly as he spoke.

He wore a multicolored robe, its surface woven with dense patterns of the Great Dao’s “720,” capable of withstanding the ceaseless waves of energy surging from the Sea of Reincarnation.

His name was Tuoba Yu, and he hailed from the ancient Tuoba clan.

This was an extremely ancient and venerable clan, one that had only gradually emerged after the collapse of the Ancient Hidden Civilization. In terms of foundational strength, even those super-multiverses guarded by figures of the "End of the Path" tier might not be able to match this ancient clan.

For the progenitor of this ancient clan was a primordial being born of Chaos, and the oldest and most enduring entity in this era of the Chaotic Heavens and Earth.

Even the Lords of Power are of roughly equal standing before the progenitor of this clan.

It can be said that this marks the greatest shift in the Vast Expanse since the complete collapse of the Ancient Hidden Civilization.

Layer upon layer of spacetime bubbles burst open, and the races and civilizations hidden within the deepest planes gradually revealed themselves.

One ancient world after another, like eggs with their shells peeled away, is now clearly and distinctly revealed before the Vast Expanse of all worlds.

The ancient Tuoba clan was one of them.

With the Heavenly Will completely faded, the entire Vast Expanse entered an unprecedented "Age of No Heaven." Freed from the constraints and oversight of the Heavenly Will, the barriers to cultivation that were once nearly insurmountable are now easily breached.

Many realms have also given birth to a host of bizarre entities never seen before.

The entire Vast Expanse was descending into chaos and disorder.

Order had vanished completely.

Though the Heaven Slaying Alliance was powerful and harbored ambitions to unify the entire Vast Expanse, all living beings understood that the collapse of the Tongzang Civilization would inevitably trigger an even more fierce backlash and wrath from the Land Of Truth.

From now on, the realms of the Vast Expanse will know no peace.

For the Heaven Slaying Alliance to fully restore order and return the world to its former state, it would require at least tens of millions of years—but would the Land Of Truth grant the Vast Expanse of Cangmang that much time?

Yet it seems unlikely that Gu Changge, the leader of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, will ever reappear.

With the Five Heavens in a state of fragmentation and the Heaven Slaying Alliance monopolizing the central region of the Vast Expanse, the major super-multiverses and civilizations are also vying for sovereignty over the various spatiotemporal domains within the Vast Expanse.

Under such circumstances, the races that have evaded the repeated purges and Grand Sacrifices must still emerge to heal the world.

"Since the Sage Ancestor himself said so, it must be true..."

"The sapling of the Epoch Mother Tree is right here."

"According to the Confucian Ancestor, the Sea of Reincarnation actually emerged alongside the Epoch Mother Tree. Wherever the Epoch Mother Tree is born, that place becomes a realm of reincarnation. The fact that this Sea of Reincarnation is now steadily drying up is actually a process of nurturing the birth of the Epoch Mother Tree’s sapling." The figure beside Tuoba Yu spoke; he wore a tall crown and wide sashes, dressed in the full regalia of a Confucian scholar.

His name was Ru Ming, of the Qiu Yi clan of the Confucian tribe.

The Ru Clan is also one of the most ancient and enduring clans. Their progenitor, the Ru Ancestor, was the first being in this chaotic primordial era to expound upon and establish the Way of Ru, and is thus revered as the Patriarch of the Way of Ru.

The teachings of the Confucian Way have been passed down through countless generations; as long as the true essence of the Confucian Way continues to circulate in the world, their clan will never vanish.

"Apart from our few clans, other civilizations are coveting the saplings of the Epoch Mother Tree. I do not know where they heard the rumor, but they believe that by seizing these saplings, they stand a chance of surviving the bitter winter and the end of this chaotic cosmic era..."

"The Ancient Hidden Civilization once secured a sapling of the Epoch Mother Tree, which is why it became an outpost of the Land Of Truth, enduring countless calamities without perishing." Tuoba Yu’s gaze was profound as he stared at the Sea of Reincarnation ahead.

Although the Ancient Hidden Civilization had perished, the true Eon of Winter had not yet arrived. When even more powerful beings from the Land Of Truth arrived on this side, that would mark the beginning of the end of everything.

"The Cold Winter is coming; the embers of all civilizations will be extinguished. The only hope for survival is to find the sapling of the Epoch Mother Tree and replace the Ancient Hidden Civilization," Ru Ming said.

They harbored no hope for the Heaven Slaying Alliance, nor for the other “Five Heavens.”

Even if the "Five Heavens" were to eventually become beings on par with the Heavenly Will, they would still be no match for the Land Of Truth—for it is there that three True Ancestors hold sway.

Only by understanding the remnants of those ancient civilizations and knowing the events that unfolded during the previous era of the Chaotic Heavens and Earth could they truly grasp just how terrifying and unimaginable the True Ancestors were.

The True Ancestors create all things, establish realms, and determine the origins and overarching principles of the world—this is simply not something living beings can contend with.

"Regardless, this is our last hope," Tuoba Yu thought heavily.

The Ancient Hidden Civilization was originally founded by multiple tribes and lineages; only after a long period of integration did it ultimately become a brand-new civilization.

And the path they now sought to emulate was precisely that of the Ancient Hidden Civilization. Even if it meant becoming “dogs” of the Land Of Truth, it was far better than perishing into eternal oblivion during the Epoch’s Winter and the Final Reckoning.

It can be said that, at present, the Vast Expanse of the multiverse can be broadly divided into three factions.

The first camp is associated with the Heaven Slaying Alliance—a path of disgrace and death marked by betrayal and defiance.

The second faction seeks the protection of the “Five Heavens.” After all, the “Five Heavens” are manifestations of the Heavenly Will, each containing a strand of its primordial essence. Their power is vast; if they were to fully transform and merge in the future, they might become a brand-new Heavenly Will.

The third faction consists of ancient lineages such as the Tuoba and Ru clans, which have banded together to form a new Civilization. They aim to supplant the fallen Ancient Hidden Civilization and become the new "rulers" of the Land Of Truth.

BOOM!!!

Suddenly, waves of silver light surged and churned. From the depths of the Sea of Reincarnation, colossal waves rose, as if some terrifying entity were about to burst forth and awaken from within.

Endless silver soul-light swirled and rose, dazzling and brilliant as the Milky Way, a sight to behold.

This spectacle instantly drew the attention of all living beings in the vicinity.

Tuoba Yu and Ru Ming’s gazes also turned toward it instantly.

Amidst the churning silver waves, a massive, ancient, weathered stone stele rose from the ground. Its entire body was a dull gray, with threads of Reincarnation energy swirling around it. Behind this weathered stele, six ancient portals could be faintly seen opening and closing, with boundless light gushing forth from within.

"The Stele of Reincarnation..."

"The Six Paths Gates..."

"The Sage of Confucianism was indeed correct. Although the Ancient Hidden Civilization has perished, the Stele of Reincarnation and the Six Paths Gates within the Sea of Reincarnation were not taken away by the Heaven Slaying Alliance." Ru Ming was overcome with excitement, his breathing becoming rapid.

Only the Sea of Reincarnation could give birth to the Reincarnation Stele and the Six Paths Gates.

The Stele of Reincarnation records the past lives of all sentient beings. It is even said that as long as a single strand of undissipated true spirit remains, upon approaching the Stele, one can, through the ancient characters of reincarnation inscribed upon it, reflect upon past lives and recall memories from countless eons ago…

The Six Paths Gate, meanwhile, represents rebirth within the Six Realms. All beings in the world, regardless of their postnatal race, belong to one of these six realms—the Three Good Realms and the Three Evil Realms—forming a cycle of reincarnation.

The Reincarnation Stele and the Six Paths Gate are, in fact, the beginning and the end of the evolution of the spirits of the Six Paths.

The entire area was filled with tremors and an uproar.

All living beings were deeply shaken as they witnessed this heart-stirring scene.

"Though we do not know why the Gate of the Six Realms and the Stele of Reincarnation suddenly appeared, we cannot allow the Heaven Slaying Alliance to arrive. We must relay this news back immediately..."

Ru Ming and Tuoba Yu exchanged a glance and immediately sent a message to inform their clan of this matter. Although the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization had been completely cleared out, there were still many spies from the Heaven Slaying Alliance remaining here.

Not to mention that the Heaven Slaying Alliance was now teeming with experts, and its Lord of Power had even transcended the True Path, with one foot already stepping into a higher realm. Beings of their caliber would soon sense the commotion here.

Just as earth-shattering changes were unfolding in the depths of the Sea of Reincarnation, in the central region of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, billions of light-years away.

On a majestic, imposing peak shrouded in thick purple mist, the Three-Headed Master, who was seated opposite the Lord of Power, suddenly looked up.

“Everything has already been predestined in the depths of the cosmos,” the Three-Headed Master sighed softly.

The Lord of Power’s expression grew grave as he said, “You were right after all.”

With a whoosh, the two figures vanished instantly, and E Zhengzi arrived at the Chen Huan Yan Hall.

At the same moment, the two leaders of the Palace of Defiance—the Master of Judgment and the Lord of Restraint—also arrived here. A half-step behind them, Gu Xihan, the First Ancestor of the Gu Clan, followed closely, his expression as cold as ice.

"During this time, I have pored over the traces buried deep within the Chaos and captured some remnants of history. The gatekeeper who dealt a crushing blow to the Gu family’s distant ancestor was none other than the vessel of that Great God of Reincarnation."

The Elder of Forgotten Immortality, who was the last to enter the hall, spoke with a grave expression.

Lord of Power Li Zhu continued, “The Gu family’s ancient texts also record this: before heaven and earth were divided, they were one and the same; when the Two Instruments first separated, Yin and Yang took their places—thus the pure qi rose to form the Yang Heaven, and the turbid qi descended to form the Yin Earth. After the Vast Expanse came into being, primordial beings established the Five Heavens, placing the sun and moon above and arranging the mystical constellations. Thus, the Five Yellow Elements multiplied, the Five Qi condensed, and they bore the rivers, seas, mountains, forests, and dwellings…”

When heaven and earth first came into being, there was no concept of reincarnation in the world. Beings born of the post-heavenly world, upon death, returned to the earth and dust. Those with powerful cultivation and outstanding strength, whose true spirits had not yet dissipated, drifted through the vast void, becoming what were known as Shadow Spirits.

Thus, during that era, the yin and yang of heaven and earth were in chaos, with the living and the dead coexisting, disrupting the natural order.

A primordial being, meditating within a great abyss, comprehended this law of order and, in accordance with Heaven’s will, created reincarnation, establishing the division between Yin and Yang, and the Underworld.

This being came to be known by later generations as the Great God of Reincarnation.

"In the end, the Great God of Reincarnation actually lost his true self and became part of the chaos of heaven and earth. If the Will of Chaos had not been slain by the Three True Ancestors, then it would have been the Will of Chaos that created the cycle of reincarnation, and the Great God of Reincarnation would not have come to the Great Abyss to comprehend the remnants of the Will of Chaos..."

"Alas, no being can escape the influence of the Heavenly Will," the Three-Headed Lord said regretfully.

"In any case, the Gatekeeper dealt a severe blow to our distant ancestor. The Gu family cannot forget this grudge."

Gu Xihan’s expression was icy.

"That may be so, but if we can help the Great God of Reincarnation restore his true will and escape from the end of that gateway, for us," the Lord of Restraint said,

"it would actually be the best choice and solution."

"The Reincarnation Stele and the Six Paths Gate are manifestations of the Reincarnation Great God’s source, used to stabilize the cycle of reincarnation. They manifest whenever the Sea of Reincarnation begins to dry up. Such a massive disturbance indicates that the Sea of Reincarnation is not far from drying up."

"If we do not act now, I fear the Stele of Reincarnation and the Six Paths Gate will be seized by those clans and lineages that know only how to hide and seek refuge." The Lord of Power spoke of this matter with a grave expression, yet his tone was icy.

"When confronting the Ancient Hidden Civilization and the Land Of Truth, those clans and lineages hid in the deepest recesses of spacetime, refusing to show themselves.

But as soon as the situation eased and the crisis subsided, they all came crawling out.

"They want to be the Land Of Truth’s lackeys—who can’t see through that?" Gu Xihan’s tone was icy.
