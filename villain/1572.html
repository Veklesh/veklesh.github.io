A terrifying surge swept across the Vast Expanse in an instant, as if the Milky Way had burst its banks. Countless great universes and worlds trembled in this violent storm, like withered leaves in raging waves, on the verge of being torn to shreds at any moment.

This was an aura that transcended the True Path realm, towering above all the supreme powers that had ever appeared in the Vast Expanse of the cosmos.

All beings at the End of the Path level trembled.

Even True Path-level figures and those who had broken through to the Supreme Shattering felt their hearts race, unable to hide their shock.

BOOM!!!

Deep within the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization, everything was utterly shattered. Even the traces of its former existence vanished completely, vaporized without a trace.

The celestial light path was cracking layer by layer, covered in countless terrifying fissures—billions of them, too numerous to count.

Every step twisted and collapsed, resembling a disintegrating star or a crumbling landscape. Accompanied by terrifying tides of light and darkness, shards as fleeting as flowing fire and sand scattered in all directions.

This surge of energy rippled out, relentlessly crashing through layer upon layer of temporal turbulence and the void, striking terror into the Vast Expanse of all worlds.

Countless living beings and cultivators trembled in fear.

"Even the remains of the Great God of Reincarnation are no simple matter; even with such means, they have not been pierced..." The Confucian Ancestor muttered to himself, showing no concern for Tuoba Hong’s fate; his demeanor remained as aloof as ever.

Clearly, Tuoba Hong’s desperate cry of despair moments ago was not a plea; the “Ancestor” he spoke of was someone else.

"Ancestor, how should our clan respond now?"

A voice sounded from beside the Confucian Ancestor.

Ten great realms floated and shifted, like ten dazzling orbs of light encircling him. Within each realm, boundless Confucian and Daoist righteous energy swirled, enveloping him.

Ten disciples at the End of the Path level stood guard around him, supporting him as if he were a ancestral god of the human race, towering above all.

"The outcome is set; none of this can be changed. This is a gamble with everything on the line." The Confucian Patriarch replied to his clan, yet his gaze remained fixed on the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization.

There, amidst the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization—which had already completely evaporated and vanished—a massive skeletal hand reached down. It was struck by the rune, which shone as brightly as the sun, and was pierced as though by a sharp sword.

A tiny black hole appeared directly in the vast, boundless palm.

Immediately, the black hole began to expand, spreading outward like a spider’s web, and in an instant, it covered the entire skeletal hand.

Yet despite this, the skeletal hand did not dissipate or disintegrate; it retained its complete form, still surging with the storm of the Dao, and continued to advance toward the True Void where the Confucian Ancestor stood.

"O soul, O soul, return! Great God of Reincarnation, when will you finally awaken?"

Witnessing this scene, the Confucian Ancestor’s expression remained impassive, utterly unmoved, his words filled with a faint sigh.

In the next instant, a flicker of madness crossed his eyes—a look entirely at odds with his refined demeanor, making him appear almost possessed.

"The foundation of countless eons, the schemes of infinite ages—all come to fruition today."

"Ancestor, I’ll help you."

the Confucian Ancestor roared.

BOOM!!!

As the words fell, it was as if the primordial chaos had just unfolded, and the world fell into an age-old silence.

Above his head, a faint glow appeared, taking the form of a true dragon, yet also resembling a celestial phoenix. It manifested the mournful cries of the Black Tortoise and the howls of the White Tiger, among many other divine forms. One by one, ancient primordial clans emerged, reenacting their evolutionary cycles and their grand, ancient histories.

The faint light unfurled like a scroll or a book, and one by one, portals rose and fell into view—these were the gates of the Wusu Tuke clan’s Pimingju.

From each portal burst forth a beam of radiant light—a supreme treasure, the very crystallization of Civilization, and the pinnacle of wisdom representing bygone eras.

Amidst the surging radiance, there appeared the Eight Trigram Mirror, bronze lamps, the Seven-Treasure Tree, the Cloud-Haze Scarf, as well as swords, spears, halberds, axes, halberds, hooks, and forks, along with bells, cauldrons, furnaces, and chimes… a total of one hundred and eight distinct treasures flew forth.

Each treasure radiated boundless light, embodying the infinite and the endless, the inexpressible and the wondrous, the cause-less and the effect-less—all manner of the inconceivable.

Amidst them, one could even sense the aura of the embryonic forms of treasures of Civilization.

It must be understood that these treasures originated from an era far more ancient than any known Civilization. At that time, there were no blueprints for such treasures; one could say that for a Civilization to forge even a single such treasure required the culmination of all wisdom, fortune, and possibility.

And yet, the Ancestor of Confucianism possessed a full one hundred and eight such treasures.

At this moment, every living being and cultivator witnessing this scene was utterly stunned.

The very next moment, a scene that no living being could have anticipated unfolded once more.

Even within the Heaven Slaying Alliance, figures like the Lord of Restraint and the Lord of Power, who witnessed all this from afar, could not help but be startled and deeply shaken.

"What is he doing?" Old Man Wangxian could not help but exclaim in astonishment.

One hundred and eight treasures rose and fell, like one hundred and eight super-multiverses, surging with intense energy—resembling the vast cosmos, the boundless sky, and even the dawn of a new day.

Each supreme treasure reenacted scenes from the past, transforming into a vast river of ancient civilizational history—from birth, development, prosperity, and peak to decline and eventual exhaustion… every stage of the process unfolded before their eyes.

These rivers of time layered upon one another, numbering exactly one hundred and eight, all surging together to suppress that skeletal hand. In the process, these vast, boundless rivers of Civilization exploded one after another, as if they had journeyed from prosperity to their final curtain call, falling into utter, eternal silence.

Countless streams of light, like shards of crystal, rained down in a swirling cascade—a vast, boundless rain of Civilization, both magnificent and tragic.

Clearly, the Confucian Ancestor was utterly ruthless, directly sacrificing one hundred and eight supreme treasures to awaken this colossal mountain.

BOOM!!!

Every drop of the rain of Civilization that fell upon that skeletal hand carved out a terrifying, deep pit. It was an unimaginable wound; in an instant, the hand seemed to have been pierced through like a sieve, riddled with countless holes.

Having suffered such a devastating blow, the skeletal hand began to tremble and sway, no longer having the strength to continue its descent.

"Woo-woo-woo..."

Deep within the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization, at the end of that celestial path, an even greater, violent tremor echoed—as if the heavens were collapsing and the earth splitting apart. A primal, agonized wail resounded.

The sound was like the low chant of an ancient demon, yet also like the weeping of an ancient immortal, piercing through ancient history and stirring the river of time, causing terrifying storms of the Dao to erupt across vast universes and distant realms.

Countless living beings and cultivators trembled, gazing in that direction with utter terror.

Rip!!!

Another massive skeletal hand reached out from that direction, effortlessly tearing the sky as though shredding a scrap of paper, creating a terrifying, bottomless black hole that plunged into the void.

"Woo-woo-woo..."

The agonized wails grew louder and louder. Countless mists of chaotic light boiled in that place, as if a new world were being reborn. Fragments of cosmic debris seemed to be swept out by the surging tide, while rolling mists engulfed the area, rushing toward the four seas and the eight corners of the world like a burst river.

All the supreme beings were startled, trembling uncontrollably.

An aura—one that seemed to have truly transcended the True Path, rather than merely dipping a toe into it—permeated the air from that place.

Within that sky-piercing fog, a figure of unimaginable, towering stature slowly rose to its feet. It seemed as though it had slumbered for countless eons, its entire form submerged in chaotic matter, making it impossible to discern.

At the slightest of its movements, the celestial light path connecting to the Land Of Truth began to rapidly collapse and twist. Step after step flew through the air, tumbling endlessly and scattering in all directions.

Witnessing this scene, even those at the End of the Path could not help but feel a chill run down their spines.

"The Great God of Reincarnation—no, it must be the Grave Keeper of that path—it has actually awakened..."

"What exactly are the Confucian Ancestor and the others trying to do? Is it merely to awaken this Grave Keeper?"

"Could it be that he intends to destroy that Path, thereby blocking the descent of beings from the Land Of Truth?"

Many beings at the End of the Path were deeply shaken; they simply could not fathom the Confucian Ancestor’s motives.

If he intended to submit to the Land Of Truth, like the Ancient Hidden Civilization, and become its new outpost, then destroying that path would make no sense.

"And who is this 'Ancestor' that the Confucian Ancestor just mentioned..."

"Could there be another entity scheming behind him? Just how deeply hidden is this?"

Some of the most powerful beings had been closely watching the Confucian Ancestor’s movements. Upon hearing his roar, they recalled that the founding ancestor of the Tuoba clan had uttered similar words before his body vanished into nothingness.

Could it be that behind these two, there exists another, even more terrifying being? The Confucian Ancestor is, after all, among the earliest of the Chaos Beings—could he actually be following the orders of another being?

The moment this suspicion arose, a chill instantly ran down the spines of many.

One after another, gazes filled with shock, trembling, and bewilderment turned toward the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization. Originally, they had come only to seize the Wheel of Reincarnation Stele and the Six Paths Gate, and to search for the sapling of the New Era Mother Tree. Who could have imagined that such earth-shattering events would unfold?

If that celestial path is destroyed, will the Land Of Truth be unable to descend?

Many more questions arose in everyone’s minds, but there was no time to dwell on them now.

"It hurts..."

"It hurts..."

"Pain..."

One muffled cry after another, filled with agony and brimming with a malevolent fury that cursed the heavens, the earth, all spirits, and all living beings, echoed ceaselessly. Each cry unleashed countless storms of the Dao, causing particles of chaotic light to rise and swirl. With every collision, dazzling light erupted, as if a new universe were being forged—a sight beyond compare.

The moment the Grave Keeper appeared, he unleashed a power capable of shattering the heavens and obliterating the earth. A single casual wave of his hand seemed poised to tear apart the ages; the river of time itself twisted and shattered. Many "End of the Path" level beings watched in horror, fearing they would be crushed to death on the spot.

Clearly, this was a catastrophic disaster for them. Many sensed something was amiss and immediately attempted to tear open space-time to flee.

However, some beings were too slow. A skeletal hand, vast enough to obscure the entire cosmos, had already reached out. No matter how extraordinary their abilities might be, they were powerless in that moment—they were seized outright.

Crunch!!!

This End of the Path-level being didn’t even have time to let out a scream before being crushed into a cloud of blood mist.

That skeletal hand, as if catching a small mouse, shoved the blood mist and the remains into its mouth all at once, emitting a chewing sound that sent shivers of terror through countless beings.

The remaining beings at the End of the Path, their souls trembling, hurriedly fled far away, daring not to linger. Yet that skeletal hand could effortlessly tear through space and time; no matter how far away, with a single grab, that being at the End of the Path was pinned to the spot, unable to move, and could only watch in despair as the skeletal hand descended upon them.

Yet another being at the End of the Path met its end.

Witnessing this, the remaining supreme beings grew even more terrified. An unprecedented chill swept over their bodies; they dared not linger for a moment longer and fled in all directions.

Even the Lords of Power and Lords of Restraint of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, witnessing this scene from afar, could not help but feel a chill run through them.

"The Divine Being of Reincarnation’s consciousness has long since dissipated; all that remains is the instinct to guard that gateway. As the gateway shattered and collapsed, the essence of Xiang Jin Shi Ni Yuan Zheng has dissipated..."

“This means the beast within the cage has been released. All beings of the End of the Path Realm and above across the Vast Expanse will become its prey.” The Three-Headed Lord’s eyes gleamed as he spoke.

"What exactly is the Ancestor of Confucianism trying to achieve with this?"

An enraged End-of-the-Path-level being who had rushed to the Heaven Slaying Alliance spoke up—it was the Arrow Lord, who had once aided the Alliance during the great war that destroyed the Ancient Hidden Civilization.

"He intends to feed the Great God of Reincarnation, allowing him to recover..." The Lord of Power said in a deep voice, having already guessed his true purpose.

As his words fell, the ruins of the Ancient Hidden Civilization lay in shambles.

Accompanied by a terrifying, wailing sound, the Grave Keeper’s immense, terrifying form—wrapped in endless primordial chaos—strolled slowly through the ruins.

"Hungry..."

"Hungry..."

Wherever it passed, everything was pulverized. Whether it was a world or the universe itself, all turned to dust, utterly unable to withstand the aura it emitted.

Even the laws of the universe shattered and exploded, leaving nothing behind.

Such a scene sent shivers down the spines of all living beings; it was nothing short of an apocalyptic catastrophe.

In the Grave Keeper’s eyes, only beings of the End of the Path tier or higher existed; all other living creatures seemed not even worthy of being called ants. Of course, it did not actively “hunt” them—those below the End of the Path tier did not even have the right to approach. From a great distance, they were crushed by that silent, terrifying aura and reduced to dust.

“Next, it’s time to enter the jar.”

The Sage watched this scene, his expression as impassive as ever. He seemed to have foreseen all of this long ago; even when the Grave Keeper turned its gaze upon him, he remained utterly unmoved.

"Ah..."

That being at the End of the Path was overcome with utter despair. Without even a chance to resist, it was mercilessly shoved into the mouth of the pursuing Grave Keeper, who began to chew it voraciously.

"Is that not enough?"

Ruzu kept a close watch on the fluctuations in the Grave Keeper’s aura, his movements unhesitating as he continued to search for the remaining beings of the End of the Path.

Soon, he noticed the Blade Demon in another direction—having already broken Tuoba Hong’s seal, its aura now withered and exhausted. The Blade Demon was now fleeing far away, seemingly having never anticipated that the struggle over the Wheel of Reincarnation Stele would lead to such a terrifying outcome.

"Very well. Since you care for the human race, I shall let you go..."

When the Vast Expanse faced great calamities and disasters, these beings never made an appearance. Yet now that the war’s turmoil has subsided and peace has returned, they have all emerged one after another, seeking opportunities and pursuing their own selfish gains.
