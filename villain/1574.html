"Have I failed again...?"

"Not only is the path ahead cut off, but there are also obstructors, not to mention the overwhelming forces of heaven and the calamities of the ages—it’s all come to naught..."

"Is there truly no hope at all?"

Within the Heaven Slaying Alliance, numerous sighs could be heard.

The Lord of Power, the Lord of Restraint, and others had intended to intervene and offer assistance, but upon witnessing the scene before them, they chose to hold back.

This was a tribulation belonging to the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth himself; even in his current state, he could not resist it—what could the rest of the sentient beings possibly do?

At the end of the path, shrouded in thick mist, stood nine unimaginably terrifying figures. They radiated no sign of life, as if bound by some force to guard that place and block the path of all who dared to advance.

Yue Mingkong gripped the Eight Desolate Demon Halberd tightly, wanting to act, yet she sensed an overwhelming, unstoppable aura shrouding the void—one that suppressed all living beings, leaving them utterly immobilized.

"The entire world is exerting this suppression." Her slender hand tightened around the Eight Desolate Demon Halberd, her jade-like features turning icy.

The First Ancestor of the Gu Clan, the Lord of the Bureau, and the others all sighed deeply.

“So it comes down to the wire…”

Chains of unknown origin bound him tightly; this power transcended even the True Path.

He felt that this was the true power of the Heavenly Dao Realm.

"In the end, I have still failed to meet your expectations..."

Indeed, one cannot defy the will of Heaven, cannot defy the course of destiny, and certainly cannot defy what is predestined.

An atmosphere of desolation and despair enveloped the hearts of all beings witnessing this scene.

Even the Ancient Ancestor Qiankun, who had plotted so much and borne so much, was still unable to defy heaven and alter fate, or reshape the path ahead.

"Whether it be the old order or the new cosmic order, Yan was wrong."

In the central hall of the Heaven Slaying Alliance, the Three-Headed Lord’s breathing grew rapid as countless thoughts flashed through his mind in an instant.

Whether it be the old order’s cosmic spacetime representing the Heavenly Dao Realm, or the new order’s cosmic spacetime representing the Heretic Dao Realm, both are in fact remnants left behind after the Land Of Truth had carried out its grand reckoning across the Vast Expanse, countless times over.

In other words, these two paths were already dead ends in and of themselves; there was no way to continue along them.

Even if one were to walk down them, they would ultimately be blocked and slaughtered by the barriers.

And to think there were as many as nine such blockers—it was utterly despairing.

"How could the Land Of Truth not know of the Old Order Universe, or the New Order Universe? What of the King of Anomalies, or the existence that transcends even the King of Anomalies—how could such things possibly escape the Land Of Truth’s notice?"

As the Three-Headed Master contemplated this, a deep chill ran down his spine.

The Vast Expanse still pins its future hopes on that King of Anomalies, for it is a prophecy left behind by the old cosmic order.

But is it possible that, in some past order of time and space, during the great reckoning of the Vast Expanse, an existence similar to the King of Anomalies had actually appeared?

This King of Anomalies once fought against the Land Of Truth, attempting to continue the path forward and prevent the boundless multiverse from falling into ruin once more.

And such "Kings of Anomaly"—who knows how many have been born in the orderly spacetime of the past.

Such cycles of destruction and rebirth, reckoning and reincarnation—who knows how many times they have been repeated.

Yet the beings of this chaotic cosmic era remain unaware of this, and thus continue to pin their hopes on the "King of Anomalies" of this very timeline.

"Ridiculous..."

"Truly laughable."

"Whether it be the old cosmic order or the new cosmic order, both are flawed. No being can ever traverse these two realms of order to step onto the path ahead."

His body was cracking like shattered porcelain, covered in countless fissures. From every crack, black mist gushed forth, threatening to devour him.

That calamity energy and those chains of the Great Dao were everywhere—omnipresent, unstoppable, and penetrating every pore.

This made his aura—which had already surpassed the End of the Path tier—even more overwhelming, with a hint of the Heavenly Dao permeating the air.

This was the aura of the “Uncommon.”

The Ancestor of the Cosmos’s aura reached a peak unprecedented in his lifetime—a level no other being in this chaotic cosmic era had ever attained.

The Heretic Dao Dao Realm.

His entire body glowed like a moth drawn to a flame as he swung a fist forward, bold as the blazing sun, charging fearlessly toward the massive hands reaching out from the severed path ahead.

unable to fend off the calamity’s aura—what could they possibly do?
