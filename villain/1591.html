Even while simply standing still, his vital energy was astonishingly powerful, like boiling lava.

During this Ascension Tribulation, he had done his utmost to protect his inner space, ensuring it remained intact and suffered not a single scratch. Ultimately, in the Sea of the Buried Emperors, he engaged in a decisive battle with the former ruler of the alien races, finally subduing him and bringing an end to the tens of thousands of years of turmoil that had plagued the Barren Continent.

Later, he scoured every corner of the Wasteland Continent in search of the secrets of attaining the Dao. After immense toil and hardship, he gathered the shattered fragments of the Ascension Platform and restored it to its original state.

This had led many who had once attained the Dao to be forced to seal their physical bodies and sink into silence within the forbidden zones… If they could not enter the Immortal Realm, even with their talents, their lifespans would amount to no more than a few thousand years; eventually, they would be nothing more than a handful of yellow earth.

It would not be an exaggeration to say that Zhu Wanjun was the love of his life.

He claimed that ascension doesn’t even exist and told us not to ascend.”

Why wouldn’t ascension exist?
