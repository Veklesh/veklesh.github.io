"The Alliance of the Heavenly Dao?"

Having narrowly escaped death, Feng Yuqiu was still trembling with fear, staring in astonishment at the mysterious End of the Path-level being standing before him.

It was the first time he had ever heard that name.

"Oh, so you actually know of the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao? In that case, how dare you lay a hand on the possessor of the Fragment of the Heavenly Dao?" The End of the Path-level being standing before Feng Yuqiu said indifferently.

He wore a hood, exuding an air of mystery, and his jet-black robe, like swirling mist, shrouded his entire body.

Only the strange emblem on his chest emitted a faint, pure glow, like a green lotus petal slowly unfurling, radiating the subtle essence of the creation of all things.

"Hah, to presume to embody the Heavenly Dao—do you lot even have the qualifications?"

The figure shrouded in blazing light spoke coldly.

With the emergence of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, a fierce struggle erupted across the Vast Expanse. Many powerful holders of these fragments, seeking mutual support, formed the so-called Alliance of the Heavenly Dao.

They claimed the authority of the Heavenly Dao as they traversed the multiverse, attempting to gather the scattered fragments of the Heavenly Dao to ultimately become the new Heavenly Dao of this era.

Many "End of the Path" level beings, including Yan, had heard of this so-called Alliance.

Especially over the past century, as the whereabouts of many Heavenly Dao fragments have come to light, sparking various bloody conflicts, the shadow of this Alliance’s members has loomed behind them all.

The members of the Alliance of the Heavenly Dao are almost all possessors of fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

However, even among fragments of the Heavenly Dao, differences in size result in varying divine abilities among their bearers.

This also means that different possessors of Heavenly Dao fragments possess different supernatural abilities.

"You are not a possessor of a Heavenly Dao Fragment, yet you willingly serve as this Alliance’s lackey. As fellow practitioners of the End of the Path, this truly brings shame upon me," the figure in the blaze of light said coldly.

The "End of the Path" tier is divided into four stages: Origin Traversing, Source Fusion, Shedding Impurity, and Ascension.

He was at the second stage—the Source Fusion level.

The person before him, shielding Feng Yuqiu, was clearly far stronger—he had reached the third stage of the End of the Path: Shedding Impurity.

Individuals of this caliber are already capable of independently creating their own super-multiverse; they stand among the very elite few at the pinnacle of the Vast Expanse—yet they willingly serve as lackeys for this alliance.

This was something he could not fathom or comprehend.

"There’s no point in talking. With me here today, don’t even think about taking the Fragment of the Heavenly Dao."

Upon hearing this, the figure before Feng Yuqiu spoke in the same calm tone. A colossal and overwhelming aura surged forth, as if countless universes were spinning around him, emitting a rumbling, thunderous roar that shook the entire cosmos.

"In that case, I’ll test your capabilities first and see if you can stop me."

The Fragment of the Heavenly Dao lay right before him—the very hope of becoming a Forerunner. How could he possibly give it up? The figure bathed in blazing light hesitated not a moment, launching his attack immediately.

BOOM!!!

The figure at the End of the Path who had saved Feng Yuqiu also said nothing. Raising a massive hand—like a boundless golden sun rising from the depths of the abyss, illuminating the ages—he struck out.

A terrifying, earth-shaking collision erupted between them.

Feng Yuqiu’s face turned pale; he felt as though he had become a withered leaf in a raging storm, on the verge of shattering at any moment.

A battle of this magnitude was beyond the comprehension of those at the Dao Realm. Even the mere aftershocks were enough to dim and extinguish an entire universe, draining it of all life. Even the Sea of Time rippled endlessly, giving rise to colossal waves that shook the heavens.

After several exchanges, the figure within the blaze suffered severe injuries—one arm severed, blood flowing freely. His eyes blazed like the scorching sun, terrifying and intimidating. Despite employing every possible tactic, he was wounded all too quickly.

Though both were at the End of the Path, a clear disparity existed between them. Especially when his opponent summoned a mysterious furnace, he was forced to retreat continuously, sensing a terrifying threat emanating from it.

"Where did you obtain this furnace?"

A tremor lingered in his voice.

The mysterious figure at the End of the Path said little. Raising a massive hand, he lifted the furnace’s lid. With a clatter, it was as if the Milky Way above the Nine Heavens had spilled forth, and boundless True Fire of the Dao rained down.

Each strand was capable of reducing an entire universe to ashes. Endless radiance surged across the sky, engulfing the area and transforming it into a realm of utter stillness. The sky was filled with blinding light, making it impossible to keep one’s eyes open.

Moreover, deep within that furnace, it seemed there was a fiery abyss capable of swallowing all things, obliterating every rule and order. A forbidden power transcending the End of the Path emerged, seeking to drag living beings into its depths.

The figure, engulfed in flames, looked utterly disheveled and filled with terror. With no choice but to retreat, it dared not confront the force head-on and fled toward another region of spacetime.

That furnace was eerie; it was clearly not something the End of the Path could have forged. He suspected that a figure beyond the End of the Path’s reach was involved.

As is widely known, in the Vast Expanse of the multiverse today, the pinnacle of combat power—aside from the known Pathfinders of the Heaven Slaying Alliance—includes the mysterious Five Heavens, which existed even before the Ancient Hidden Civilization was destroyed.

After all, the Five Celestial Beings were incarnations of the Heavenly Will—sharing the same origin and lineage. Their methods were unfathomable, and they possessed terrifying power rivaling that of the True Path. In certain aspects, they even surpassed it, giving them the standing to challenge the Forerunners.

Now... with the emergence of fragments of the Heavenly Dao, certain mysterious entities seem to possess strength on par with the Forerunners.

He suspected that another Forerunner might have emerged in a realm unknown to all sentient beings.

"They’re quite sensible."

"After all, it is a divine artifact bestowed by the Master; it is more than sufficient to deal with ordinary beings of the End of the Path level."

Seeing the figure in the light and flames flee without further coveting the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, the mysterious figure shrouded in black mist ceased its pursuit and recalled the Annihilation Furnace.

Feng Yuqiu watched all this with trembling eyes, never having imagined that this mysterious End of the Path-level being before him would actually speak of a so-called “Master.”

What kind of figure could this “Master” be?

As if anticipating Feng Yuqiu’s shock and confusion, the mysterious Road’s End-level figure smiled faintly and said, “The crisis has been temporarily averted; you need not fear. Since you are the possessor of a Heavenly Dao Fragment, it means you have been recognized by the Heavenly Will. You are the ‘fellow traveler’ my master spoke of, so rest assured I will not covet your Heavenly Dao Fragment.

"As for my origins, I will explain everything to you later so you can understand. Before that, however, I must first take you away from this place. That End of the Path-level figure from earlier was concealing his identity, but I have already deduced who he is—he is likely the Flame Lord who emerged after the collapse of the Ancient Hidden Civilization. I managed to drive him back, but that doesn’t mean he will give up."

"Furthermore, the disturbances here have likely already drawn the attention of the other supreme beings in the Vast Expanse. Though the Five Heavens have remained relatively quiet these past years, they have secretly amassed considerable power and acquired some fragments of the Heavenly Dao. If they sense your fragment, there will be no stopping them."

With that, he tore open this frozen region of spacetime and, carrying the still-shaken Feng Yuqiu, departed from here, stepping into another sea of spacetime filled with void and chaos.

Shortly after their figures vanished, several equally formidable auras descended, sweeping through the area like a mighty gale.

"We’re too late..."

"The battle is over. It seems someone beat us to that fragment of the Heavenly Dao."

Several overwhelming and terrifying thoughts surged like a vast ocean, then gradually faded away.

However, several figures refused to give up, tracing the battle’s aftermath to discover who had claimed the fragment of the Heavenly Dao.

As for what had transpired in the Tuolin Universe, it was of no consequence to them. In the Vast Expanse of the cosmos, universes perished and new worlds were born every day. Since the most crucial fragment of the Heavenly Dao had already been snatched away, the fate of a single universe—whether it lived or died—was of no concern to them.

"The Tuolin Universe has been left in such a shattered state, and even a powerhouse like the ancient Liu Clan has collapsed..."

On a barren, gray-brown planet, Mo Tong appeared there with Yan Ning’er. Gazing toward the direction of the Tuolin Universe, they could still sense the terrifying fluctuations emanating from that direction.

Yan Ning’er stood frozen in place. Her clan was still in the Tuolin Universe, but judging by the current situation, they would undoubtedly be swept up in the aftermath. Whether they would survive was now highly uncertain.

She could only pray silently, hoping her clan would escape unscathed.

Mo Tong, however, felt relieved that they had left in time. Had they been caught up in an End of the Path-level battle, even ten thousand lives would not have been enough to save her.

"Wherever a fragment of the Heavenly Dao appears, it is bound to trigger a terrifying battle—unless one finds a fragment that has not yet awakened."

She began to ponder her next course of action.

Although she possessed the Banner of a Hundred Million Souls, it was impossible to wrest a Heavenly Dao Fragment from the hands of a figure at the End of the Path; such a trial was far too perilous.

However, there was one possibility: the Heavenly Dao fragment’s owner might not have awakened it yet. If she could find the fragment before that happened, she might have a chance.

Every possessor of a Heavenly Dao Fragment goes through a period of "unawakened" existence.

Suddenly, a cross-domain communication jade talisman in Mo Tong’s bosom glowed, radiating an inexplicable radiance. She paused for a moment, then took it out. Amidst the hazy shower of light, a bright and captivating figure emerged, her hair flowing in the breeze,her graceful, slender form resembling a blooming celestial lotus, and a pair of pale golden eyes that were particularly striking.

"Mo Tong..."

The woman spoke, her voice clear as a silver bell, accompanied by a charming smile as faint dimples appeared on her cheeks.

"Sister Mu Yan." A faint smile touched Mo Tong’s face, which was usually as cold as ice.

It could be said that Mu Yan and the White Bone Ancestral King were the people closest to her. As for Gu Changge, her sense of reverence toward him outweighed any feelings of closeness.

"I heard you’re near the Tuolin Universe. There’s news of fragments of the Heavenly Dao appearing there. Are you all right?" Mu Yan asked through a communication jade talisman, her voice filled with concern.

"I managed to avoid it; I wasn’t in any danger," Mo Tong explained.

The Heaven Slaying Alliance was also closely monitoring the movements of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao within the Vast Expanse. The leaders of the major civilizations were all eager to obtain these fragments and become Pathfinders.

As far as Mo Tong knew, aside from the Lord of Power and the Ancient Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth, the Heaven Slaying Alliance had not yet produced a third Pathfinder.

Those considered most likely to step onto the Path—the Lord of the Bureau, the Lord of Restraint, and Gu Xihan, the First Ancestor of the Gu Clan—had yet to make a move.

Many high-ranking members believe that the key to these figures stepping onto the Path lies in the fragments of the Heavenly Dao; therefore, as soon as news of a fragment surfaces, they immediately dispatch agents to investigate.

With the assistance of the Lord of Power and the Ancestor of the Heavens and Earth, the Heaven Slaying Alliance has also established an area resembling a containment zone—a region capable of suppressing certain properties of the Heavenly Dao Fragments—which they have named the Heavenly Dao Hall.

Mu Yan would learn of the news from the Tuo Lin Universe, and Mo Tong’s intentions were not hidden.

"I’m glad you’re safe. Don’t push yourself too hard." Mu Yan exhaled in relief; she understood Mo Tong well and was aware of the emotional changes he had undergone following the death of the White Bone Ancestral King.

Mo Tong nodded.

At that moment, Mu Yan noticed Yan Ning’er standing beside Mo Tong and was slightly surprised.

Mo Tong thought for a moment, then recounted Yan Ning’er’s story and explained why she had saved her, suggesting that it might be a test set by Alliance Leader Gu Changge.

"You ran into him here?"

Mu Yan wasn’t particularly interested in Yan Ning’er’s story; rather, she was somewhat surprised that Mo Tong had actually encountered Gu Changge, a figure as elusive as a dragon—seen only in fleeting glimpses.

After all, over the past hundred years, almost none of the high-ranking members of the Heaven Slaying Alliance had seen Gu Changge. Many believed he might have been injured in his struggle against the Heavenly Will and was recuperating somewhere.

Even when she went looking for Gu Changge, she hadn’t found him.

"That guy..." Mu Yan muttered under her breath, then paused to think before adding, "Wait here for me. I’ll come find you."

Mo Tong was slightly surprised, but she quickly recalled the relationship between Mu Yan and Gu Changge; she assumed that was why Mu Yan had come.

Back in the Xianling Civilization, Mu Yan had always been by Gu Changge’s side; their relationship was not something she could casually speculate about.

Yan Ning’er, listening to their unabashed conversation, was completely stunned. She had wondered about the origins of this mysterious woman, but never in her wildest dreams had she imagined she was connected to the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

Sharp-witted as she was, she had already vaguely guessed Mo Tong’s identity from this simple exchange.

"Senior, do you know the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance?" Yan Ning’er couldn’t help but ask, her eyes filled with reverence and awe.

Across the Vast Expanse of the cosmos, there was scarcely a living being unaware of the existence of the Heaven Slaying Alliance—especially its Alliance Master.

He was the supreme figure who had defied the Land Of Truth, shattered the Heavenly Will, and annihilated the Ancient Hidden Civilization. In the many legends that circulated, he was already regarded as no different from a deity—indeed, to describe him as a deity was almost a form of blasphemy.

Mo Tong nodded upon hearing this, offering no explanation.

Yan Ning’er was even more stunned. No wonder Mo Tong, who appeared to be about the same age as her, was capable of standing against a Real Dao Realm cultivator. Such a prodigy could likely only be found within the Heaven Slaying Alliance.

"Tell me about that Fragment of the Heavenly Dao belonging to Feng Yuqiu. Just how extraordinary is it?" Mo Tong asked.

As far as she knew, the power of each Heavenly Dao Fragment was unique; some could even grant wishes, create objects out of thin air, reverse time, freeze the future, ward off misfortune, or reveal one’s destiny… truly beyond belief.

"Yes, Senior..."

Yan Ning’er snapped back to her senses and replied respectfully, “As far as I understand, the nature of Feng Yuqiu’s Heavenly Dao Fragment is related to taking concubines. Whenever he takes a concubine, he gains power, alters her innate constitution and talent, and can even obtain divine artifacts, scriptures, and elixirs…”

"However, there is one prerequisite: the concubines he takes must possess a certain level of innate talent and constitution; they cannot be ordinary people. The stronger their innate talent and constitution, the greater the benefits he gains. It is through this method that Feng Yuqiu has been able to grow to his current level in just a few decades."

Even she found it hard to believe when she thought about it—that such a miraculous and extraordinary thing truly existed in this world. Without the need for arduous cultivation, it could allow one to make leaps of a thousand miles overnight, achieving what would take ordinary people thousands or even tens of thousands of years of arduous practice.

No wonder the fragments of the Heavenly Dao drive so many beings into a frenzy, causing them to flock to them like birds to grain.

Mo Tong nodded, inwardly marveling. This was the very thing reshaping the landscape of the Vast Expanse—it was simply too defiant of the heavens.

In the face of the Fragments of the Heavenly Dao, effort and talent are meaningless; everything depends on luck.

"From the looks of it, the Heavenly Dao Fragment Feng Yuqiu obtained can only be considered of upper-middle quality." Mu Yan, listening through the communication jade talisman, couldn’t help but nod in agreement.

Over the past century, the Heaven Slaying Alliance had encountered numerous fragments of the Heavenly Dao—or rather, their possessors. After studying their inherent properties, they had broadly categorized them into three tiers: superior, middle, and inferior.

Take the upper-middle-grade fragments of the Heavenly Dao, such as the one Feng Yuqiu possesses: to reap their benefits, one must first meet certain prerequisites—specific exemption requirements.

The lower the difficulty of the exemption requirement, the higher the quality of the Heavenly Dao Fragment; conversely, the higher the difficulty, the lower the quality.

Taking a concubine is the exemption requirement for Feng Yuqiu’s Heavenly Dao Fragment. For him, this is not difficult; therefore, in Mu Yan’s view, this Heavenly Dao Fragment could be classified as “Superior” quality.

Similar requirements include bearing offspring, signing up servants, making friends, and gaining subordinates...

As far as she knows, there are also some top-quality Heavenly Dao Fragments whose exemption requirements are even simpler—simply going to a certain location and checking in is enough to receive rewards. There are similar ones as well, such as being able to check in anew every day to refresh the rewards.

As for the lower-tier fragments of the Heavenly Dao, the requirements are much more challenging and involve tasks of varying difficulty.

Of course, this is just one category of Heavenly Dao Fragments. There are other categories that do not offer tiered rewards but instead directly grant the possessor special innate abilities, such as Conceptual Invincibility, Blustering into Reality, Plunder and Devour, Causal Bonding, Immunity to Death, Cost Transfer, Artifact Creation, and Time-Space Travel, among others.
