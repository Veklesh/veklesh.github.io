Directly above the barrier, a massive gaping hole could be seen, leading into the depths of the unknown. Within it, a colossal heart pounded violently, with fragments of the Heavenly Dao seemingly embedded and imprinted there.

In the boundless void surrounding it, tentacles spread out like gaping mouths, ceaselessly devouring and consuming the cosmic energy of the waves. Layer upon layer of spiritual energy, surging like raging waves, roared toward this place, only to be swallowed up by those gaping mouths.

Upon closer inspection, one would discover that the surrounding star systems bordering this cosmic barrier had long since been drained dry; all life-giving energy was converging here, as if an invisible and terrifying giant hand were strangling and plundering one universe after another.

Countless living beings, regardless of their realm, were now nothing more than piles of bleached bones; a mere breath of wind would scatter them into dust and ashes.

A cultivator in the Dao Realm, secluded in some remote corner of the cosmos, was suddenly jolted awake. He froze for a moment, then his expression turned ashen.

Before he had a chance to react, ripples of energy spread out, gently sweeping over his body. His Dao Fruit shattered, the flame of life extinguished, his face still frozen in shock and horror, and in the next instant, his entire being turned to ash. Feng Yuqiu, Lin Busi,Luo Wuyan, and the others watched this scene through the luminous mirror, their expressions varying—some trembling with fear, others stunned, and still others filled with disbelief.

Only Li Qingxu remained calm, his hands clasped behind his back.

A moment later, as if sensing something, a hint of world-weariness flashed across his calm, profound eyes.

"Return."

Li Qingxu spoke.

Thump, thump, thump…

The colossal heart within that massive cavern, as if hearing his voice, began to beat even more violently, pulsing with a deafening rhythm. The sound was like the beating of war drums; even Feng Yuqiu and the others, who possessed fragments of the Heavenly Dao, felt a chill run through their hearts.

It was as if some ancient, malevolent demon from before the dawn of time was about to awaken there.

They all instinctively took several steps back.

Gradually, they saw more and more of that black, viscous liquid seeping out from within the "777" spacetime barriers. It carried a blood-like stench, having likely been stagnant for countless eons.

"This substance..."

Feng Yuqiu’s expression turned pale as he suddenly looked up. Seeing his reaction, the others hurriedly raised their heads as well, gazing toward the distant heights. There, a faint yet colossal figure was coalescing and reappearing. It was so immense that even the universe before their eyes seemed no more than dust and sand in its presence.

And the thick tentacles they saw were merely strands of hair on that colossal figure.

“Senior Brother Li, what on earth is that?”

As the only woman in the group, Luo Wuyan couldn’t help but ask, her voice trembling with fear.

This being hadn’t even fully materialized yet, yet it already exuded such a terrifying sense of oppression. After all, she possessed a fragment of the Heavenly Dao and was confident she could defend herself even against an entity at the End of the Path.

Just what kind of being was this entity about to awaken?

"Before the last Era of Chaos and Heaven and Earth, it was known as Biwei. It once served as the faith and totem of war in the hearts of countless tribes and beings," Li Qingxu explained.

“Biwei?”

They all winced. This was the first time they’d heard that name; at least in this era, none of them had ever heard of such a spirit.

"Senior Brother Li, wasn’t it said that after the last Chaos Heaven and Earth Era ended, everything returned to the Void and began anew, with nearly all sentient beings perishing? How has it survived until now? Just how many eons have passed?"

“It did indeed die, but now it must be temporarily revived.”

“It must fight for us once more.”

Li Qingxu’s gaze fell upon the being named Biwei, a flicker of nostalgia passing through his eyes.

Soon, his gaze swept over the barrier of time and space, passing through one barren universe after another, until it settled upon the Feather-Flower-Tail Temple.

An immense universe lay fully exposed before his eyes, holding no secrets whatsoever.

To a being of his caliber, a Middle Realm where not even beings from the Dao Realm had yet emerged—from past to future—unfolded before him like a series of scroll paintings; no creature’s fate could escape his gaze.

Although the presence of the Alliance Master of the Heaven Slaying Alliance caused some of those around him to be shrouded in mist, rendering them unobservable,

Yet he still possessed the means to clearly perceive the traces of their passage.

Thus, many years ago, he had already left anchors and bait in this universe known as Yuhua.

……

In the Yu Hua Universe, above the ancestral lands of the Tengmu Clan, light surged. Endless green radiance surged like a tidal wave as two aged figures at the pinnacle of the True Immortal Realm simultaneously activated the quasi-Immortal King’s shell left behind by their ancestor, resisting the Bear Enemy Immortal King below who was about to raise his claw to destroy them.

A great battle was inevitable.

The Xiongdi Immortal King showed no mercy, directly slaying one of their elders, which filled all members of the Fujiki clan with seething rage. Normally, they held the Xiongdi Immortal King in the highest regard and would pay tribute to him on schedule, offering all manner of treasures.

Who could have imagined that, in pursuit of bandits, he would come before the mighty Vine Clan to display his might?

"You dare to block my path? You're asking for death."

The Bear Enemy Immortal King’s true form plunged into the depths of the starry river, vast and boundless. He, too, was enraged—he never expected these two old fools of the Tengmu Clan to actually dare block his path, and to have even summoned a Quasi-King’s shell.

In an instant, its aura shifted. A murderous energy surged like a vast ocean; a crack split open between its brows, revealing a vertical eye that blazed with a blood-red light as startling as thunder.

It raised its massive claw and struck toward the two of them. The resulting wave of energy was spine-chilling—enough to crush a True Immortal with ease, its casual strike surging with power like a raging ocean.

Though the Quasi-King’s body erupted with divine might to resist, it was to no avail. The two elders of the Tengmu Clan were sent flying, coughing up blood, their bodies covered in cracks, and their life-flames dimmed.

"Grand Elder..."

"Second Elder."

Witnessing this scene, every member of the Tengmu Clan’s eyes widened in fury, their hatred reaching the heavens. Then, some began to sit cross-legged on the ground of their own accord, chanting incantations as boundless light radiated from their bodies—especially from their third eyes, where a brilliant green glow erupted.

The green light gathered bit by bit, like a surging river and ocean, transforming into a river of life that infused the two elders with new vitality.

At the same time, within the shell of the quasi-King, specks of green light gathered, and the fluctuations of the Shield of Ruin resonated within Xia Su.

This was the Fuji clan’s method of sacrifice—offering up the life force that shared their origin.

"Little Dao."

Xiong Di, the Immortal King, paid it no mind. A divine light suddenly shot forth from the vertical pupil in his brow—the order of Immortal King-level laws, weaving into a sea of divinity that engulfed the two elders of the Fujiki clan.

With a thunderous crash, as if the heavens were splitting and the earth shattering, the two elders coughed up blood once more. They exchanged a glance, then without a moment’s hesitation—just like the clan members below—began chanting incantations. Clusters of green light ignited, completely engulfing the two of them. They offered up their own lives, unhesitatingly channeling their essence into that quasi-King’s shell.

“May the will of the Ancestor awaken and bless our clan.”

The two roared at the very end of their lives, then exploded with a bang, turning into a rain of blood that poured down upon the shell.

They knew that if they did not make a decision today, the Fujiki clan would be completely erased from the Yu Hua Universe and buried forever in the river of time.

"Aaaah..."

"May the Ancestor awaken and protect our clan."

Countless members of the Fujiki clan, their eyes bloodshot, were filled with rage yet powerless. This was a calamity they did not deserve; even now, they still did not know what had brought this disaster upon their clan.

With a thunderous boom.

Perhaps in response to their cries, a sudden humming began to emanate from the once-withered vine, shaking the heavens and earth as a vague figure began to materialize.

In the next instant, an aura that stirred both excitement and bitter grief among the Vinewood clan swept down. The shadowy figure stepped forward—a being shrouded in green light, resembling the very King of Life.

Countless members of the Vine Clan fell to their knees, weeping bitterly as they poured out their sorrows.

After countless years, they had once again laid eyes upon their ancient progenitor—even though it was merely a phantom, a manifestation of the guardian’s resolve to protect his people.

"It is merely the lingering will of a quasi-King. How dare they act so presumptuously."

"Even if a true King were to descend, I would not give him a second thought."

Xiong Di, the Immortal King, knew he could delay no longer. The fluctuations from the battle here would inevitably attract the attention of the other Immortal Kings in the Yuhua Universe. If they arrived, they might uncover the secret of the opportunity hidden within that little thief, and he might not succeed.

BOOM!!!

Its colossal form charged toward the materialized progenitor of the Vine Clan. Immortal King-level fluctuations swept out, shaking the heavens and the earth, engulfing the surrounding star systems. Countless stars and meteorites were instantly shattered.

……

"Someone, go find that little thief who just brought disaster upon our clan."

A voice echoed through the grand palace of the Tengmu Clan. A woman with a green spiral hairpin and clad in a blue gauze skirt spoke coldly, her eyes brimming with hatred.

She was the current Empress of the Fujiki Clan, named Fujin, possessing the cultivation level of a True Immortal.

"Yes, Your Majesty."

The members of the Tengmu Clan within the palace gazed with a mixture of grief, fury, and murderous intent. They felt both anger and helplessness toward the Bear Enemy Immortal King, yet they were also desperate to track down the petty thief who had brought this calamity upon them and eliminate him.

This had nothing to do with them, yet now they faced the catastrophe of their clan’s annihilation.

A buzzing sound!!!

Suddenly, within this magnificent palace, the void twisted and blurred, and immediately afterward, three figures materialized out of thin air.

Empress Fujiyun was taken aback for a moment, then quickly regained her composure. Her expression darkened as she demanded, “Who is Zou Shi Shi?”

“Guards, protect the Empress!”

The remaining guards in the hall rushed forward.

“Don’t misunderstand—we mean no harm.”

Mu Yan spoke with a radiant smile, her flawless face radiating a warmth as soothing as a spring breeze.

Mo Tong’s face remained cold; he did not speak, but merely released a faint trace of his Dao Realm aura. This caused time and space within the hall to freeze, halting everyone’s senses and thoughts, leaving them unable to move.

Only Empress Tengyun, standing in the center, remained able to move. Her expression shifted repeatedly; as the empress of a clan, she was no fool. She immediately realized that the cultivation levels of the three before her had reached a realm she could never have imagined.

"Greetings, esteemed elders."

She hurriedly bowed. “I wonder how my Tengmu clan has offended the three of you?”

“No, no, no—we’ve already said we mean no harm. You haven’t offended us at all.” Mu Yan waved her hand dismissively.

Mo Tong’s Billion Souls Banner could sense the location of the fragments of the Heavenly Dao, but it couldn’t pinpoint a specific area. Therefore, if they wanted to find the exact location of the fragments, they would have to investigate more thoroughly. Mu Yan didn’t seem to be the kind of person who enjoyed killing like Mo Tong, so she decided to ask politely first—to be polite before resorting to force.

If all else failed, they could always resort to soul-searching.

The battle raging outside between the Immortal King and the Near-King couldn’t possibly be without cause; it was highly likely a struggle over the fragments of the Heavenly Dao.

"What brings you here, honored elders?"

Empress Teng Yun asked cautiously.

Compared to the Immortal Kings battling outside with earth-shaking ferocity, these three before her—whose cultivation levels she could not perceive, making them seem as ordinary as mortals—instilled far greater fear in her. She feared that a single thought from any one of them could annihilate the entire Tengmu Clan.

"Let me ask you this: has your clan produced any particularly exceptional or powerful members recently?" Mu Yan asked with a smile.

Upon hearing this, Mo Tong couldn’t help but shoot her a speechless glance.

Wasn’t this essentially asking them if they’d seen the fragments of the Heavenly Dao?

Empress Tengyun shook her head hurriedly. She had actually been in seclusion the entire time; had she not been caught in the crossfire today, she wouldn’t have even woken up.

"I told you asking like this wouldn’t get us anywhere—it’s just a waste of time."

“Then you ask.”

“Why go to all that trouble?”

Mo Tong’s gaze swept over the palace halls and towers; nothing that occurred within the entire territory of the Tengmu Clan could escape her perception.

“This clan has merely suffered an undeserved calamity. As for the whereabouts of the true Fragment of the Heavenly Dao, only that fellow would know.”

As the words fell, Empress Tengyun didn’t even have time to get a clear look before the woman in the black dress—as cold and impassive as an ice sculpture—vanished from where she stood.

The entire sky suddenly fell silent. Xiongdi, the Immortal King, who had just reached out to seize the shell of the Tengmu Clan’s quasi-King, felt an overwhelming sense of dread descend upon him. His massive body could no longer move an inch. His blood-red eyes shifted, landing on the woman before him—one who appeared as insignificant as a speck of dust.

The disparity in their stature was negligible—there was simply no comparison—yet, under the gaze of countless members of the Vinewood Clan, the Immortal King Xiongdi began to tremble, as if shaken by a sieve.

The spectral form of the Tengmu Clan’s ancestor, which still retained some vestiges of sentience, stood frozen in place as well. As a quasi-Immortal King, it was already considered one of the most powerful beings in this Yu Hua Universe.

Yet in the presence of this diminutive woman in black, they felt as though they were mere ants and specks of dust.

"E... Elder..."

Xiong Di Immortal King spoke. His colossal form, vast enough to obscure the sky, began to shrink, transforming into the size of an ordinary black bear. He wore a robe that looked somewhat tattered, and the wings on his back were folded away, his demeanor utterly respectful.

"I ask, you answer."

Mo Tong said coldly.

"Yes, yes, yes."

Xiongdi Immortal King nodded frantically, his heart trembling with fear. How could such a terrifying being suddenly appear in the Feathered Universe? This was undoubtedly an existence capable of traversing the Vast Expanse, far surpassing even an Immortal Emperor—likely the legendary Dao Realm…

“You’re searching for fragments of the Heavenly Dao, aren’t you?”

Mo Tong couldn’t be bothered with small talk and cut straight to the chase.

Xiong Di, the Immortal King, trembled. He had never even heard of any "Fragments of the Heavenly Dao" and had no idea what they were. Steeling himself, he replied, "I have never seen the Fragments of the Heavenly Dao you speak of, Senior, but I am indeed pursuing a petty thief. He likely possesses the Fragments of the Heavenly Dao you mentioned."

In the presence of such a being, it dared not lie.

"Just as I thought."

Mo Tong nodded.

The Bear Enemy Immortal King hurriedly explained, “Senior, that youngster is currently hiding within the territory of the Vine Clan ahead. It might be difficult for you to find him. He’s used some method to conceal his aura, and I’ve lost his trail.”

“Moreover, that youngster’s physical body is incredibly resilient and indestructible. Even if I were to shatter him, he could heal rapidly—he’s nearly impossible to kill.”

Upon hearing this, Mo Tong realized that the Heavenly Dao Fragment she was seeking had already been obtained by someone else.

Moreover, that indestructible, resilient physique was likely a trait bestowed upon him by that fragment of the Heavenly Dao.

She paid it no mind. With a wave of her hand, the river of time surged forth. Before the eyes of all the members of the Fujiki clan, the shattered sky unfurled like a scroll, its scenes flowing backward ceaselessly.Everything was being reshaped; all the events that had transpired were unfolding anew—including the battle between Bear Enemy Immortal King and the Quasi-King of the Tengmu Clan, followed by the scenes of the two elders sacrificing themselves—until the sequence finally froze just before Bear Enemy Immortal King descended upon the Tenghe Clan.

In the distant starry expanse, a young man, blood spurting from his body and with a hole pierced through his chest, was fleeing for his life. Ignoring the shouts of the Third Elder of the Tengmu Clan, he charged straight toward the Life Star below.

"It’s him."

The moment they recognized the young man’s face, every member of the Fujiki Clan was filled with rage; their eyes seemed to blaze with fury, desperate to track him down.

Deep within the vast, boundless emerald forest, Su Beichen—concealing his aura and disguised—was also closely watching the scene unfolding in the sky. When he realized he had been identified, his heart skipped a beat.

"However, even if this woman is enigmatic and her cultivation level unknown, she certainly won’t be able to detect me."

Su Beichen comforted himself with this thought, holding his breath and focusing his mind to mask all traces of his presence.

Suddenly, a chill ran through his body. He sensed that from high above the starry sky, a pair of eyes as cold and distant as the stars had suddenly swept toward his location.
